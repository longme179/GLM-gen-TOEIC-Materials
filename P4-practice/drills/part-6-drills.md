# Part 6 Drills — Text Completion
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 4 texts, 16 blanks

*VI: File luyện tập Part 6 — mỗi đoạn văn (thư nội bộ, biên bản, bài báo, thư tín chính thức) có bốn chỗ trống; chọn phương án đúng nhất trong (A)–(D). Chỗ trống có thể kiểm tra ngữ pháp, từ vựng hoặc tính liên kết giữa các câu với nhau. Sau mỗi câu hỏi đều có đáp án, giải thích tiếng Anh, phần diễn giải tiếng Việt và phân tích bẫy.*

## How to use this file

1. **Read each text once WITHOUT the options first.** Cover the questions, read the passage from top to bottom, and write a one-line purpose statement — for example, "announces a cafeteria closure" or "requests a free replacement of damaged goods." Part 6 rewards knowing what a text is for before you fill in anything.
2. **Then work the blanks in order.** For each blank, reread the entire sentence that contains it before you choose. Grammar and word-form blanks can usually be solved inside that one sentence.
3. **Cohesion blanks need the surrounding sentences.** Connectors, pronoun references, and the one full-sentence blank in Set C cannot be solved from the blank's own sentence — always read the sentence before and the sentence after before committing.
4. **Numbering note.** In Set A and Set B the blank numbering restarts at (1); in Set C it continues across the two texts, so Text 4 uses blanks (5)–(8) with questions Q5–Q8. On Day 23 these 16 blanks follow the 30 items of `part-5-drills.md` in one ~22-minute circuit — budget about 45 seconds per blank and 2.5–3 minutes per text.

## Part 6 — Text Completion Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2) — 1 email, 4 blanks (Q1–Q4)

*Focus: grammar ×2 (Q1–Q2), word form ×1 (Q3), connector ×1 (Q4). Blanks __(1)__–__(4)__, questions Q1–Q4.*

### Text 1 — Email: internal announcement (Questions 1–4)

> **To:** All employees, Riverside Office
> **From:** Karen Doyle, Facilities Coordinator
> **Date:** March 4
> **Subject:** Temporary Closure of the Staff Cafeteria
>
> Dear colleagues,
>
> The staff cafeteria on the second floor __(1)__ closed for renovations from Monday, March 10, to Friday, March 21. During this period, the vending machines in the lobby __(2)__ available around the clock, and food trucks will be parked in the east lot from 11:30 A.M. to 2:00 P.M. The same menu will be offered in the trucks at a __(3)__ price, so meals should remain affordable. __(4)__ the renovation causes some short-term inconvenience, the new kitchen will allow us to serve fresher and more varied meals when it reopens. Thank you for your patience.
>
> Karen Doyle

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
For blank (1), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) are
(B) were
(C) will be
(D) have been

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The closure runs "from Monday, March 10," a period still in the future at the time of writing, so the cafeteria "will be closed." / *VI: Việc đóng cửa bắt đầu từ ngày 10/3 — khoảng thời gian vẫn ở tương lai lúc viết thư — nên dùng "will be closed" (sẽ bị đóng để sửa chữa).*
- **Traps:** (A) present and (B) past ignore the future dates; (D) present perfect cannot combine with a period that has not started yet.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
For blank (2), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) is
(B) are
(C) was
(D) has been

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The subject is "the vending machines," plural, and the sentence states the arrangement that applies during the closure period. / *VI: Chủ ngữ là "the vending machines" (số nhiều); câu nêu sự sắp xếp áp dụng trong thời gian đóng cửa → dùng "are".*
- **Traps:** (A) and (D) are singular verbs attracted by the nearest noun "the lobby"; (C) is past tense, but the arrangement is current.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
For blank (3), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) reduce
(B) reduces
(C) reducing
(D) reduced

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The blank sits between "a" and the noun "price," so an adjective is needed: "a reduced price" means a price that has been lowered. / *VI: Chỗ trống nằm giữa "a" và danh từ "price" nên cần tính từ: "a reduced price" nghĩa là mức giá đã được giảm.*
- **Traps:** (A) is a base verb; (B) is a third-person verb; (C) is an -ing form that cannot fill this slot.

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
For blank (4), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) Although
(B) Because of
(C) Therefore
(D) In addition

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The sentence concedes a drawback (short-term inconvenience) and then states the benefit (a better kitchen); "Although" introduces a full clause and expresses exactly this concession. / *VI: Câu thừa nhận một bất tiện nhỏ rồi nêu lợi ích (bếp mới tốt hơn); "Although" + mệnh đề diễn tả đúng sự nhượng bộ này.*
- **Traps:** (B) "Because of" must be followed by a noun phrase, but here a full clause follows; (C) and (D) are transition adverbs, and neither matches the concede-then-praise logic.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3) — 1 memo, 4 blanks (Q1–Q4)

*Focus: modal passive (Q1), relative word (Q2), pronoun reference (Q3), verb collocation (Q4). Numbering restarts at blank (1).*

### Text 2 — Memo: new warehouse procedures (Questions 1–4)

> **MEMORANDUM**
> **To:** All Warehouse Staff
> **From:** Miguel Santos, Operations Supervisor
> **Date:** April 14
> **Subject:** New Barcode Tracking System
>
> To reduce shipping errors, the warehouse will introduce a barcode-based tracking system on May 1. Under the new procedures, every package __(1)__ with a handheld scanner before it leaves the loading dock. Training sessions will be held in the break room every morning next week, and each one lasts about 40 minutes. Staff may attend __(2)__ session best fits their schedule. Afterward, employees should present their training card and scanner badge to the front office; the supervisor will inspect __(3)__ before granting scanner access. Because the system records every scan automatically, the weekly inventory report __(4)__ far less time to prepare than before.

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
For blank (1), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) must be scanned
(B) must scan
(C) must have scanned
(D) must have been scanned

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The package receives the scanning, so the modal passive "must + be + past participle" is required. / *VI: Bưu kiện chịu hành động được quét, nên cần dạng bị động với động từ khuyết thiếu: "must be scanned".*
- **Traps:** (B) is active, as if the package did the scanning itself; (C) and (D) are perfect forms that clash with "before it leaves," which refers to the present routine.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
For blank (2), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) whenever
(B) wherever
(C) whatever
(D) whichever

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Whichever" means "any one from the known set" — the training sessions listed in the previous sentence — and it is followed directly by the noun "session." / *VI: "Whichever" nghĩa là bất kỳ buổi nào trong số các buổi đã nêu ở câu trước, và từ này đi kèm ngay với danh từ "session".*
- **Traps:** (A) refers to time and (B) to place, so neither can modify "session"; (C) "whatever" is for unspecified things, not a choice from a defined set of sessions.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
For blank (3), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) it
(B) them
(C) this
(D) that

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The pronoun points back to two items — "their training card and scanner badge" — so the plural object pronoun "them" is required. / *VI: Đại từ quay về hai sự vật đã nêu trước đó ("thẻ tập huấn" và "thẻ quét") nên phải dùng đại từ tân ngữ số nhiều "them".*
- **Traps:** (A) "it" would cover only one of the two items; (C) and (D) are demonstratives that would need to repeat a noun (for example, "these documents").

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
For blank (4), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) spends
(B) pays
(C) takes
(D) lasts

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** A task or document "takes time" to prepare; "take" is the verb that pairs a duration with an activity. / *VI: Một công việc hoặc một văn bản "takes time" (tốn thời gian); "take" là động từ kết hợp thời lượng với một hoạt động.*
- **Traps:** (A) "spends" needs a person as its subject — only people spend time; (B) "pays" concerns money; (D) "lasts" describes duration but does not fit the "___ time to prepare" pattern.

### Text 3 — Article excerpt: trade magazine (Questions 1–4)

> **Excerpt from "The Rise of the Micro-Warehouse," *Logistics Review* (May edition)**
>
> Five years ago, national retailers stored almost all of their goods in a handful of giant distribution centers. Soaring fuel costs and shoppers' rising expectations of next-day delivery, however, __(1)__ many of these companies to redesign their supply networks. Dozens of firms have now opened small "micro-warehouses" in suburban areas, __(2)__ online orders can be picked and packed within hours. __(3)__ The change is not inexpensive, though: leasing nearby space and hiring local staff require considerable investment, and companies that __(4)__ such costs can quickly erode their profits.

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (1), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) have led
(B) has led
(C) leads
(D) is leading

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The subject is the compound "Soaring fuel costs and shoppers' rising expectations," which is plural, and the causes still shape the present, so the present perfect "have led" fits. / *VI: Chủ ngữ là cụm ghép "Soaring fuel costs and shoppers' rising expectations" (số nhiều), và các nguyên nhân này vẫn còn tác động đến hiện tại → hiện tại hoàn thành "have led".*
- **Traps:** (B) and (D) are singular, attracted by the nearest noun "delivery"; (C) present simple misses the connection between the past five years and the current situation.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (2), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) which
(B) that
(C) where
(D) when

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The clause that follows already has its own subject and verb ("orders can be picked"); what the sentence needs is the place link back to the warehouses, and "where" (= in which) provides it. / *VI: Mệnh đề theo sau đã có đủ chủ ngữ và động từ ("orders can be picked"); câu cần liên kết về nơi chốn với "micro-warehouses", và "where" (= nơi mà) đảm nhận vai trò đó.*
- **Traps:** (A) and (B) would have to play a grammatical role inside the clause, which is already complete; (D) refers to time.

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (3), choose the sentence that best fits the paragraph.
(A) Nevertheless, fuel prices are expected to fall again next year.
(B) As a result, many city customers now receive online orders within a single day.
(C) The company also plans to close its oldest suburban store next spring.
(D) Warehouse staff, however, must complete the same safety training as before.

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The previous sentence explains that micro-warehouses let orders be picked and packed within hours; the sentence that fits draws the natural consequence for customers. / *VI: Câu trước giải thích rằng micro-warehouse cho phép chọn và đóng gói đơn hàng trong vòng vài giờ; câu phù hợp nhất nêu kết quả tự nhiên dành cho khách hàng: nhiều khách ở thành phố nhận được hàng trong vòng một ngày.*
- **Traps:** (A) jumps to fuel-price predictions that nobody raised; (C) speaks of one "company," but the article discusses many firms — and "suburban store" shifts the topic; (D) drifts to staff training, which the paragraph never mentions.

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (4), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) subsidize
(B) itemize
(C) reimburse
(D) overlook

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The sentence warns about eroding profits: the companies in danger are those that pay no attention to the costs, and "overlook" means fail to notice. / *VI: Câu mang sắc thái cảnh báo về việc lợi nhuận bị bào mòn: những công ty nguy hiểm là những công ty không chú ý đến các khoản chi phí — "overlook" nghĩa là bỏ qua, không để ý.*
- **Traps:** (A) "subsidize" means pay part of a cost, which would protect rather than erode profits; (B) "itemize" means list items one by one; (C) "reimburse" means pay someone back.

### Text 4 — Formal letter: request for replacement (Questions 5–8)

> **Lanier & Rowe, Inc.**
> 44 Harborview Road, Portland, Oregon 97204
> June 20
>
> Mr. Daniel Okafor
> Customer Relations Manager
> Vantage Office Supplies
> 1200 Fell Street, Seattle, Washington 98122
>
> Dear Mr. Okafor,
>
> I am writing about order #55821, which arrived on June 17. Upon inspection, we found that 14 of the 60 printer cartridges were leaking. We must therefore insist that the damaged cartridges __(5)__ replaced at no charge. We would also like to suggest a change to your packing process. __(6)__, future shipments could include absorbent padding around each cartridge. Our office manager, Ms. Reyes, has photographed the damaged items and __(7)__ an inspection report for your records. We value our five-year partnership with Vantage and trust that you will resolve this matter quickly. __(8)__ the replacements arrive by June 27, we will have to order cartridges from another supplier.
>
> Sincerely,
> Elaine Marsh
> Procurement Manager

#### Q5. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (5), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) are
(B) were
(C) be
(D) will be

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Insist that" requires the subjunctive; because the cartridges receive the action, the passive subjunctive "be replaced" is needed. / *VI: "Insist that" đòi hỏi giả định cách; hộp mực chịu hành động được thay thế nên dùng bị động giả định cách "be replaced".*
- **Traps:** (A), (B), and (D) are ordinary indicative tenses, which formal usage rejects after "insist that" in its demanding sense.

#### Q6. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (6), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) Nevertheless
(B) For instance
(C) Otherwise
(D) Meanwhile

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The previous sentence announces "a change to your packing process," and this sentence gives one concrete example of that change — padding around each cartridge. / *VI: Câu trước nêu "một thay đổi trong quy trình đóng gói của quý công ty"; câu này đưa ra một ví dụ cụ thể của thay đổi đó — đệm lót quanh mỗi hộp mực → dùng "For instance".*
- **Traps:** (A) signals contrast; (C) signals a negative consequence; (D) marks simultaneous time — none of them introduces an example.

#### Q7. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (7), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) attach
(B) attaching
(C) attachment
(D) attached

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The sentence continues the present perfect "has photographed ... and ...", so the second verb must also be a past participle: "attached." / *VI: Câu tiếp nối hiện tại hoàn thành "has photographed ... and ...", nên động từ thứ hai cũng phải là quá khứ phân từ: "attached".*
- **Traps:** (A) is the base form; (B) is a gerund; (C) is a noun — a classic word-form triple of wrong options.

#### Q8. (Bands: B4 B5)
For blank (8), choose the word or phrase that best completes the sentence.
(A) Unless
(B) Provided that
(C) Even though
(D) While

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The deadline logic is "if the cartridges do NOT arrive by June 27, we will buy elsewhere," and that negative condition is exactly what "Unless" expresses. / *VI: Logic của hạn chót là "nếu hộp mực KHÔNG đến trước ngày 27/6 thì chúng tôi sẽ phải mua của hãng khác" — điều kiện phủ định này chính là "Unless" (trừ khi).*
- **Traps:** (B) "Provided that" sets a positive condition (if they DO arrive) and reverses the logic; (C) and (D) express contrast and time, which break the sentence's warning.
