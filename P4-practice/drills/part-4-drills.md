# Part 4 Drills — Talks
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 5 talks, 15 questions

*VI: Phần 4 gồm các bài nói của một người duy nhất — thông báo, tin nhắn thoại, bài họp ngắn — mỗi bài kèm ba câu hỏi. Như ở Part 3, bạn tự học nên không có băng nghe: hãy nhờ một người đọc kịch bản, hoặc dán kịch bản vào ứng dụng đọc văn bản thành giọng nói (TTS) để phát bài nói. Trong mỗi khối dưới đây, kịch bản nằm ngay trên các câu hỏi — đừng liếc nhìn kịch bản trước khi trả lời xong cả ba câu.*

## How to use this file

- **Pick your set by band.** B1 → Set A only. B2 → Set A, then add Set B from Week 2. B3 → Sets B and C. B4/B5 → Sets B and C with timing: answer all three questions within the 10-second pause.
- **Always pre-read the three questions before the talk is read, and never read the script yourself before answering.** Uncover the script only afterwards, for review — hearing it first is the whole exercise.
- **Take notes while you listen.** Use the same four-line template for every talk:
  - **Who** — who is speaking: a manager, a store clerk, an announcer?
  - **What changes** — the times, dates, rules, or plans that differ from the usual.
  - **Numbers** — prices, quantities, percentages, deadlines.
  - **Required action** — what listeners must do: bring something, press a number, confirm by a date.
- **For the reader or TTS app:** one voice, a natural pace, and keep the pause marked at the end of each talk.

*VI: Cách dùng: chọn tập theo trình độ như tập Part 3 (B1 → Set A; B2 → Set A + B từ tuần thứ hai; B3 → Set B + C; B4/B5 → Set B + C kèm bấm giờ). Luôn đọc trước ba câu hỏi và không tự nhìn kịch bản trước khi trả lời xong. Trong khi nghe, ghi chú theo mẫu bốn dòng: Ai — Điều gì thay đổi — Con số — Việc cần làm.*

*VI: Dành cho người đọc kịch bản hoặc ứng dụng TTS: chỉ dùng một giọng đọc duy nhất, giữ nhịp đọc tự nhiên, và giữ nguyên khoảng tạm dừng được đánh dấu ở cuối mỗi bài nói.*

## Part 4 — Talk Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2)

*Two talks — Questions 1–3 and 4–6: a facility announcement and a store's recorded message.*

### Talk 1 (Questions 1–3)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> Good evening, members. This is a short announcement from the front desk of the Riverside Fitness Center. On Saturday, the main parking area will be closed from six in the morning until two in the afternoon while it is resurfaced. During the work, members are welcome to use the smaller lot on the north side of the building, a five-minute walk from the main entrance. The front desk will remain open as usual, and every morning class will go ahead as scheduled. If you are coming to a class before two o'clock, please allow a few extra minutes to park and walk over. Thank you for your patience.
> [PAUSE 10 s]

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
What is the main purpose of the announcement?
(A) To advertise new classes
(B) To explain a temporary change in parking
(C) To apologize for cancelled classes
(D) To introduce a new walking path

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The announcement's focus is that the main lot is closed "from six in the morning until two in the afternoon" for resurfacing and that members should use the north-side lot in the meantime — a temporary parking change. / *VI: Trọng tâm của thông báo là bãi đỗ chính bị đóng "từ sáu giờ sáng đến hai giờ chiều" để trải lại mặt đường và hội viên được hướng dẫn dùng bãi phía bắc trong thời gian đó — một thay đổi tạm thời về chỗ đỗ xe.*
- **Traps:** (A) The classes go ahead as scheduled — nothing new is advertised; (C) nothing is cancelled; (D) the "walk" is only the distance from the north lot, not a path.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
While the resurfacing work is going on, where should members park?
(A) In the main lot, from two o'clock
(B) In front of the main entrance
(C) On the street beside the building
(D) In a lot that requires a short walk

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** During the work members are directed to "the smaller lot on the north side of the building, a five-minute walk from the main entrance" — a lot that requires a short walk. / *VI: Trong lúc thi công, hội viên được hướng dẫn dùng "bãi đỗ nhỏ hơn ở phía bắc tòa nhà, cách lối vào chính năm phút đi bộ" — tức là bãi đỗ phải đi bộ một đoạn ngắn mới tới.*
- **Traps:** (A) The main lot is closed during the work; it only reopens in the afternoon; (B) no parking is offered at the entrance; (C) no street parking is mentioned.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
What does the speaker say about morning classes?
(A) They will run as planned.
(B) They are cancelled until two o'clock.
(C) They will move to the north lot.
(D) They will start earlier than usual.

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The speaker states that "every morning class will go ahead as scheduled," which means the classes run as planned. / *VI: Người nói khẳng định "mọi lớp học buổi sáng vẫn diễn ra theo đúng lịch", nghĩa là các lớp vẫn chạy đúng kế hoạch.*
- **Traps:** (B) Only the parking area is affected; (C) the north lot is for cars, not classes; (D) listeners are asked to arrive earlier, not the classes.

### Talk 2 (Questions 4–6)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> Thank you for calling Greenleaf Garden Center. Our store is open Monday through Saturday from eight in the morning until six in the evening, and we are closed on Sundays. This week only, we are offering free delivery on any order over fifty dollars within the city — just mention the code "spring" when you check out. If you know the extension of the department you need, you may dial it at any time. For seasonal plants, press one; for tools and equipment, press two. For anything else, including questions about a delivery, please stay on the line and a colleague will be with you shortly.
> [PAUSE 10 s]

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
What is offered this week only?
(A) Later opening hours on Sundays
(B) A discount on tools and equipment
(C) Free delivery above a certain amount
(D) An extension for seasonal plants

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The message says "This week only, we are offering free delivery on any order over fifty dollars within the city" — free delivery once an order passes a set amount. / *VI: Bản tin nói "chỉ trong tuần này, chúng tôi miễn phí giao hàng cho mọi đơn hàng trên năm mươi đô trong nội thành" — miễn phí giao hàng khi đơn vượt mức quy định.*
- **Traps:** (A) The store is closed on Sundays; (B) no discount is mentioned; (D) "extension" here means a phone extension, not extra time.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
What should callers with a question about a delivery do?
(A) Press one
(B) Remain on the call
(C) Dial the department directly
(D) Visit the store on Monday

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** Callers with "anything else, including questions about a delivery" are told to "stay on the line and a colleague will be with you shortly," so they must remain on the call. / *VI: Người gọi có "vấn đề khác, kể cả câu hỏi về giao hàng" được yêu cầu "giữ máy, nhân viên sẽ sớm bắt máy" — nghĩa là phải giữ máy chờ.*
- **Traps:** (A) Pressing one is for seasonal plants; (C) direct dialing is for callers who already know their department's extension; (D) nobody is told to come in person.

#### Q6. (Bands: B1 B2)
On which day is the store closed?
(A) On Mondays
(B) On Saturdays
(C) On the day the offer ends
(D) On Sundays

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The recording gives the hours as "open Monday through Saturday" and "closed on Sundays," so Sunday is the day the store is closed. / *VI: Bản ghi âm nêu rõ cửa hàng "mở từ thứ Hai đến thứ Bảy" và "đóng cửa vào chủ nhật", nên chủ nhật là ngày cửa hàng nghỉ.*
- **Traps:** (A) and (B) are full opening days; (C) the offer lasts "this week only," but no closing day is attached to it.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3)

*Two talks — Questions 1–3 and 4–6; question numbering restarts from the previous set: a voicemail that asks for something, and a team briefing full of numbers.*

### Talk 1 (Questions 1–3)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> Hi, Sandra, it's Marco from the third floor. I'm calling about the budget draft you sent around yesterday. Overall it looks solid, but before it goes to the directors I need you to double-check two things. First, the travel figures for the second quarter look about ten percent higher than what we agreed in March — could you confirm them with finance? Second, the appendix still shows last year's supplier list, so please swap in the updated one. If you can get the revised file to me by Thursday at noon, I'll still have time to review it before Friday's meeting. Call me if anything is unclear. Thanks.
> [PAUSE 10 s]

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
Why is the caller leaving the message?
(A) To request corrections to a document
(B) To confirm a meeting on Friday
(C) To complain about travel costs
(D) To introduce a new supplier

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** Marco says the budget draft "looks solid, but before it goes to the directors I need you to double-check two things," so the message is a request for corrections to the document. / *VI: Marco nói bản dự thảo ngân sách "khá ổn, nhưng trước khi gửi cho các giám đốc, tôi cần cô kiểm tra lại hai điểm" — nên tin nhắn này nhằm yêu cầu sửa lại tài liệu.*
- **Traps:** (B) Friday's meeting is the deadline context, not the reason for calling; (C) he questions figures, he does not complain; (D) the supplier list is being updated, not replaced by a new vendor.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
What does the caller say about the travel figures?
(A) They were approved in March.
(B) They are missing from the draft.
(C) They appear larger than expected.
(D) They were calculated by finance.

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The caller points out that the second-quarter travel figures "look about ten percent higher than what we agreed in March," meaning they appear larger than expected. / *VI: Người gọi chỉ ra rằng số công tác phí quý hai "có vẻ cao hơn khoảng mười phần trăm so với mức chúng ta đã thống nhất hồi tháng Ba" — nghĩa là chúng trông lớn hơn dự kiến.*
- **Traps:** (A) March is when the numbers were agreed, not approved now; (B) the figures are present but possibly wrong; (D) finance is asked to confirm them, not said to have produced them.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
By when does the caller want the revised file?
(A) By Friday morning
(B) By the end of March
(C) By ten o'clock
(D) By midday on Thursday

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** He asks her "to get the revised file to me by Thursday at noon" so he still has time to review it before Friday's meeting — the deadline is Thursday midday. / *VI: Anh nhờ cô "gửi bản sửa lại cho tôi trước trưa thứ Năm" để anh còn kịp xem trước cuộc họp thứ Sáu — hạn chót là giữa trưa thứ Năm.*
- **Traps:** (A) Friday is when the meeting takes place; (B) March refers to the earlier agreement; (C) "ten" belongs to the ten percent, not to a time.

### Talk 2 (Questions 4–6)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> Good morning. Before we open the doors, a few numbers from yesterday, and two changes for today. We shipped four hundred and twenty packages — thirty more than our target — well done. However, eleven items came back with address errors — higher than I want to see. Because of the holiday sale, we are expecting around five hundred orders today, so two extra people will join the packing line from noon. One more thing: the courier pickup has moved forward from five o'clock to three thirty, so anything for today's truck must be labeled by three. Save your questions for the morning break — let's get started.
> [PAUSE 10 s]

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
How many packages did the team ship yesterday?
(A) 390
(B) 420
(C) 500
(D) 11

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The speaker reports "We shipped four hundred and twenty packages — thirty more than our target," so the number shipped yesterday is 420. / *VI: Người nói báo cáo "chúng ta đã gửi bốn trăm hai mươi kiện — nhiều hơn ba mươi kiện so với chỉ tiêu", nên số kiện gửi đi hôm qua là 420.*
- **Traps:** (A) Three hundred ninety is the implied target — four twenty minus thirty; (C) five hundred is today's expected order count; (D) eleven is the number of returned items.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
What does the speaker say about the courier?
(A) It will arrive earlier than usual.
(B) It has been cancelled for today.
(C) It will come after the morning break.
(D) It carries only labeled items.

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The speaker says the courier pickup "has moved forward from five o'clock to three thirty," so the courier will arrive earlier than its usual time. / *VI: Người nói cho biết xe lấy hàng "được dời sớm từ năm giờ xuống ba giờ ba mươi" — bên chuyển phát sẽ đến sớm hơn giờ thường lệ.*
- **Traps:** (B) The pickup still happens, only earlier; (C) the morning break is for questions; (D) labeling by three is the team's duty, not the truck's.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
Why will additional staff join at noon?
(A) To replace absent workers
(B) To sort out address errors
(C) To handle a heavier workload
(D) To meet the earlier pickup time

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** Because the holiday sale means "around five hundred orders today," "two extra people will join the packing line from noon" — extra staff for a heavier workload. / *VI: Vì đợt giảm giá dịp lễ đồng nghĩa với "khoảng năm trăm đơn hàng hôm nay", nên "hai nhân viên thêm sẽ vào dây đóng gói từ trưa" — thêm người để xử lý khối lượng công việc lớn hơn.*
- **Traps:** (A) Nobody is reported absent; (B) address errors are yesterday's regret, not today's staffing reason; (D) noon was chosen for the order volume, not for the three-thirty pickup.

### Set C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5)

*One talk — Questions 1–3: a policy-change announcement with one exception rule.*

### Talk 1 (Questions 1–3)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> Good afternoon, everyone. The revised expense policy takes effect on the first of next month. From that date, receipts for any meal over twenty dollars must list the names of everyone present, and every claim will need a manager's approval before submission rather than afterward. There is one exception: staff traveling with clients for more than three days may hand in a single summary sheet at the end of the trip instead of daily receipts. Note that this exception does not cover internal travel — it applies only to client-facing trips. If you are unsure, contact the finance office before you file, not after.
> [PAUSE 10 s]

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
What is the main purpose of the talk?
(A) To request approval for a client trip
(B) To introduce the finance office staff
(C) To report on last month's spending
(D) To outline updated procedures for claims

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The talk announces that "the revised expense policy takes effect on the first of next month" and then details the new rules for receipts and approvals, so its purpose is to outline updated claim procedures. / *VI: Bài nói thông báo "chính sách chi tiêu sửa đổi có hiệu lực từ mùng một tháng sau" rồi trình bày chi tiết các quy định mới về hóa đơn và phê duyệt — mục đích là giới thiệu quy trình yêu cầu chi đã được cập nhật.*
- **Traps:** (A) The approval discussed is for claims, not for a trip; (B) the finance office is only a contact point; (C) no spending figures are reported.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
Under the new policy, what must employees obtain before submitting a claim?
(A) A summary sheet from finance
(B) Authorization from a supervisor
(C) The names of all travelers
(D) Receipts for meals under twenty dollars

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** Under the new policy "every claim will need a manager's approval before submission rather than afterward," which is authorization from a supervisor. / *VI: Theo chính sách mới, "mọi khoản chi đều cần được quản lý phê duyệt trước khi nộp chứ không phải sau đó" — tức là phải có sự chấp thuận của cấp trên trước.*
- **Traps:** (A) A summary sheet belongs to the exception, not to everyone; (C) names on receipts are required for meals over twenty dollars; (D) the rule concerns meals over twenty dollars.

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)
What exception to the policy is described?
(A) Internal travel may skip receipts.
(B) Managers may approve claims afterward.
(C) Long client trips can use one combined form.
(D) Meals under twenty dollars need no names.

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The one exception lets staff "traveling with clients for more than three days" hand in "a single summary sheet at the end of the trip instead of daily receipts" — one combined form for long client trips. / *VI: Trường hợp ngoại lệ duy nhất cho phép nhân viên "đi công tác với khách hàng hơn ba ngày" nộp "một bản tổng hợp duy nhất vào cuối chuyến đi thay vì hóa đơn từng ngày" — một biểu mẫu gộp chung cho các chuyến đi kéo dài cùng khách hàng.*
- **Traps:** (A) The exception expressly does not cover internal travel; (B) approval moves to before submission, not after; (D) the rule concerns meals over twenty dollars — and it is about names, not exemption.
