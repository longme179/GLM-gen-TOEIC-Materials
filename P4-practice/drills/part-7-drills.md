# Part 7 Drills — Reading Comprehension
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 24 questions

Part 7 is where most of the Reading section's points live: e-mails, letters, notices, advertisements, schedules, forms, and short articles, followed by questions on details, purpose, audience, vocabulary, inference, and — on linked double passages — information that only appears when you put two texts together. This file gives that practice in three banded sets: Set A (Foundation) for bands B1–B2, Set B (Core) for B3, and Set C (Challenge) for B4–B5. All three double-passage sets carry cross-reference questions that need both texts.

*VI: Part 7 là nơi có phần lớn điểm của phần Reading: e-mail, thư từ, thông báo, quảng cáo, lịch trình, biểu mẫu và bài báo ngắn, kèm các câu hỏi về chi tiết, mục đích, đối tượng đọc, từ vựng, suy luận, và — ở các bộ đoạn văn đôi có liên kết — những thông tin chỉ hiện ra khi ghép hai văn bản lại với nhau. File này luyện đúng những dạng đó trong ba bộ theo băng: Set A (nền tảng) cho băng B1–B2, Set B (lõi) cho B3, Set C (thử thách) cho B4–B5. Cả ba bộ đoạn đôi đều có câu hỏi đối chiếu bắt buộc dùng cả hai văn bản.*

## How to use this file

Work set by set, by band: B1–B2 learners start with Set A, B3 with Set B, and B4–B5 with Set C — and use the set one band above yours as a stretch goal. On every question, run the **3-step scanning loop: question stem → type tag → anchor scan → decide.**

*VI: Làm theo từng bộ, theo băng điểm: người băng B1–B2 bắt đầu với Set A, băng B3 với Set B, băng B4–B5 với Set C — và dùng bộ cao hơn một băng làm mục tiêu vươn lên. Ở mỗi câu hỏi, hãy chạy vòng lặp quét 3 bước: thân câu hỏi → gắn nhãn dạng → quét mỏ neo → quyết định.*

1. **Question stem.** Read the stem only, with the options covered — the options are bait, not information.
2. **Type tag.** Name the type — detail, purpose, audience, vocabulary, inference, task/next action, cross-reference — because the type tells you where to look: purpose lives in subject lines and first/last lines, details live at anchors, vocabulary lives in one sentence plus its neighbors.
3. **Anchor scan, then decide.** Scan for the stem's anchor — a name, a number, a day, a capitalized word — read the one or two sentences around it, and decide. No full-passage rereading.

*VI: (1) Thân câu hỏi: chỉ đọc thân đề, che các phương án — phương án là mồi câu, không phải thông tin. (2) Gắn nhãn dạng: chi tiết, mục đích, đối tượng, từ vựng, suy luận, nhiệm vụ/hành động tiếp theo, đối chiếu hai đoạn — vì dạng câu hỏi chỉ cho bạn phải tìm ở đâu: mục đích nằm ở dòng subject và các câu đầu/cuối, chi tiết nằm ở mỏ neo, từ vựng nằm trong một câu cùng các câu kề bên. (3) Quét mỏ neo rồi chốt: quét tìm mỏ neo lấy từ thân đề — tên riêng, con số, ngày tháng, từ viết hoa — đọc một đến hai câu quanh nó rồi quyết định, tuyệt đối không đọc lại cả đoạn.*

On double passages, answer the single-text questions first, then the both-texts questions, holding a one-line summary of each text in mind. Check answers only after finishing a whole set. When you review, underline the proving line in the passage — the one sentence that settles each question. An answer without a proving line is a guess, even when it is right. Course pace: about 1.5–2 minutes per single passage, about 3 minutes per double set, and every miss goes into the error log with a trap name attached.

*VI: Với bộ đoạn đôi, trả lời trước các câu chỉ liên quan một đoạn, rồi mới đến câu cần cả hai đoạn, trong đầu luôn giữ tóm tắt mỗi đoạn một dòng. Chỉ đối chiếu đáp án sau khi làm xong trọn một bộ. Khi xem lại, gạch dưới dòng chứng minh trong đoạn văn — câu duy nhất quyết định câu hỏi. Câu trả lời không có dòng chứng minh là câu đoán, kể cả khi nó đúng. Nhịp độ theo khóa học: khoảng 1,5–2 phút mỗi đoạn đơn, khoảng 3 phút mỗi bộ đôi, và mỗi câu sai ghi vào sổ lỗi kèm tên cái bẫy.*

## Part 7 — Reading Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2)

Four single passages — an e-mail, a notice, an advertisement, and a schedule — with detail, purpose, and audience questions (Questions 1–8).

*VI: Bốn đoạn văn đơn — e-mail, thông báo, quảng cáo, lịch trình — với các câu hỏi chi tiết, mục đích và đối tượng đọc (câu 1–8).*

### Reading Passage 1 (Questions 1–2)

> **From:** r.oconnell@lakesidefitness.com
> **To:** members@lakesidefitness.com
> **Subject:** Indoor pool closed Monday, March 3
>
> Dear Members,
>
> The indoor pool will be closed on Monday, March 3, from 7:00 A.M. to 2:00 P.M. while a repair team replaces the water filters. The sauna and the stretching room will stay open as usual.
>
> While the work is going on, members may swim at the Grand Street Sports Center at no extra cost — just show your Lakeside membership card at their front desk. Grand Street's outdoor pool is heated, so it is comfortable even in cool weather.
>
> We apologize for the inconvenience. Questions? Please call the front desk at 555-0176.
>
> Rita O'Connell
> Membership Services

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
What will happen at Lakeside Fitness on Monday, March 3, between 7:00 A.M. and 2:00 P.M.?
(A) The sauna will be closed for repair work.
(B) Members will pay an extra fee to swim at Grand Street.
(C) Members will not be able to use the indoor pool while its filters are replaced.
(D) The stretching room will be taken over by the repair team.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Detail question** — the stem's anchors (Monday, March 3, 7:00 A.M.–2:00 P.M.) land on the first sentence: the indoor pool is closed while the filters are replaced. / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo từ đề (thứ Hai 3/3, 7:00–14:00) rơi đúng câu đầu: bể bơi trong nhà đóng cửa trong lúc thay lọc nước.*
- **Traps:** (A) quiet reversal — the sauna "will stay open as usual"; (B) quiet reversal — members swim at Grand Street "at no extra cost"; (D) out of scope — the repair team is never linked to the stretching room.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
Why did Ms. O'Connell send this e-mail?
(A) To tell members about a temporary closure and their options during it.
(B) To invite members to a swimming class at Grand Street.
(C) To remind members to renew their membership cards.
(D) To apologize for a mistake in a member's bill.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Purpose question** — the genre default for a member-wide e-mail with a closure schedule: announce the closure and offer an alternative, the Grand Street pool. / *VI: Dạng mục đích — quy tắc mặc định của e-mail gửi toàn thể hội viên kèm lịch đóng cửa: thông báo việc đóng cửa tạm thời và đưa ra lựa chọn thay thế là bể bơi ở Grand Street.*
- **Traps:** (B) Grand Street appears as a temporary option, not as a class invitation; (C) echo trap — the card only needs to be shown, not renewed; (D) out of scope — no bill is mentioned.

### Reading Passage 2 (Questions 3–4)

> **NOTICE — Fourth-Floor Shared Refrigerator**
>
> Every Friday afternoon, the cleaning staff will empty the shared refrigerator in the fourth-floor kitchen. Any food or drink left inside at 3:00 P.M. on Friday will be thrown away.
>
> If you would like to keep something for the following week, write your name and the date on a label and attach it to the container. Labeled items will be kept until the end of the next week.
>
> Please do not leave food on the counter — the kitchen is not cleaned on weekends. Thank you for keeping the kitchen pleasant for everyone.
>
> — The Facilities Team

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
According to the notice, what will happen to food left in the refrigerator at 3:00 P.M. on Friday without a label?
(A) It will be kept until the end of the following week.
(B) It will be discarded by the cleaning staff.
(C) It will be moved to the kitchen counter.
(D) It will be returned to its owner by the Facilities Team.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** **Detail question** — anchor: food "left inside at 3:00 P.M. on Friday" → "will be thrown away", restated as discarded. / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo: đồ ăn "còn lại trong tủ lúc 15:00 thứ Sáu" → "sẽ bị vứt đi", diễn đạt lại là bị loại bỏ.*
- **Traps:** (A) quiet reversal — kept-until-next-week applies to labeled items only; (C) echo trap — the counter is the one place food must NOT be left; (D) out of scope — the Facilities Team wrote the notice; no returns are made.

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
Who is the intended audience of the notice?
(A) The weekend cleaning staff.
(B) Supervisors of the Facilities Team.
(C) Companies that rent the fourth floor.
(D) Employees who keep food in the shared kitchen.
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** **Audience question** — the imperatives ("write your name", "please do not leave food") are addressed to the people who use the refrigerator — the building's employees. / *VI: Dạng đối tượng đọc — các câu mệnh lệnh ("hãy viết tên bạn", "đừng để thức ăn...") nhắm đến những người dùng chiếc tủ lạnh chung, tức nhân viên trong tòa nhà.*
- **Traps:** (A) the cleaning staff is described, not addressed; (B) echo trap — the Facilities Team is the author, not the reader; (C) out of scope — no outside renters are mentioned.

### Reading Passage 3 (Questions 5–6)

> **HILLCREST BICYCLE WORKS — Spring Tune-Up Special**
>
> Get your bicycle ready for the season! During April, bring your bike to our Fairmount Avenue shop and choose one of our service packages:
>
> | Service | Regular price | April price |
> |---|---|---|
> | Basic Tune-Up | $49 | $35 |
> | Full Service | $89 | $65 |
> | Full Service + new brake pads | $119 | $89 |
>
> Every package includes a free safety check and a wash. No appointment is needed on weekdays before 5:00 P.M. Offer ends April 30 — mention this advertisement to get the discount.
>
> Hillcrest Bicycle Works, 214 Fairmount Ave. · Open Monday–Saturday, 9:00 A.M.–6:00 P.M.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
How much will a customer pay in April for the Full Service with new brake pads?
(A) $35
(B) $65
(C) $89
(D) $119
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Detail question** — row "Full Service + new brake pads", column "April price": $89. / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — dòng "Full Service + new brake pads", cột "April price": 89 đô-la.*
- **Traps:** (A) the April price of the Basic Tune-Up — right column, wrong row; (B) the April price of the Full Service without brake pads — one row off; (D) the regular price of the same package — right row, wrong column.

#### Q6. (Bands: B1 B2)
What is the main purpose of the advertisement?
(A) To publicize limited-time discounts on bicycle services.
(B) To announce the opening of a second shop location.
(C) To recruit technicians experienced in brake repair.
(D) To explain how to book an appointment online.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Purpose question** — the headline ("Spring Tune-Up Special"), the lower April column of the price table, and "Offer ends April 30" all serve one goal: selling discounted services for a limited period. / *VI: Dạng mục đích — tiêu đề "Spring Tune-Up Special", cột giá tháng Tư thấp hơn trong bảng, và câu "Offer ends April 30" cùng phục vụ một mục tiêu: bán dịch vụ giảm giá trong thời gian giới hạn.*
- **Traps:** (B) out of scope — one address is listed, not a new branch; (C) echo trap — brake pads appear only as a service item; (D) quiet reversal — no appointment is needed on weekdays before 5:00 P.M.

Where does the yoga class take place?

- **Traps:** (A) echo trap — instructors are listed, not recruited; (B) out of scope — the schedule lists spring-term evening classes, no children's programs; (C) out of scope — room rental for private events is never mentioned.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3)

Three single passages — a letter, a form, and an article opener — then one linked double passage: an e-mail and the schedule it references (Questions 1–8).

*VI: Ba đoạn văn đơn — một bức thư, một biểu mẫu, phần mở của một bài báo — rồi một bộ đoạn đôi có liên kết: một e-mail kèm lịch trình mà e-mail đó nhắc đến (câu 1–8).*

### Reading Passage 1 (Questions 1–2)

> **Ms. Marta Ngo, Office Manager**
> **Bramwell Insurance Group, 88 Corliss Street, Suite 400**
>
> March 21
>
> Mr. Thomas Vega
> Customer Service Manager
> Ridgeline Office Interiors
>
> Dear Mr. Vega,
>
> Thank you for the quick delivery of our order 58217, which arrived on March 18, ahead of the promised date. The six desk chairs and the bookcase already look excellent in our new reception area.
>
> Unfortunately, the corner workstation in the same shipment arrived with a deep scratch across its top surface. Because our new branch opens on April 1, we cannot wait for a repair visit. I would like to ask you to arrange delivery of a replacement workstation before March 29 and to pick up the damaged piece at the same time. Please confirm the date by Friday so that I can plan the installation.
>
> Sincerely,
> Marta Ngo

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
What does Ms. Ngo request that Mr. Vega do?
(A) Repair the scratched workstation at her office.
(B) Deliver six more chairs before April 1.
(C) Refund the cost of the damaged workstation.
(D) Send a replacement and collect the damaged item.
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** **Task / next-action question** — the request chain in the final paragraph: "arrange delivery of a replacement workstation before March 29 and... pick up the damaged piece at the same time". / *VI: Dạng nhiệm vụ / hành động tiếp theo — chuỗi đề nghị ở đoạn cuối: "giao một bàn làm việc thay thế trước ngày 29/3 và... nhận lại món hàng hư cùng lúc đó".*
- **Traps:** (A) quiet reversal — she cannot wait for a repair visit; (B) wrong text — the six chairs arrived in good condition; (C) out of scope — a refund is never requested.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
What does Ms. Ngo indicate about order 58217?
(A) It arrived earlier than the supplier had committed to.
(B) It was the first order Bramwell had placed with Ridgeline.
(C) It arrived too late for the reception area.
(D) It contained a bookcase that was damaged in transit.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Detail question** — "arrived on March 18, ahead of the promised date", restated: earlier than the supplier committed to. / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — "đến ngày 18/3, sớm hơn ngày đã hứa", nói lại là: đến sớm hơn mức nhà cung cấp đã cam kết.*
- **Traps:** (B) out of scope — the letter says nothing about order history; (C) quiet reversal — the delivery was early and the branch opens April 1; (D) wrong text — the bookcase "looks excellent"; the damaged piece is the workstation.

### Reading Passage 2 (Questions 3–4)

> **PINEHURST PUBLIC LIBRARY — Meeting Room Reservation Form**
>
> | Field | Entry |
> |---|---|
> | Applicant | J. Ferrand |
> | Organization | Northgate Garden Club |
> | Room requested | Conference Room B (seats 20) |
> | Date(s) requested | May 6 and May 20 |
> | Hours | 6:00–8:30 P.M. |
> | Purpose | Monthly members' meeting with a plant-swap activity |
> | Equipment needed | Projector, 6 folding tables |
>
> Room use is free for noncommercial groups. Applications must be received no later than seven working days before the event. The library does not provide laptops. Groups must leave the room by 8:45 P.M., when the building closes. Send completed forms to the front desk or to roombookings@pinehurstlib.org.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
What is true about the reservation requested on the form?
(A) It is for a commercial event lasting until 8:45 P.M.
(B) It is for a room that seats more than twenty people.
(C) It is for two evening meetings in the same month.
(D) It is for a single meeting on May 20.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Detail question** — combine two rows: Date(s) "May 6 and May 20" plus Hours "6:00–8:30 P.M." gives two evening meetings, both in May. / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — ghép hai dòng: "Date(s)" ghi ngày 6/5 và 20/5, cộng "Hours" ghi 18:00–20:30, ra hai buổi tối trong cùng tháng Năm.*
- **Traps:** (A) double reversal — the club is noncommercial and must leave by 8:45; (B) quiet reversal — the room seats exactly twenty; (D) half-right echo — May 20 is real, but it is the second of two dates.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
Why does the Garden Club most likely need folding tables?
(A) To provide seating for twenty dinner guests.
(B) To display plants brought for the swap activity.
(C) To support the library's projector.
(D) To replace furniture the library removed.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** **Inference question** — the Purpose row ties the tables to the "plant-swap activity": a swap most likely needs surfaces to show the plants members bring. / *VI: Dạng suy luận — dòng "Purpose" gắn số bàn với "hoạt động trao đổi cây": một buổi trao đổi gần như chắc chắn cần mặt bàn để trưng bày cây mà thành viên mang đến.*
- **Traps:** (A) echo trap — "seats 20" describes the room, not a dinner; (C) echo trap — the projector is separate equipment, and no table is requested for it; (D) out of scope — no library furniture is removed.

### Reading Passage 3 (Questions 5–6)

> **ARTICLE — Westfield Gazette, March 14: "Market Moves to the Riverfront"**
>
> After fifteen years in the parking lot of City Hall, the Westfield Farmers' Market will open its season on May 4 at a new home: Harbor Plaza, beside the riverfront promenade. The change follows the city's decision to repave the City Hall lot this summer.
>
> The new location gives the market room to grow. Whereas forty vendors crowded into the old lot, Harbor Plaza can accommodate up to sixty-five stalls, and shoppers will find a covered pavilion where bakers and cheese makers can keep their products cool. Market director Amara Osei said the larger site will also allow a weekly cooking demonstration tent.
>
> The market will keep its Saturday hours, 8:00 A.M. to 1:00 P.M., and the free shuttle from Central Station will follow its usual route.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
The word "accommodate" in the second paragraph is closest in meaning to
(A) arrange
(B) build
(C) hold
(D) supply
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Vocabulary-in-context question** — "Harbor Plaza can accommodate up to sixty-five stalls" means the plaza has room to hold that many stalls. / *VI: Dạng từ vựng trong ngữ cảnh — "Harbor Plaza can accommodate up to sixty-five stalls" nghĩa là quảng trường có đủ chỗ để chứa chừng ấy gian hàng.*
- **Traps:** (A) arrange describes planning, not capacity; (B) the plaza already exists — nothing is built; (D) vendors supply products; a place holds them.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
What is the article mainly about?
(A) The relocation of a farmers' market to a larger site
(B) A city project to repave the City Hall parking lot
(C) An increase in the number of bakers at the market
(D) A new shuttle route between the market and Central Station
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Main-idea question** — every paragraph serves one news point: the market is moving to a bigger riverside site for the new season. / *VI: Dạng ý chính — mọi đoạn đều cùng phục vụ một tin chính: chợ nông sản chuyển đến địa điểm mới, rộng hơn, ven sông cho mùa bán mới.*
- **Traps:** (B) true but not the point — the repaving is one sentence of background explaining the move; (C) echo distortion — bakers and cheese makers appear only as pavilion users; (D) quiet reversal — the shuttle keeps its "usual route".
