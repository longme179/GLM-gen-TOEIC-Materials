# Part 3 Drills — Conversations
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 6 conversations, 18 questions

*VI: Phần 3 gồm các đoạn hội thoại ngắn giữa hai hoặc ba người; mỗi đoạn kèm ba câu hỏi. Khóa học này dành cho người tự học nên không có băng nghe: hãy nhờ một người đọc kịch bản, hoặc dán kịch bản vào ứng dụng đọc văn bản thành giọng nói (TTS) để phát hội thoại. Trong mỗi khối dưới đây, kịch bản nằm ngay trên các câu hỏi — đừng liếc nhìn kịch bản trước khi trả lời xong cả ba câu.*

## How to use this file

- **Pick your set by band.** B1 → Set A only. B2 → Set A, then add Set B from Week 2. B3 → Sets B and C. B4/B5 → Sets B and C with timing: answer all three questions within the 8-second pause, and on a second pass ask the reader to speak faster.
- **Always pre-read the three questions before the script is read.** Scan the questions and the four options; underline who, what, when, and where. Predicting the answer is half the work.
- **Never read the script yourself before answering.** Hand the file to your reader (or cover the script lines), keep your eyes on the questions only, and uncover the script afterwards for review. Reading the script with your eyes first destroys the listening practice.
- **For the reader or TTS app:** give each speaker a distinct voice or speed, read at a natural pace, and keep the pause marked at the end of each script.

*VI: Cách dùng: B1 chỉ làm Set A; B2 làm Set A rồi thêm Set B từ tuần thứ hai; B3 làm Set B + C; B4/B5 làm Set B + C và bấm giờ — trả lời xong ba câu trong 8 giây như đề thật. Luôn đọc trước ba câu hỏi trước khi nghe; tuyệt đối không tự nhìn kịch bản trước khi trả lời xong cả ba câu — sau đó mới dùng kịch bản để soát lại.*

*VI: Dành cho người đọc kịch bản hoặc ứng dụng TTS: hãy cho mỗi nhân vật một giọng đọc hoặc tốc độ riêng, đọc với nhịp tự nhiên, và giữ nguyên khoảng tạm dừng được đánh dấu ở cuối mỗi kịch bản.*

## Part 3 — Conversation Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2)

*Two conversations — Questions 1–3 and 4–6 — on clear workplace topics: a schedule change and a supply order.*

### Conversation 1 (Questions 1–3)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> W: Mr. Evans, do you have a minute? There's a change to your week.
> M: Sure, Dana. What's going on?
> W: The quarterly planning meeting won't be on Monday morning anymore. Head office wants everyone at the sales workshop that day.
> M: Oh, right, the workshop. I'd completely forgotten. So when is the planning meeting now?
> W: They moved it to Wednesday, same time, nine o'clock. And it'll be in the small conference room instead of your office.
> M: Good to know. I'll tell the team and update the calendar.
> W: Thanks. One more thing — could you bring the budget figures? The director would like to see them.
> M: Will do. I'll have everything ready the night before.
> [PAUSE 8 s]

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
What are the speakers mainly discussing?
(A) Preparations for a sales workshop
(B) A problem with a conference room
(C) A meeting being moved to another day
(D) A request for budget approval

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The woman opens with "a change to your week": the planning meeting "won't be on Monday morning anymore" and has been moved to Wednesday, so the conversation centers on the meeting's relocation to another day. / *VI: Người nữ mở đầu bằng tin "tuần của anh có một thay đổi": buổi họp lập kế hoạch "không còn vào sáng thứ Hai nữa" mà đã được dời sang thứ Tư, nên toàn bộ đoạn hội thoại xoay quanh việc buổi họp được chuyển sang ngày khác.*
- **Traps:** (A) The workshop is the reason for the change, not the topic itself; (B) the room is one small detail; (D) the director wants to see figures, but nobody asks for approval.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
When will the planning meeting take place?
(A) On Wednesday morning
(B) On Monday morning
(C) On Monday afternoon
(D) On Wednesday afternoon

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The woman says the meeting was moved "to Wednesday, same time, nine o'clock," and nine o'clock is a morning hour, so the meeting is now set for Wednesday morning. / *VI: Người nữ nói buổi họp được "dời sang thứ Tư, vẫn giờ cũ, chín giờ", mà chín giờ là giờ buổi sáng, nên buổi họp giờ diễn ra vào sáng thứ Tư.*
- **Traps:** (B) Monday morning was the original day, before the change; (C) the original day with the wrong part of the day; (D) the right day, the wrong part of the day.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
Why does the woman mention the director?
(A) To warn the man about a complaint
(B) To explain the reason for a request
(C) To offer the man an extra task
(D) To remind the man of an earlier meeting

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** She brings up the director right after asking the man to bring the budget figures, explaining that "the director would like to see them" — that wish is the reason behind her request. / *VI: Cô nhắc đến giám đốc ngay sau khi nhờ ông mang theo số liệu ngân sách, kèm lý do "giám đốc muốn xem các số liệu đó" — mong muốn của giám đốc chính là nguyên nhân của lời đề nghị.*
- **Traps:** (A) Nobody complains about anything; (C) she asks for one thing only; (D) no earlier meeting is brought up.

### Conversation 2 (Questions 4–6)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> M: Hi, this is Ben from Parkside Office Solutions, returning your call about a supply order.
> W: Oh, thanks for calling back. We ordered two boxes of printer paper and a box of pens last Tuesday, but nothing has arrived.
> M: Let me check... I have the order here. It was held up because the item number for the paper was written down incorrectly.
> W: That would explain it. So when will the shipment go out?
> M: It's on the truck this afternoon. You should have everything by Thursday morning at the latest.
> W: That works — we're running low, but we can manage until then. Could you also add a box of file folders to the order?
> M: No problem. I'll send an updated confirmation e-mail in a few minutes.
> [PAUSE 8 s]

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
What is the conversation mainly about?
(A) An incorrect charge on an invoice
(B) A delayed delivery of office supplies
(C) A change to a product's price
(D) The opening of a new office

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The woman reports that although the paper and pens were ordered "last Tuesday," "nothing has arrived," and the whole call centers on when the held-up shipment will finally be delivered. / *VI: Người nữ cho biết dù đã đặt giấy và bút từ "thứ Ba tuần trước" nhưng "chưa có gì đến nơi", và cả cuộc gọi xoay quanh việc lô hàng bị đình trệ bao giờ mới được giao.*
- **Traps:** (A) No invoice or charge is mentioned; (C) no price is discussed; (D) "Parkside Office Solutions" is a supplier's name, not a new office.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
What caused the problem with the order?
(A) The truck broke down on the way
(B) The order was placed too late
(C) The pens were out of stock
(D) A product code was entered incorrectly

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The man states the order "was held up because the item number for the paper was written down incorrectly" — exactly a wrongly entered product code. / *VI: Người nam giải thích đơn hàng "bị đình trệ vì mã hàng của giấy bị ghi sai" — chính là việc mã sản phẩm bị nhập sai.*
- **Traps:** (A) The truck leaves this afternoon — that is the solution, not the problem; (B) the order was placed last Tuesday, early enough; (C) stock is never mentioned.

#### Q6. (Bands: B1 B2)
What can be inferred about the woman's office?
(A) It can get by with what it has for now.
(B) It has already ordered too much.
(C) It cannot afford new supplies.
(D) It prefers buying from a different vendor.

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** When told everything will arrive by Thursday morning, the woman replies "we're running low, but we can manage until then," showing the office can get by on what it has for now. / *VI: Nghe tin mọi thứ sẽ đến trước sáng thứ Năm, người nữ đáp "chúng tôi sắp hết, nhưng vẫn xoay xở được đến lúc đó" — cho thấy văn phòng có thể tạm dựa vào số hàng hiện có.*
- **Traps:** (B) She is waiting for an order, not sitting on a surplus; (C) money is never discussed; (D) she never suggests another vendor.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3)

*Two conversations — Questions 1–3 and 4–6; question numbering restarts from the previous set. Details are tested through paraphrases, and one reply in the conversation is given indirectly.*

### Conversation 1 (Questions 1–3)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> M: Front desk, Marcus speaking. How can I help you today?
> W: Hi, Marcus. It's Elena in Marketing, up on five. The printer on our floor has stopped working, and I have client handouts to prepare for this afternoon.
> M: I'm sorry about that. Is the machine showing any error message?
> W: It says "paper jam," but I've opened every tray and found nothing stuck. I've even tried switching it off and on.
> M: All right, I've logged a ticket — the technician is out on calls this morning, but she can be on your floor after one o'clock.
> W: That may be too late. The presentation is at three, and I need thirty copies before then.
> M: In that case, why not use the printer in the second-floor lounge? It's a heavier model, and it's rarely busy before lunch.
> W: I'll head down there now. And could you call me when she's on her way?
> [PAUSE 8 s]

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
What is the woman's problem?
(A) She has missed a client presentation.
(B) Her presentation has been moved forward.
(C) A machine she relies on is out of order.
(D) She is locked out of her office.

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** Elena reports that "the printer on our floor has stopped working" just when she needs it for client handouts, so the machine she relies on is out of order. / *VI: Elena báo rằng "máy in ở tầng chúng tôi đã ngừng hoạt động" đúng lúc cô cần nó để in tài liệu cho khách, nên chiếc máy cô vẫn dựa vào đã hỏng.*
- **Traps:** (A) The presentation is this afternoon — it has not happened yet; (B) no time change is mentioned; (D) no door or key is mentioned.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
What does the man suggest the woman do?
(A) Use equipment on another floor
(B) Wait until one o'clock for the technician
(C) Clear the jam herself
(D) Reduce the number of handouts

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** Since the technician can only come after one o'clock, the man suggests she "use the printer in the second-floor lounge" — equipment on another floor. / *VI: Vì kỹ thuật viên chỉ có thể đến sau một giờ, người nam gợi ý cô "dùng máy in ở phòng chờ tầng hai" — tức là dùng thiết bị ở một tầng khác.*
- **Traps:** (B) One o'clock is the technician's earliest arrival, which she says may be too late; (C) she has already opened every tray; (D) thirty copies stays thirty — nobody cuts the number.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
Why does the woman mention switching the printer off and on?
(A) To ask for a replacement machine
(B) To complain about the model
(C) To teach the man a technique
(D) To show she has already tried the basic fix

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** Having found no paper stuck in any tray, she adds "I've even tried switching it off and on" — she mentions it to show the basic fix has already been tried and failed. / *VI: Sau khi không thấy giấy kẹt ở khay nào, cô nói thêm "tôi thậm chí đã thử tắt rồi bật lại máy" — cô nhắc việc này để cho thấy cách xử lý cơ bản nhất đã được thử mà không hiệu quả.*
- **Traps:** (A) She never asks for a new printer; (B) she reports a fault, not a complaint about the brand; (C) she is not instructing anyone.

### Conversation 2 (Questions 4–6)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> W: Pavel, do you have a minute to go over Friday's workshop? I want to make sure we're on the same page.
> M: Mostly. The only open item is the room — Room 12 was double-booked, so I requested the one next to the library instead.
> W: That's fine. Now, will you be there for the whole afternoon? Ms. Ito would like someone to take notes.
> M: Hmm — I have a dentist appointment at four, but I can stay until then.
> W: Perfect. That covers the main session, and I can handle the wrap-up on my own.
> M: I'll bring my laptop so the notes are ready to share right away.
> W: Great. Last thing: should we order coffee for fifteen, or has the sign-up list grown?
> M: It's up to eighteen now, so I'd order for twenty to be safe.
> [PAUSE 8 s]

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
What are the speakers mainly discussing?
(A) Notes from a previous workshop
(B) Final arrangements for an upcoming session
(C) A double booking at the library
(D) Coffee preferences of the attendees

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** They tie up the remaining items for Friday's workshop — the room swap, who takes notes, and the coffee order — so they are finalizing arrangements for the upcoming session. / *VI: Hai người xử lý nốt các việc còn lại cho buổi hội thảo thứ Sáu — đổi phòng, phân công ghi biên bản, và gọi cà phê — tức là hoàn tất các chuẩn bị cho buổi sắp diễn ra.*
- **Traps:** (A) The notes are for this coming Friday; (C) the double booking is one solved detail; (D) only the amount of coffee is discussed, not anyone's tastes.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
What does the man indicate about his schedule on Friday?
(A) He is unavailable all afternoon.
(B) He plans to arrive after four o'clock.
(C) He would like to change the workshop time.
(D) He can stay for most of the session.

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The man says he has "a dentist appointment at four, but I can stay until then," and the woman confirms that covers the main session, so he can stay for most of it. / *VI: Người nam nói anh có "lịch nha sĩ lúc bốn giờ, nhưng tôi có thể ở lại đến lúc đó", và người nữ xác nhận như vậy là đủ cho phần chính — nghĩa là anh ở lại được trong hầu hết buổi hội thảo.*
- **Traps:** (A) He is free for most of the afternoon; (B) the appointment pulls him away after four, not in; (C) he never proposes moving the workshop.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
How many servings does the man recommend ordering?
(A) 15
(B) 18
(C) 20
(D) 4

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** With the sign-up list "up to eighteen now," the man recommends "I'd order for twenty to be safe." / *VI: Vì danh sách đăng ký "đã lên tới mười tám người", người nam khuyên "tôi sẽ đặt hai mươi phần cho chắc" — tức là hai mươi suất.*
- **Traps:** (A) Fifteen was the woman's earlier figure; (B) eighteen is the current list, not the order; (D) four is the time of his appointment, not a quantity.

### Set C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5)

*Two conversations — Questions 1–3 and 4–6; numbering restarts again. Expect faster exchanges, idioms, and one changed decision. Conversation 1 has three speakers (W, M, W2).*

### Conversation 1 (Questions 1–3)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> W: Morning, you two. Small wrinkle — the Henderson review just got pushed up. They want our proposal Wednesday, not Friday.
> M: Wednesday? That's brutal. I'd planned to polish the numbers all day Thursday.
> W2: I can have the sales charts ready by tomorrow morning, but I'll need final figures from you tonight.
> M: Tonight's tight — I promised my daughter I'd be at her recital.
> W: Then let's swap things around: send rough numbers after the recital, and Rita can drop them into the deck first thing tomorrow.
> M: That works for me. I'd rather burn the midnight oil than let the client down.
> W2: Should I book the small room, or is the review at Henderson's office?
> W: Their office. And let's touch base at eight tomorrow morning so nothing slips.
> [PAUSE 8 s]

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
What does the woman announce at the start of the conversation?
(A) A client has withdrawn a proposal.
(B) A meeting room has been reserved.
(C) A report must be shortened.
(D) A deadline has been moved up.

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The woman opens by announcing that the Henderson review "just got pushed up" — they now want the proposal on Wednesday instead of Friday, so the deadline has been moved earlier. / *VI: Người nữ mở đầu bằng thông báo buổi đánh giá Henderson "vừa được đẩy sớm lên" — họ muốn đề xuất vào thứ Tư thay vì thứ Sáu, nên hạn chót đã bị dời sớm hơn.*
- **Traps:** (A) The client still wants the proposal; (B) the room question comes later and is answered "their office"; (C) nobody shortens any report.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
What does the man mean when he says "That's brutal"?
(A) The client has treated the team unfairly.
(B) The new timing is very difficult.
(C) The proposal is badly written.
(D) The charts will take too long.

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "That's brutal" is his reaction to the Wednesday deadline, which wrecks his plan to polish the numbers "all day Thursday," so he means the new timing is extremely difficult. / *VI: "That's brutal" là phản ứng của anh trước hạn chót thứ Tư, khiến kế hoạch "cả ngày thứ Năm" dành cho việc hoàn thiện số liệu sụp đổ — ý anh là khung thời gian mới cực kỳ khó khăn.*
- **Traps:** (A) He never blames the client; (C) he worries about time, not writing quality; (D) the charts are Rita's task, and she says they will be ready.

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)
What have the speakers agreed the man will do?
(A) Provide figures late in the evening
(B) Finish the sales charts by tomorrow morning
(C) Skip his daughter's recital
(D) Present the proposal at the client's office

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** Under the rearranged plan he is to "send rough numbers after the recital," which he accepts even though "tonight's tight," so he will provide the figures late in the evening. / *VI: Theo kế hoạch sắp xếp lại, anh sẽ "gửi số liệu thô sau buổi diễn", và anh chấp nhận dù "tối nay rất eo hẹp" — nghĩa là anh sẽ cung cấp số liệu vào buổi tối muộn.*
- **Traps:** (B) The charts are Rita's job; (C) keeping the recital promise is the whole point of the swap; (D) nobody says who will present.

### Conversation 2 (Questions 4–6)

> **Script (for a reader or TTS app):**
> M: So, we're all set for the landing page to go live Monday, right?
> W: About that — I got a look at the vendor's banner artwork this morning, and the colors are off. Nothing like the palette we approved.
> M: Can't they just re-export the file? That sounds like a five-minute fix.
> W: I wish. Their designer is away until Thursday, so the earliest we'd have corrected files is Friday.
> M: Then Monday's off the table. What if we soft-launch without the banners? The page itself is clean.
> W: We could, but I'd rather hold out and launch it properly — a half-finished look isn't the first impression we want.
> M: Fair point. Let's push the whole thing to the following Tuesday, and I'll ask the vendor to expedite.
> W: I'll e-mail them today and confirm the new date with the team.
> [PAUSE 8 s]

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)
What problem does the woman report?
(A) The landing page contains a coding error.
(B) A designer has resigned from the vendor's team.
(C) Artwork from a supplier is unsuitable.
(D) A launch date was forgotten.

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The woman reports that in the vendor's banner artwork "the colors are off," nothing like the approved palette, so the supplier's artwork cannot be used as it stands. / *VI: Người nữ báo rằng trong bản banner của nhà cung cấp thì "màu sắc bị lệch", hoàn toàn khác bảng màu đã duyệt, nên thiết kế của nhà cung cấp không thể dùng được như hiện tại.*
- **Traps:** (A) She says the page itself is clean; (B) the designer is away until Thursday, not gone for good; (D) the date is discussed at length, not forgotten.

#### Q5. (Bands: B4 B5)
What do the speakers finally decide to do?
(A) Launch on Monday without the banners.
(B) Ask a different vendor to redo the artwork.
(C) Correct the colors themselves.
(D) Postpone the launch until the next week.

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** They abandon the Monday soft-launch, and the man concludes "Let's push the whole thing to the following Tuesday," which the woman agrees to confirm with the team — the launch is postponed to the next week. / *VI: Hai người bỏ ý tưởng ra mắt thầm lặng vào thứ Hai, và người nam kết luận "hãy dời toàn bộ sang thứ Ba tuần sau", người nữ đồng ý sẽ xác nhận lại với cả nhóm — đợt ra mắt bị hoãn sang tuần kế tiếp.*
- **Traps:** (A) That idea is raised and then dropped; (B) they keep the same vendor and ask it to hurry; (C) nobody re-exports or repaints the files in-house.

#### Q6. (Bands: B4 B5)
What does the woman say she will do next?
(A) Redesign the banners herself
(B) Contact the supplier about the timing
(C) Schedule a meeting with the designer
(D) Update the web page over the weekend

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The woman closes with "I'll e-mail them today and confirm the new date with the team," so she will contact the vendor about the revised timing. / *VI: Người nữ kết thúc bằng "hôm nay tôi sẽ gửi e-mail cho họ và xác nhận ngày mới với cả nhóm" — cô sẽ liên hệ với nhà cung cấp về lịch trình mới.*
- **Traps:** (A) The banners stay with the vendor; (C) no meeting with the designer is set; (D) the page is already clean, and no weekend work is planned.
