# Part 2 Drills — Question–Response
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 20 items

*VI: Bộ luyện tập Part 2 — hỏi và đáp. Mỗi câu gồm một câu hỏi hoặc một câu phát biểu được đọc thành tiếng, kèm ba câu trả lời (A, B, C); bạn chọn câu trả lời phù hợp nhất. Tổng cộng 20 câu, chia thành ba bộ theo trình độ: A — Nền tảng (B1 B2), B — Lõi (B3), C — Thử thách (B4 B5).*

## How to use this file

- **Pick your set by band.** B1 → Set A only. B2 → Set A now, add Set B from Week 2. B3 → Set B, and add Set C once B feels routine. B4 → Sets B and C. B5 → Set C only.
- **Hear it, never read it.** A partner or a TTS app reads the stem and the three responses aloud, pausing about one second between responses; after the third you have about five seconds to commit. Never do a set by reading the stems and responses yourself.
- **Say the question type out loud before choosing.** Where, when, who, why, yes/no, tag, statement — naming the type first is the surest guard against options that answer a question nobody asked.
- **Log every miss.** Each miss goes to the error log (`P2-reference/error-log-template.md`) with a named trap from the Part 2 taxonomy (`P2-reference/part-2-strategy.md`): echo bait, sound-alike swap, wrong-question answer, yes/no mismatch, detail-stuffed decoy, indirect-answer rejection, statement treated as a question.

*VI: Cách dùng file này: chọn bộ câu theo băng — B1 làm bộ A; B2 làm bộ A, thêm bộ B từ tuần thứ hai; B3 làm bộ B, thêm bộ C khi đã chắc tay; B4 làm bộ B và C; B5 chỉ làm bộ C. Hãy nghe thay vì đọc: nhờ người cùng học hoặc ứng dụng TTS đọc câu hỏi và ba câu trả lời, mỗi câu cách nhau khoảng một giây; sau câu thứ ba, bạn có chừng 5 giây để chốt đáp án. Trước khi chọn, hãy đọc to loại câu hỏi — Where, When, Who, Why, có/không, câu hỏi đuôi hay câu phát biểu — vì gọi đúng loại câu là cách chắc chắn nhất để tránh những phương án trả lời câu mà chẳng ai hỏi. Mỗi câu sai ghi vào nhật ký lỗi kèm tên bẫy: mồi tiếng vang, hoán đổi âm gần giống, trả lời sai loại câu hỏi, lệch khung có/không, mồi nhồi chi tiết, tự loại câu trả lời gián tiếp, coi câu phát biểu là câu hỏi.*

## Part 2 — Question–Response Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2)

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
Where can I pick up my visitor badge?
(A) At the security desk on the ground floor.
(B) Your badge is valid until six o'clock.
(C) Yes, I picked mine up this morning.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The question asks where; A gives the place — the security desk on the ground floor. / *VI: Câu hỏi hỏi "ở đâu"; A đưa ra địa điểm — quầy bảo vệ ở tầng trệt.*
- **Traps:** (B) answers "How long is the badge valid?" — a time offered to a place question; (C) a "Yes" is glued onto a where-question, and it talks about the listener's own badge, not the speaker's.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
When does the parking garage close on Saturdays?
(A) It's across from the main tower.
(B) At seven in the evening, I think.
(C) I usually park on level three.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The question asks when; B gives the closing time — seven in the evening. / *VI: Câu hỏi hỏi "khi nào"; B đưa ra giờ đóng cửa — bảy giờ tối.*
- **Traps:** (A) answers "Where is the garage?" — a place for a time; (C) answers "Where do you usually park?" — the listener's habit, a question nobody asked.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
Who's in charge of ordering office supplies?
(A) In the cabinet next to the kitchen.
(B) The order form is on the staff intranet.
(C) Ms. Ruiz in the administration department.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The question asks who; C names the person — Ms. Ruiz in administration. / *VI: Câu hỏi hỏi "ai"; C nêu đúng người phụ trách — chị Ruiz ở bộ phận hành chính.*
- **Traps:** (A) answers "Where are the supplies kept?" — a place for a person; (B) answers "Where is the order form?" — a process detail, not the person in charge.

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
You've already met the new accountant, haven't you?
(A) We were introduced at the holiday party, actually.
(B) Yes, the accounting department is on the fourth floor.
(C) She'll start on the first of the month.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The tag asks whether the listener has met the accountant; being introduced at the holiday party means the answer is yes. / *VI: Câu hỏi đuôi hỏi rằng bạn đã gặp chưa; "được giới thiệu ở bữa tiệc" nghĩa là đã gặp.*
- **Traps:** (B) a "Yes" is glued to a location — it answers "Where is the accounting department?", not the tag; (C) answers "When does she start?" — a schedule for a yes/no.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
I can't get the vending machine to accept my coins.
(A) The machine was installed last year.
(B) Try the one in the basement — this one jams all the time.
(C) Yes, the drinks there are quite cheap.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** A statement of trouble calls for help, not a yes/no; B offers a working alternative machine. / *VI: Câu than phiền cần được giúp đỡ chứ không cần trả lời có/không; B gợi ý một máy khác dùng được.*
- **Traps:** (A) answers "When was it installed?" — a fact about the machine, offered to a complaint; (C) treats the statement as a yes/no question and praises prices nobody mentioned.

#### Q6. (Bands: B1 B2)
What time does the morning shuttle leave?
(A) From the side entrance on Park Road.
(B) Every fifteen minutes during rush hour.
(C) At a quarter past seven.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The question asks what time; C gives the departure time — a quarter past seven. / *VI: Câu hỏi hỏi "mấy giờ"; C đưa ra giờ xe xuất phát — bảy giờ mười lăm.*
- **Traps:** (A) answers "Where does it leave from?" — a place for a clock time; (B) answers "How often does it run?" — a frequency for a departure time.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3)

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
When does the new intern start?
(A) The internship runs for three months.
(B) Right after the holiday weekend.
(C) The intern's desk is by the window.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Right after the holiday weekend" answers When without echoing any word of the question — an indirect answer. / *VI: "Ngay sau kỳ nghỉ lễ dài" trả lời câu hỏi When mà không lặp lại từ nào của câu hỏi — một câu trả lời gián tiếp đúng kiểu.*
- **Traps:** (A) answers "How long is the internship?" — a duration for a start date; (C) the echo bait — "intern" is repeated, but the response says where the desk is, not when she starts.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
Why is the loading dock closed today?
(A) The loading dock is behind Building C.
(B) The dock supervisor arrives at eight.
(C) They're resurfacing the pavement, so deliveries are going to the side entrance.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** C gives the reason and a helpful extra instruction — both parts answer Why. / *VI: C nêu lý do (lại mặt đường) kèm chỉ dẫn bổ ích — cả hai đều trả lời cho Why.*
- **Traps:** (A) the echo bait — "loading dock" is repeated, but a location is offered, not a reason; (B) answers "When does the supervisor arrive?" — a time for a reason.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
Did you send the invoice to the Chicago office?
(A) Yes — I e-mailed it yesterday afternoon.
(B) I haven't received any invitations this week.
(C) The clients visited Chicago in the spring.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** A direct "Yes" plus the proof — e-mailed yesterday afternoon — answers the yes/no question completely. / *VI: Một lời "Yes" kèm bằng chứng đã gửi email chiều qua đáp trọn câu hỏi có/không.*
- **Traps:** (B) the sound-alike swap — "invoice" drifts into "invitations," and receiving invitations answers a different question; (C) echoes "Chicago" but answers "When did the clients visit?" — a different event.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
Have you had a chance to review the contract?
(A) The contact list is in the shared drive.
(B) It covers two years of servicing.
(C) Not yet, but it's first on my list after lunch.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Not yet" plus a promise answers the yes/no question indirectly but completely. / *VI: "Not yet" cộng một lời hứa trả lời câu hỏi có/không theo cách gián tiếp nhưng đầy đủ.*
- **Traps:** (A) the sound-alike swap — "contract" drifts into "contact"; (B) echoes "contract" but answers "What does it cover?" — a content question nobody asked.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
How often does the department newsletter come out?
(A) Twice a month, usually on Fridays.
(B) The marketing team edits it.
(C) I read it on the train home.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Twice a month, usually on Fridays" gives a frequency — exactly what How often asks for. / *VI: "Hai lần một tháng, thường vào thứ Sáu" đưa ra đúng tần suất mà How often cần.*
- **Traps:** (B) answers "Who edits it?" — a doer for a frequency; (C) answers the hidden "When do you read it?" — a personal habit, not a publication schedule.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
Where should we hang the new fire-safety posters?
(A) They were delivered this morning.
(B) The fire drill is scheduled for March.
(C) How about the wall by the elevators, where everyone will see them?
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** C proposes a place, and a suggestion counts as a full answer to Where. / *VI: C đề xuất một địa điểm — một lời đề xuất cũng là câu trả lời trọn vẹn cho Where.*
- **Traps:** (A) echoes "posters" but answers "When were they delivered?"; (B) echoes "fire" but answers "When is the drill?" — a real date, a wrong question.

#### Q7. (Bands: B3)
These sample boxes are heavier than I expected.
(A) The samples come in three sizes.
(B) Let me give you a hand with those.
(C) Yes, they were delivered on Tuesday.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The statement hides a request for help; B offers exactly that. / *VI: Câu phát biểu ẩn lời nhờ giúp đỡ; B đáp đúng điều được ngầm đề nghị.*
- **Traps:** (A) echoes "samples" but answers "What sizes do they come in?"; (C) treats the statement as a yes/no and answers "When were they delivered?" — two wrong frames stacked.

### Set C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5)

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
Is there any chance the conference room is free at two?
(A) The conference room seats twelve people.
(B) Not unless the budget review gets moved — it's booked until three.
(C) Yes, the quarterly meeting starts at two sharp.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Not unless…" is the idiomatic indirect answer: the room is not free, and the condition for freeing it is named. / *VI: "Not unless…" là cách trả lời gián tiếp rất tự nhiên: phòng chưa trống, và điều kiện để có phòng cũng được nêu rõ.*
- **Traps:** (A) echoes "conference room" but answers "How many does it seat?"; (C) echoes "two" but reports a different meeting's start time — it answers "When does the quarterly meeting start?", not whether the room is free.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
The merger isn't official yet, is it?
(A) Yes, it was announced last month.
(B) The merger involves two overseas partners.
(C) Right — the paperwork won't be complete until June.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Right" agrees with the negative statement — the merger is not official yet — and adds the reason. / *VI: "Right" đồng ý với phát biểu phủ định — thương vụ chưa chính thức — và nêu thêm lý do.*
- **Traps:** (A) the negation flip — "Yes" cannot confirm "isn't official," and last month's announcement did not make the merger final; (B) echoes "merger" but answers "Who is involved?" — nobody asked.

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)
I don't suppose you know whether Ms. Tanaka kept a copy of the lease?
(A) Your best bet is the filing cabinet in her old office.
(B) She retired at the end of April.
(C) The lease runs through December.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Your best bet is…" answers the real question behind the polite yes/no — where to look for the copy. / *VI: "Your best bet is…" trả lời câu hỏi thực sự ẩn sau vỏ có/không — nên tìm bản sao ở đâu.*
- **Traps:** (B) answers the hidden "When did she retire?" — true about Ms. Tanaka, useless for the lease; (C) echoes "lease" but answers "How long does it run?"

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)
Would you mind if I opened the window?
(A) Yes, this one faces the courtyard.
(B) Not at all — go right ahead.
(C) The heating on this floor switches off after six.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Not at all" is the idiomatic way to grant permission — "I don't mind." / *VI: "Not at all" là cách nói tự nhiên để đồng ý cho phép — nghĩa là "tôi không hề ngại".*
- **Traps:** (A) "Yes" would mean "I do mind," and the courtyard detail answers a hidden "Which way does it face?"; (C) a building fact that answers no question asked — decoration, not response.

#### Q5. (Bands: B4 B5)
How did the inspection at the food plant go?
(A) The inspectors stayed at a downtown hotel.
(B) The plant tour takes about an hour.
(C) Without a hitch — we passed on the first attempt.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Without a hitch" is an idiom for "with no problems," and it answers How did it go. / *VI: "Without a hitch" là thành ngữ nghĩa "không một trục trặc" — trả lời đúng câu hỏi How did it go.*
- **Traps:** (A) answers the hidden "Where did they stay?"; (B) echoes "plant" but answers "How long does the tour take?" — a duration for an outcome.

#### Q6. (Bands: B4 B5)
If I want to add a workshop to my registration, is there a deadline?
(A) Not until the Friday before — after that you pay a late fee.
(B) The workshops are all held in the west hall.
(C) Yes, I signed up for two myself.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Not until the Friday before" gives the deadline inside a polite, indirect shape, with the late-fee warning as a bonus. / *VI: "Not until the Friday before" đưa ra hạn cuối trong vỏ bọc lịch sự gián tiếp, kèm cảnh báo phí trễ.*
- **Traps:** (B) echoes "workshop" but answers "Where is it held?"; (C) answers the hidden "Did you sign up?" — a yes/no about the wrong person.

#### Q7. (Bands: B4 B5)
It looks like we won't need the overflow room after all.
(A) The overflow room holds fifty people.
(B) That's what I heard — the RSVPs came in low.
(C) Yes, I reserved it last week, just in case.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "That's what I heard" agrees with the negative statement, and the low RSVP count explains it. / *VI: "That's what I heard" đồng ý với ý phủ định của câu phát biểu, và số người xác nhận tham dự ít giải thích thêm.*
- **Traps:** (A) echoes "overflow room" but answers "How many does it hold?"; (C) a "Yes" is glued onto a statement that was never a question, and the reservation answers a hidden "Did anyone book it?"
