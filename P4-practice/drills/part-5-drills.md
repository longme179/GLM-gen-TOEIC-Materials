**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 30 items

Sau mỗi câu là đáp án, giải thích tiếng Anh, phần diễn giải tiếng Việt và phân tích bẫy. Hãy làm theo từng bộ (Set) đúng với băng trình độ hiện tại và chỉ xem đáp án sau khi đã làm xong trọn bộ.*

## How to use this file

1. **Start with the set that matches your band.** Set A (B1–B2) builds the core patterns, Set B (B3) is the heart of the real test, Set C (B4–B5) trains the rare traps that separate the top bands. When a set feels comfortable, move up one set — never down two.
2. **Apply the 3-second triage.** Before you look at the options, label the blank out loud: "verb form", "noun", "adjective", "connector", "preposition", or "word choice". Only then read (A)–(D). The options are written to confirm a label you already hold — if you have no label, the options will decide for you, and that is how traps get chosen.
3. **Check answers per set, not per item.** Do all ten items of a set on paper, then read the **Key**, **Why**, and **Traps** lines together. Read both the English explanation and the *VI:* line — the English line teaches you the terminology, the Vietnamese line makes sure you actually understood it.
4. **Day 23 of the course runs ALL 46 Part 5+6 items in ~22 minutes**: the 30 items in this file followed by the 16 blanks of `part-6-drills.md`, in one timed circuit. The Day-23 checkpoint: Part 5 finished by minute 10 (about 20 seconds per item), Part 6 in the remaining ~12 minutes. Until then, work for accuracy — speed is trained on Day 23, not here.

## Part 5 — Incomplete Sentence Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2) — 10 items (Q1–Q10)

*Focus: word forms ×3 (Q1–Q3), subject–verb agreement ×2 (Q4–Q5), simple tenses ×2 (Q6–Q7), preposition ×1 (Q8), basic connector ×1 (Q9), word choice ×1 (Q10).*

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
The director praised the team for handling the client's complaint ----.
(A) efficient
(B) efficiently
(C) efficiency
(D) efficiencies

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The blank tells us how the team handled the complaint, so it modifies the verb "handling" and must be an adverb. / *VI: Chỗ trống nói về cách nhóm đã xử lý khiếu nại, tức là bổ nghĩa cho động từ "handling", nên phải dùng trạng từ "efficiently" (một cách hiệu quả).*
- **Traps:** (A) "efficient" is the adjective form and cannot modify a verb; (C) and (D) are nouns — "efficiency" is the abstract quality, "efficiencies" a plural count of savings.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
All elevators in the tower undergo a ---- inspection every six months.
(A) schedule
(B) scheduling
(C) scheduled
(D) schedules

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The blank sits between the article "a" and the noun "inspection," so an adjective is required; "scheduled" (planned in advance) is a past participle working as an adjective. / *VI: Chỗ trống nằm giữa mạo từ "a" và danh từ "inspection" nên cần tính từ; "scheduled" (được lên lịch sẵn) là quá khứ phân từ dùng như tính từ.*
- **Traps:** (A) is a verb or a noun; (B) is the -ing form, used for processes, not before a noun with this meaning; (D) is a third-person verb or a plural noun.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
Ms. Tran received a bonus in recognition of her outstanding ---- during the busy season.
(A) perform
(B) performed
(C) performer
(D) performance

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** After the possessive "her" and the adjective "outstanding," only a noun fits; "performance" means how well someone did the job. / *VI: Sau tính từ sở hữu "her" và tính từ "outstanding" chỉ có thể là danh từ; "performance" = hiệu suất, thành tích công việc.*
- **Traps:** (A) is the base verb; (B) is a past form; (C) "performer" is a person who performs on stage.

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
The list of approved vendors ---- posted on the intranet every Monday.
(A) is
(B) are
(C) were
(D) have been

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The grammatical subject is "The list" (singular); "of approved vendors" is only a prepositional phrase and cannot control the verb. "Every Monday" signals the simple present. / *VI: Chủ ngữ ngữ pháp là "The list" (số ít); "of approved vendors" chỉ là cụm giới từ, không quyết định động từ. "Every Monday" báo hiệu hiện tại đơn.*
- **Traps:** (B) and (D) agree with "vendors," the nearest noun instead of the true subject; (C) is both plural and past.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
Two of the new interns ---- sharing an office on the third floor.
(A) is
(B) was
(C) are
(D) has been

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The subject is "Two (of the new interns)," which is plural, and the sentence describes a current, ongoing arrangement. / *VI: Chủ ngữ là "Two" (hai thực tập sinh, số nhiều) và câu mô tả việc đang diễn ra ở hiện tại, nên dùng "are sharing".*
- **Traps:** (A) and (D) are singular verbs; (B) is both singular and past.

#### Q6. (Bands: B1 B2)
The delivery driver usually ---- his route at 7 A.M., before traffic gets heavy.
(A) begun
(B) beginning
(C) has begun
(D) begins

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Usually" marks a habitual action, so the simple present is needed, and the third-person singular subject takes "begins." / *VI: "Usually" diễn tả thói quen lặp lại nên dùng hiện tại đơn; chủ ngữ ngôi ba số ít phải thêm -s: "begins".*
- **Traps:** (A) "begun" is a past participle and cannot stand alone; (B) is a participle/gerund; (C) present perfect does not describe a routine with "usually."

#### Q7. (Bands: B1 B2)
Last Friday, the accounting department ---- the quarterly statements to all clients.
(A) sends
(B) sending
(C) send
(D) sent

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Last Friday" is a finished past time marker, so the simple past "sent" is required. / *VI: "Last Friday" là mốc thời gian quá khứ đã kết thúc nên phải dùng quá khứ đơn "sent".*
- **Traps:** (A) is present simple; (B) cannot serve as the main verb of the sentence; (C) is the base form and matches no subject here.

#### Q8. (Bands: B1 B2)
Because of a scheduling conflict, the budget meeting was moved ---- June 12.
(A) at
(B) to
(C) on
(D) in

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Move to + a date" is the fixed pattern for changing a planned date — the meeting moves from one date to another. / *VI: "Moved to + ngày" là cụm cố định khi dời lịch: cuộc họp được dời sang ngày 12/6.*
- **Traps:** (C) "on" is tempting because dates normally take "on," but here the preposition belongs to "moved," not to the date; (A) and (D) never follow "move" in this pattern.

#### Q9. (Bands: B1 B2)
The flight was delayed, ---- several passengers missed their connecting trains.
(A) so
(B) but
(C) or
(D) yet

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The first clause is the cause (the delay) and the second is its result (missed trains); "so" links a cause to its result. / *VI: Vế đầu là nguyên nhân (chuyến bay trễ), vế sau là kết quả (hành khách lỡ tàu); "so" nối nguyên nhân với kết quả.*
- **Traps:** (B) and (D) both signal contrast, which reverses the logic; (C) presents a choice or alternative.

#### Q10. (Bands: B1 B2)
Please ---- the attached document carefully before signing the contract.
(A) review
(B) agree
(C) deliver
(D) inform

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Review a document" means read and check it carefully; it is the only verb here that takes "the attached document" as a direct object with this meaning. / *VI: "Review a document" nghĩa là đọc và xem xét kỹ tài liệu; chỉ "review" mới lấy "document" làm tân ngữ trực tiếp với nghĩa này.*
- **Traps:** (B) "agree" needs "with" and a person or proposal, not a document to read; (C) "deliver" means carry something to someone; (D) "inform" needs a person as its object.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3) — 10 items (Q1–Q10)

*Focus: passive voice (Q1–Q2), perfect tenses (Q3–Q4), relative pronouns (Q5–Q6), despite / because of (Q7–Q8), collocations (Q9), comparatives (Q10).*

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
All expense claims must ---- by a department head before reimbursement is issued.
(A) approve
(B) approving
(C) be approved
(D) approved

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The claims receive the approval, so after the modal "must" the passive is needed: "must + be + past participle." / *VI: Đơn chi phí được trưởng phòng phê duyệt (chịu tác động), nên sau "must" cần dạng bị động: "must be approved".*
- **Traps:** (A) is the active base verb; (B) is a gerund; (D) is missing "be."

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
The results of the safety inspection ---- to the regional office last Monday.
(A) were sent
(B) sent
(C) were sending
(D) have sent

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The plural subject "results" receives the action, and "last Monday" requires the past: the past passive "were sent." / *VI: Chủ ngữ "results" (số nhiều) chịu tác động của hành động, kèm mốc quá khứ "last Monday" → bị động quá khứ "were sent".*
- **Traps:** (B) is active past; (C) suggests the results themselves were sending something; (D) is present perfect active.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
Ms. Alvarez ---- for the Dublin office since she joined the company in 2019.
(A) works
(B) is working
(C) worked
(D) has worked

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Since + a past event" (since she joined) demands the present perfect for the action that continues up to now. / *VI: "Since + sự kiện quá khứ" (từ khi cô gia nhập công ty) đòi hỏi hiện tại hoàn thành cho hành động kéo dài đến hiện tại.*
- **Traps:** (A) simple present ignores the link to the past starting point; (B) present progressive suggests a temporary arrangement; (C) simple past closes the action entirely in the past.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
By the time the auditor arrived, the accounting team ---- all of the receipts.
(A) organized
(B) has organized
(C) had organized
(D) organizes

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** Of the two past events, organizing was completed before the auditor arrived, and the past perfect marks the earlier action. / *VI: Trong hai hành động quá khứ, việc sắp xếp hóa đơn hoàn tất trước khi kiểm toán viên đến; quá khứ hoàn thành "had organized" đánh dấu hành động xảy ra trước.*
- **Traps:** (A) simple past loses the before/after sequence; (B) present perfect cannot sit before a past reference point; (D) is a present form.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
The consultant ---- report was submitted last week will present her findings tomorrow.
(A) who
(B) whose
(C) which
(D) whom

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The report belongs to the consultant, so the possessive relative pronoun "whose" introduces the clause. / *VI: Báo cáo thuộc về chuyên gia tư vấn, nên cần đại từ quan hệ sở hữu "whose" (của người đó) để mở đầu mệnh đề.*
- **Traps:** (A) "who" is a subject pronoun; (C) "which" refers to things; (D) "whom" is an object pronoun for people.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
The storage room ---- the spare parts are kept is locked after 6 P.M.
(A) where
(B) that
(C) which
(D) whom

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The clause "the spare parts are kept" already has its subject and verb; what is missing is the place link back to "the storage room," and the relative adverb "where" (= in which) provides it. / *VI: Mệnh đề "the spare parts are kept" đã có đủ chủ ngữ và động từ; điều thiếu là liên kết về nơi chốn với "the storage room", và trạng từ quan hệ "where" (= nơi mà) đảm nhận việc đó.*
- **Traps:** (B) and (C) would have to play a subject or object role inside the clause, which already has both; (D) refers to people.

#### Q7. (Bands: B3)
---- the heavy rain, the outdoor product launch went ahead as planned.
(A) Because of
(B) Owing to
(C) Despite
(D) As a result of

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** Heavy rain would normally stop an outdoor launch, but it happened anyway — that is contrast, and "Despite + noun phrase" expresses it. / *VI: Mưa to lẽ ra phải làm hoãn buổi ra mắt sản phẩm ngoài trời, nhưng sự kiện vẫn diễn ra — đó là quan hệ tương phản; "Despite + cụm danh từ" diễn tả đúng nghĩa này.*
- **Traps:** (A), (B), and (D) all express cause, which contradicts the logic of the sentence.

#### Q8. (Bands: B3)
The packaging line was shut down ---- a fault in the sealing machine.
(A) despite
(B) because of
(C) although
(D) whereas

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The fault caused the shutdown, and "because of + noun phrase" gives the reason. / *VI: Lỗi của máy hàn chân là nguyên nhân khiến chuyền đóng gói phải dừng; "because of + cụm danh từ" chỉ nguyên nhân.*
- **Traps:** (A) gives contrast instead of cause; (C) "although" and (D) "whereas" are conjunctions and would each need a full clause.

#### Q9. (Bands: B3)
The company decided to ---- a survey to find out why customers were canceling their subscriptions.
(A) conduct
(B) commit
(C) conclude
(D) confirm

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The standard collocation is "conduct a survey" — to carry it out and collect the answers. / *VI: Cụm collocation chuẩn là "conduct a survey" — tiến hành một cuộc khảo sát để thu thập câu trả lời.*
- **Traps:** (B) "commit" means promise or dedicate; (C) "conclude" means finish or reach a decision; (D) "confirm" means verify — none of them pairs with "survey."

#### Q10. (Bands: B3)
The new printer is far ---- than the model it replaced.
(A) most efficient
(B) efficient
(C) so efficient
(D) more efficient

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Than" signals comparison, and "far" is an intensifier that sits directly before a comparative form: "far more efficient than." / *VI: Có "than" là dấu hiệu so sánh hơn; "far" là từ nhấn mạnh đứng ngay trước dạng so sánh: "far more efficient than".*
- **Traps:** (A) is a superlative and would need "the ... of all"; (B) is the base adjective and cannot pair with "than"; (C) "so" needs the "so ... that" pattern.

### Set C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5) — 10 items (Q1–Q10)

*Focus: subjunctive after insist / recommend (Q1–Q2), reduced relative clauses (Q3–Q4), inversion after negative openers (Q5–Q6), fine collocation distinctions — comply with / conform to (Q7–Q8), word-form pairs in rare slots (Q9–Q10).*

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
The safety inspector insisted that every visitor ---- a hard hat before entering the site.
(A) wears
(B) wear
(C) is wearing
(D) has worn

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Insist that" in its demanding sense triggers the mandative subjunctive: the base form "wear," with no -s even after "every visitor." / *VI: "Insist that" (kiên quyết yêu cầu) đòi hỏi giả định cách: dùng động từ nguyên mẫu "wear", không thêm -s dù chủ ngữ là "every visitor".*
- **Traps:** (A) looks like perfect subject–verb agreement but is the indicative present, which is not used after "insist that" in this sense; (C) and (D) are ordinary tenses that the structure rejects as well.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
It is strongly recommended that the final report ---- submitted at least two weeks before the review.
(A) is
(B) was
(C) be
(D) will be

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Recommend that" requires the subjunctive, and because the report is submitted by someone, the passive subjunctive "be submitted" is needed. / *VI: "Recommend that" đòi hỏi giả định cách; báo cáo được người khác nộp (bị động) nên dùng "be submitted".*
- **Traps:** (A), (B), and (D) are ordinary indicative forms, which formal usage rejects after "recommend that."

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)
The applicants ---- for the second round of interviews will be contacted by phone this week.
(A) selected
(B) selecting
(C) were selected
(D) have selected

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** This is a reduced relative clause — "the applicants who were selected" — and the past participle "selected" keeps the passive meaning in shorter form. / *VI: Đây là mệnh đề quan hệ rút gọn của "who were selected" (được chọn); quá khứ phân từ "selected" giữ nguyên nghĩa bị động.*
- **Traps:** (B) "selecting" is active, as if the applicants did the selecting; (C) turns the phrase into a full clause and breaks the sentence structure; (D) changes the meaning to an action the applicants performed.

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)
Anyone ---- in the summer internship program should submit an application by Friday.
(A) interesting
(B) interests
(C) interested
(D) interestingly

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Anyone (who is) interested in ..." reduces to "Anyone interested in ..."; the -ed participial adjective describes the feeling of the person. / *VI: "Anyone (who is) interested in..." rút gọn thành "Anyone interested in..."; tính từ participial đuôi -ed mô tả cảm nhận của chính con người.*
- **Traps:** (A) "interesting" describes what causes the feeling, not the person who feels it; (B) is a verb or a plural noun; (D) is an adverb.

#### Q5. (Bands: B4 B5)
Not until the new software was installed ---- any improvement in processing times.
(A) employees noticed
(B) did employees notice
(C) employees have noticed
(D) employees did notice

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** When "Not until ..." opens the sentence, the main clause must invert: the auxiliary "did" comes before the subject — "did employees notice." / *VI: Khi "Not until..." đứng đầu câu, mệnh đề chính bắt buộc phải đảo: trợ động từ "did" lên trước chủ ngữ — "did employees notice".*
- **Traps:** (A), (C), and (D) all keep normal word order, which is not allowed after this negative opener; (D) adds "did" for emphasis but still refuses the inversion.

#### Q6. (Bands: B4 B5)
Not only ---- the deadline, but the team also finished the project under budget.
(A) the team met
(B) the team did meet
(C) met the team
(D) did the team meet

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Not only" at the start of the clause forces inversion in the first half: "Not only did the team meet ..., but ... also ...." / *VI: "Not only" đứng đầu câu bắt buộc phải đảo trợ động từ ở vế đầu: "Not only did the team meet..., but ... also ...".*
- **Traps:** (A) and (B) lack inversion; (C) reverses the words but without an auxiliary, which turns "the team" into an object.

#### Q7. (Bands: B4 B5)
All subsidiaries are required to ---- with the parent company's data-security standards.
(A) comply
(B) conform
(C) correspond
(D) cooperate

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Comply with + rules/standards" means obey them — the exact meaning here, and the only one of the four verbs that pairs with "with" in this sense. / *VI: "Comply with + quy định/tiêu chuẩn" nghĩa là tuân thủ — đúng nghĩa câu cần, và là từ duy nhất trong bốn từ kết hợp với "with" theo nghĩa này.*
- **Traps:** (B) "conform" pairs with "to"; (C) "correspond with" means match or exchange letters; (D) "cooperate with" means work together with someone.

#### Q8. (Bands: B4 B5)
Every product must ---- to the latest European safety regulations before it can be shipped.
(A) comply
(B) conform
(C) submit
(D) obey

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The blank is followed by "to," and "conform to + regulations" means meet a required standard — the pattern fits both grammar and meaning. / *VI: Chỗ trống đứng trước "to"; "conform to + quy định" nghĩa là đạt/khớp với tiêu chuẩn yêu cầu — vừa đúng ngữ pháp vừa đúng nghĩa.*
- **Traps:** (A) "comply" needs "with"; (C) "submit to" means give in to someone's authority; (D) "obey" takes a direct object with no preposition.

#### Q9. (Bands: B4 B5)
Compared with last year's figures, this quarter's earnings were ---- lower.
(A) significance
(B) significant
(C) significantly
(D) signify

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** An adverb is required to modify the comparative adjective "lower": "significantly lower" means lower by a large amount. / *VI: Cần một trạng từ để bổ nghĩa cho tính từ so sánh "lower": "significantly lower" nghĩa là thấp hơn hẳn.*
- **Traps:** (A) is a noun; (B) is an adjective and cannot modify another adjective; (D) is a verb.

#### Q10. (Bands: B4 B5)
The redesigned delivery route is far more ---- than the old one, which helps the company save on fuel.
(A) economic
(B) economically
(C) economy
(D) economical

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** After "more ... than," an adjective is needed; "economical" means cost-saving, which the fuel-saving clause confirms. / *VI: Sau "more ... than" cần một tính từ; "economical" nghĩa là tiết kiệm chi phí, được vế nói về việc tiết kiệm nhiên liệu xác nhận.*
- **Traps:** (A) "economic" relates to the economy as a whole (economic growth, economic policy) and does not mean thrifty; (B) is an adverb; (C) is a noun.
