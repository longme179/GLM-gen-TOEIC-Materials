# Part 1 Drills — Photographs (Scene Cards)
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Sets:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 12 items

*VI: Bộ luyện tập Part 1 — mô tả hình ảnh bằng thẻ cảnh. Vì khóa học không dùng ảnh thật, mỗi câu hỏi gồm một thẻ mô tả cảnh đóng vai bức ảnh, kèm bốn câu mô tả được đọc thành tiếng; bạn chọn câu mô tả đúng với cảnh trong thẻ. Tổng cộng 12 câu, chia thành ba bộ theo trình độ: A — Nền tảng (B1 B2), B — Lõi (B3), C — Thử thách (B4 B5).*

## How to use this file

- **Pick your set by band.** B1 → Set A only. B2 → Set A now, add Set B from Week 2. B3 → Set B, and add Set C once B feels routine. B4 → Sets B and C. B5 → Set C only.
- **The scene card is your "photo."** Study the card silently for about five seconds, then have a partner or a TTS app read the four statements aloud — twice at B1–B2, once from B3 up, about four seconds apart. Never read the statements with your own eyes while drilling.
- **Commit, check, log.** Commit within about five seconds of the last statement, then check the key. Every miss goes to the error log (`P2-reference/error-log-template.md`) with a named trap from the Part 1 taxonomy (`P2-reference/part-1-strategy.md`): wrong doer, sound-alike verb, passive confusion, swapped roles, partly true statement.

*VI: Cách dùng file này: chọn bộ câu theo băng — B1 làm bộ A; B2 làm bộ A, thêm bộ B từ tuần thứ hai; B3 làm bộ B, thêm bộ C khi đã chắc tay; B4 làm bộ B và C; B5 chỉ làm bộ C. Thẻ mô tả cảnh chính là "bức ảnh" của bạn: đọc thẻ trong im lặng chừng 5 giây, rồi nhờ người cùng học hoặc ứng dụng chuyển văn bản thành giọng đọc to bốn câu mô tả — đọc hai lần với trình độ B1–B2, một lần từ B3 trở lên, mỗi câu cách nhau khoảng 4 giây; tuyệt đối không tự đọc câu lựa chọn bằng mắt trong lúc luyện. Chốt đáp án trong vòng 5 giây sau câu cuối rồi mới xem phần giải. Mỗi câu sai đều ghi vào nhật ký lỗi kèm tên bẫy: sai người thực hiện, động từ âm gần giống, nhầm câu bị động, hoán đổi vai, câu nửa đúng.*

## Part 1 — Scene Card Drills

### Set A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2)

### Scene Card 1 — Corner Desk
> In a small office, a woman is talking on a desk phone while typing on her laptop. Behind her, a colleague is watering a plant on the windowsill. The office door is closed, and nobody is standing near it.

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
(A) The woman is watering the plant on the windowsill.
(B) A woman is talking on the phone while she works on her computer.
(C) A woman is opening the office door for a visitor.
(D) The colleague is fixing the office printer.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The woman is on a desk phone and typing on her laptop at the same time — B restates both actions with "while she works on her computer." / *VI: Người phụ nữ vừa gọi điện thoại bàn vừa gõ phím trên máy tính xách tay — B diễn đạt lại cả hai hành động là "talking on the phone while she works on her computer".*
- **Traps:** (A) wrong doer — the colleague is the one watering the plant; (C) the door is closed and nobody is near it, so nothing is being opened; (D) invented action — the colleague is watering a plant, and nothing in the scene is being repaired.

### Scene Card 2 — Café Counter
> In a café, a barista is handing a paper cup across the counter to a customer, who is reaching for it with one hand. At a small table by the window, a man is reading a newspaper. Nobody is standing in line behind the customer.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
(A) A customer is returning an empty cup to the counter.
(B) A man is standing in line behind the customer.
(C) The man by the window is typing on a laptop.
(D) A barista is passing a drink to a customer at the counter.
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The barista is handing a paper cup across the counter — D paraphrases it as "passing a drink to a customer." / *VI: Nhân viên pha chế đang đưa cốc giấy qua quầy cho khách — D diễn đạt lại là "passing a drink to a customer".*
- **Traps:** (A) the customer is receiving a cup, not returning one; (B) nobody is standing in line — the only other customer is seated; (C) the man by the window is reading a newspaper, not typing.

### Scene Card 3 — Building Lobby
> In a building lobby, a security guard is watching a computer monitor at the front desk while a visitor signs a logbook on the counter. The visitor has already clipped a visitor badge to his jacket. Behind them, the revolving door is still — nobody is coming through.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
(A) A visitor is signing in at the security desk.
(B) People are entering the building through the revolving door.
(C) The visitor is pushing a cart of boxes across the lobby.
(D) The guard is repairing the computer monitor.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** Signing a logbook on the counter at the security desk is exactly "signing in at the security desk." / *VI: Việc ký sổ đăng ký trên quầy tại bàn bảo vệ chính là "signing in at the security desk".*
- **Traps:** (B) the revolving door is still — nobody is coming through; (C) no cart appears anywhere in the scene — the visitor is signing a logbook; (D) the guard is watching the monitor, not repairing it.

### Scene Card 4 — Sidewalk Delivery
> On a city sidewalk, a delivery rider is walking his bicycle along the curb with a large insulated food bag on his back. Ahead of him, a street vendor is arranging fruit on a cart under a striped umbrella. The rain has stopped and the pavement is drying, so no one is carrying an umbrella.

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
(A) The vendor is handing a bag of fruit to a customer.
(B) A cyclist is riding down the middle of the street.
(C) A delivery rider is walking his bicycle along the sidewalk.
(D) Pedestrians are opening their umbrellas in the rain.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The rider is walking his bicycle along the curb of the sidewalk — C states exactly that. / *VI: Người giao hàng đang dắt bộ xe đạp men theo lề vỉa hè — C mô tả đúng như vậy.*
- **Traps:** (A) no customer is in the scene — the vendor is arranging fruit, not selling any; (B) the rider is walking the bicycle on the sidewalk, not riding in the street; (D) the rain has stopped and the pavement is drying — nobody is carrying an umbrella.

### Set B — Core (Bands: B3)

### Scene Card 1 — Warehouse Aisle
> In a warehouse aisle, a worker in a hi-vis vest is scanning the barcode on a cardboard box with a handheld device. Sealed boxes are stacked neatly on the metal shelving on both sides of the aisle. At the end of the aisle, a forklift is parked with its engine off and its forks empty.

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
(A) Boxes are falling from the metal shelving.
(B) A forklift is lifting a pallet in the aisle.
(C) A worker is checking a box with an electronic scanner.
(D) A worker is sealing a box with packing tape.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The worker is scanning the barcode on a box with a handheld device — "checking a box with an electronic scanner" paraphrases it. / *VI: Người công nhân đang quét mã vạch trên thùng giấy bằng thiết bị cầm tay — "checking a box with an electronic scanner" là cách diễn đạt lại.*
- **Traps:** (A) the boxes are stacked neatly on the shelving — nothing is falling; (B) the forklift is parked with its engine off and its forks empty; (D) near-action swap — he is scanning the box, not sealing it with tape.

### Scene Card 2 — Machine Gauge
> On a factory floor, two engineers wearing hard hats are standing in front of a machine, pointing at a pressure gauge on its control panel. One of them is holding a clipboard. The machine is still running, but the assembly line behind it has been stopped for servicing.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
(A) Two engineers are examining a gauge on a control panel.
(B) An engineer is repairing the machine with a screwdriver.
(C) Products are moving along the assembly line.
(D) A supervisor is handing out hard hats to the engineers.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The engineers are standing at the machine, pointing at a pressure gauge on the control panel — A compresses this into "examining a gauge." / *VI: Hai kỹ sư đang đứng trước máy và chỉ vào đồng hồ áp lực trên bảng điều khiển — A tóm gọn lại thành "examining a gauge".*
- **Traps:** (B) they are pointing at a gauge and holding a clipboard — nobody is repairing anything; (C) the assembly line behind the machine has been stopped; (D) the engineers are already wearing their hard hats — nobody is handing any out.

### Scene Card 3 — Clinic Waiting Area
> In a clinic waiting area, a nurse is calling out a patient's name while holding a folder. In response, an elderly man is rising from his chair, and the woman beside him is putting a magazine back on the side table. All the examination-room doors along the corridor are closed.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
(A) A nurse is leading a patient down the corridor.
(B) A woman is picking a magazine up off the side table.
(C) Patients are queuing at the reception desk.
(D) An elderly patient is getting up from his chair.
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The elderly man is rising from his chair after the nurse's call — D restates it as "getting up from his chair." / *VI: Ông cụ đang đứng dậy khỏi ghế sau lời gọi của y tá — D diễn đạt lại là "getting up from his chair".*
- **Traps:** (A) the nurse is calling out from the waiting area — she is not walking anyone anywhere; (B) direction flip — the magazine is going back onto the table, not being picked up; (C) the patients are seated — nobody is queuing at the desk.

### Scene Card 4 — Platform Edge
> On a train platform, a railway employee is holding up a signaling paddle while a few passengers with bags stand behind the yellow line, facing the tracks. On a bench nearby, a teenager is sitting with headphones on and a backpack at his feet. The tracks are empty — no train has arrived yet.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
(A) Passengers are stepping off a train onto the platform.
(B) Passengers are waiting behind the yellow safety line.
(C) An employee is loading suitcases onto a luggage cart.
(D) A teenager is asking an employee for directions.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The passengers are standing behind the yellow line facing the empty tracks — that is exactly what waiting for a train looks like. / *VI: Hành khách đang đứng sau vạch vàng, hướng về phía ray còn trống — đó chính là đang chờ tàu.*
- **Traps:** (A) the tracks are empty — no train has arrived, so nobody is stepping off one; (C) no luggage cart is in the scene — the employee is holding a signaling paddle; (D) the teenager is sitting with headphones on — he is not talking to anyone.

### Set C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5)

### Scene Card 1 — Copy Room
> In a copy room, one technician is loading a fresh ream of paper into a photocopier while another technician is removing a jammed sheet from the feeder of the machine next to it. The first machine's display reads "Ready." Nobody is using the paper cutter on the far counter.

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
(A) A technician is clearing a paper jam from the machine he is loading with paper.
(B) The technicians are trimming sheets with the paper cutter.
(C) Both machines are displaying error messages.
(D) One technician is loading paper into one machine while another clears a jam in the other.
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The card splits two near-identical actions between two machines and two technicians; D keeps both pairings straight — the paper goes into one machine, and the jam comes out of the other. / *VI: Thẻ cảnh chia hai hành động gần như giống hệt nhau cho hai máy và hai kỹ thuật viên; D giữ đúng cả hai cặp — giấy được nạp vào một máy, còn kẹt giấy được gỡ ra từ máy kia.*
- **Traps:** (A) nearly right, but the jam is in the neighboring machine — the same technician is not doing both jobs; (B) the paper cutter sits on the far counter and nobody is touching it; (C) the first machine's display reads "Ready," so at most one machine could be in error.

### Scene Card 2 — Rooftop Terrace
> On the rooftop terrace of an office building, a gardener is trimming a hedge that runs along the railing. Behind her, two office workers are eating lunch at a small table, with the city skyline spread out beyond them. Their phones are lying face down on the table — no one is taking photographs.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
(A) The office workers are taking photographs of the skyline.
(B) A gardener is trimming a hedge on a rooftop terrace.
(C) The office workers are cutting the hedge during their lunch break.
(D) A gardener is watering the plants along the railing.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The gardener is trimming the hedge along the railing of the rooftop terrace — B matches the doer, the action, and the place. / *VI: Người làm vườn đang cắt tỉa bờ cây dọc lan can trên sân thượng — B khớp cả nhân vật, hành động lẫn địa điểm.*
- **Traps:** (A) the skyline is the misleading background — the phones are lying face down on the table; (C) swapped roles — the office workers are eating lunch, not cutting anything; (D) near-action swap — she is trimming, not watering.

### Scene Card 3 — Bakery Kitchen
> In a bakery kitchen, a baker in a white apron is sliding a tray of bread rolls into a large oven. At the counter beside her, an assistant is dusting a finished cake with powdered sugar. The trays of dough on the proofing racks by the wall have not been shaped yet.

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)
(A) A baker is putting a tray of bread into an oven.
(B) A baker is spreading frosting over a cake.
(C) An assistant is shaping dough at the proofing racks.
(D) A baker is removing bread rolls from the oven.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The baker is sliding a tray of bread rolls into the oven — A is the plain restatement. / *VI: Thợ bánh đang đẩy khay bánh mì vào lò — A là cách diễn đạt lại trực tiếp.*
- **Traps:** (B) double bait — the cake is the assistant's task, not the baker's, and it is being dusted with sugar, not frosted; (C) the dough has not been shaped yet, and the assistant is at the counter, not at the racks; (D) direction flip — the tray is going into the oven, not coming out.

### Scene Card 4 — Parking Garage
> In an underground parking garage, a driver is backing a van into a parking space while a colleague guides him with hand signals from behind the vehicle. A row of shopping carts stands parked against the wall. Although a "Level Full" sign hangs above the ramp, several spaces on this level are empty.

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)
(A) A driver is loading shopping carts into a van.
(B) Two colleagues are guiding the van from the front.
(C) An employee is directing a driver who is backing into a space.
(D) Every parking space on the level is occupied.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** A colleague is signaling from behind the van while the driver backs into a space — "an employee is directing a driver" restates it. / *VI: Một đồng nghiệp đang ra hiệu từ phía sau xe trong khi tài xế lùi vào chỗ đỗ — "an employee is directing a driver" diễn đạt lại điều đó.*
- **Traps:** (A) the shopping carts stand against the wall — nothing is being loaded into the van; (B) nearly right — there is only one colleague, and he stands behind the van, not in front of it; (D) the "Level Full" sign is bait — several spaces on the level are empty.
