# Business Vocabulary II — Travel, Manufacturing, Housing & Health
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Domains:** 4 · **Entries:** 64 · **Every entry:** collocations + example + note or trap

*VI: Từ vựng kinh doanh II — Du lịch giao thông, Sản xuất vận tải, Nhà cửa bất động sản và Sức khỏe y tế. Mỗi chủ đề 16 mục từ; mỗi mục kèm collocation, câu ví dụ và ghi chú phân biệt bẫy hoặc sắc thái.*

## How to use this file

Meet each word at work, not in a list: airport counters, loading docks, apartment viewings and clinic desks. For every entry, cover the example sentence and produce your own line using one of the collocations — then read the note, because that is where the B4–B5 points hide. Let the error log schedule your reviews: a headword from a logged miss jumps the queue, everything else waits its turn.

*VI: Hãy gặp từng từ trong bối cảnh công việc thay vì trong danh sách từ: quầy sân bay, bãi bốc hàng, buổi xem căn hộ và bàn tiếp đón phòng khám. Với mỗi mục, hãy che câu ví dụ và tự nói một câu của mình dùng một collocation — rồi đọc ghi chú, vì đó là nơi ẩn giấu điểm của băng B4–B5. Hãy để error log lên lịch ôn tập: mục từ nào từng nằm trong câu sai đã ghi sổ sẽ được ưu tiên, phần còn lại chờ lượt của mình.*

Per band: **B1–B2** learn the headword, the first two collocations and the example. **B3–B4** cover every collocation and every note. **B5** treat the notes as the real lesson — traps, register and near-twins are where the top points are won.

*VI: Theo từng băng: B1–B2 học từ chính, hai collocation đầu và câu ví dụ; B3–B4 đọc hết collocation và ghi chú; B5 coi phần ghi chú là bài học chính — bẫy, văn phong và các cặp từ gần giống nhau chính là nơi giành được điểm cao nhất.*

## Domain 1 — Travel & transport

Travel words dominate Part 4 announcements — gates, delays, shuttle schedules — and Part 7 itineraries. Train your ear for times, gate numbers and the sound-alike pairs that fill Part 2.

*VI: Từ vựng du lịch chiếm phần lớn các thông báo ở Part 4 — cổng bay, chậm chuyến, lịch xe đưa đón — và lịch trình ở Part 7. Hãy luyện tai với giờ giấc, số cổng và những cặp âm dễ nhầm hay xuất hiện ở Part 2.*

### itinerary (n) — lịch trình chuyến đi
**Collocations:** a packed / full itinerary · follow the itinerary · a detailed itinerary · a travel itinerary
**Example:** The travel agent e-mailed a detailed itinerary with all the flight numbers.
**Note:** Confusable pair: itinerary (lịch trình chuyến đi) vs agenda (chương trình cuộc họp). Tour pages say itinerary; meeting notices say agenda — the same trap as Vocabulary I, seen from the other side.

### reservation (n) — việc đặt chỗ
**Collocations:** make / cancel a reservation · a reservation under the name of · confirm a reservation
**Example:** I have a reservation under the name Tran for two nights.
**Note:** B5 nuance: in the plural, reservations also means "sự do dự" — "I have some reservations about the plan." In Part 2, "Any reservations?" could be about a booking or about doubts; the follow-up line decides.

### boarding pass (n) — thẻ lên máy bay, tàu
**Collocations:** print / show a boarding pass · a mobile boarding pass · at the boarding gate
**Example:** You can print your boarding pass at the self-service kiosk near check-in.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): board (v: lên máy bay, tàu) vs broad (adj: rộng). "Has boarding started?" expects a gate answer, not a size; *board* as a noun returns in bulletin board.

### departure (n) — chuyến khởi hành, giờ khởi hành
**Collocations:** departure time / gate · an early departure · the departure lounge · on departure
**Example:** Check the departure board again; the gate has changed twice today.
**Note:** Word family: depart (v), departure (n). Part 4 announcements contrast departure with arrival in one breath; catching which word was said is half the question.

### layover (n) — chặng dừng trung gian
**Collocations:** a long / short layover · a layover in Tokyo · an overnight layover · during the layover
**Example:** We had a six-hour layover in Doha, so we visited the airport gardens.
**Note:** B5 nuance: a layover is usually a short technical stop, while a stopover is a planned, longer break — often to visit the city. Travel talk in Part 3 uses both without warning.

### fare (n) — giá vé, cước
**Collocations:** a one-way / round-trip fare · a fare increase · bus / train fares · a reduced fare
**Example:** The railway announced a fare increase for express trains.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): fare (giá vé) vs fair (công bằng). "Is the fare fair?" is a real possibility. Also confusable: fare (tiền vé) vs fee (phí dịch vụ, phí làm hồ sơ).

### shuttle (n) — xe đưa đón theo tuyến cố định
**Collocations:** a free / airport shuttle · the shuttle runs every twenty minutes · a hotel shuttle
**Example:** The hotel shuttle runs every twenty minutes from Gate C.
**Note:** Schedule pattern: "The shuttle runs every twenty minutes" — the noun stays singular after *every*. Part 4 repeats this pattern for shuttles, trains and buses, with the number usually the answer.

### confirm (v) — xác nhận
**Collocations:** confirm a reservation / booking · confirm in writing · confirm the details · a confirmed seat
**Example:** Please confirm your attendance by replying to this message.
**Note:** Confusable pair (Part 5): confirm (xác nhận) vs conform (tuân thủ quy định). "Parts must conform to safety standards" vs "please confirm receipt of this e-mail" — one letter apart.

### cancel (v) — hủy
**Collocations:** cancel a reservation / order · cancel in advance · a canceled flight · a cancellation fee
**Example:** If you cancel less than a day ahead, a cancellation fee applies.
**Note:** Word-form trap: cancel (v) vs cancellation (n) — "a cancellation fee," "the cancellation policy." American spelling usually writes *canceled* for the verb but keeps the double "l" in the noun.

### delay (n, v) — sự chậm trễ; làm chậm, trì hoãn
**Collocations:** a two-hour delay · delay a shipment · without delay · apologize for the delay
**Example:** Dense fog delayed all morning departures by ninety minutes.
**Note:** Confusable pair: delay (v/n: chậm trễ) vs late (adj: muộn). A flight "is delayed" or "runs late"; a person simply "arrives late." Part 4 closes announcements with "We apologize for the delay" — a fixed phrase worth memorizing.

### baggage (n) — hành lý
**Collocations:** checked baggage · baggage claim · excess baggage · carry-on baggage
**Example:** Oversized baggage must be checked in at the ticket counter.
**Note:** Grammar trap: baggage and luggage are uncountable — "two pieces of baggage," never "two baggages." Part 4 sends arriving passengers "to the baggage claim area."

### customs (n) — hải quan
**Collocations:** go through customs · a customs officer / declaration · clear customs · customs regulations
**Example:** You must declare the camera equipment when you go through customs.
**Note:** Singular-plural trap: custom (phong tục) vs customs (hải quan). "Local customs" with an "s" can still mean traditions, so Part 7 leans on context words like *declaration*, *duty-free* and *officer*.

### terminal (n) — nhà ga (sân bay)
**Collocations:** Terminal 1 / 2 · a domestic / international terminal · the terminal building · a shuttle between terminals
**Example:** The international terminal is a ten-minute ride from the domestic one.
**Note:** B5 polysemy: terminal (nhà ga sân bay) vs terminal (thiết bị đầu cuối — "a payment terminal"). Airport conversations mean the building; retail notices may mean the machine.

### round trip (n) — chuyến khứ hồi
**Collocations:** a round-trip ticket / fare · book a round trip · a round trip to and from
**Example:** A round-trip ticket costs barely more than two one-way fares.
**Note:** Hyphen trap: two words as a noun ("book a round trip") but hyphenated before a noun ("a round-trip ticket"). Part 6 blanks occasionally test the hyphen itself.

### voucher (n) — phiếu dịch vụ
**Collocations:** a meal / gift / travel voucher · redeem a voucher · issue a voucher · a voucher for one night
**Example:** Passengers received meal vouchers while the aircraft was inspected.
**Note:** B5 nuance: a voucher is prepaid or given as compensation (phiếu dùng dịch vụ), while a coupon cuts the price of something still purchased. Airlines hand out vouchers; supermarkets hand out coupons.

### jet lag (n) — tình trạng lệch múi giờ
**Collocations:** suffer from jet lag · recover from jet lag · beat jet lag · a bad case of jet lag
**Example:** She flew in a day early to recover from jet lag before the conference.
**Note:** Grammar note: jet lag is uncountable — "a lot of jet lag," never "jet lags." Post-flight small talk in Part 3 turns on this word; natural responses mention sleep, coffee or arriving a day early.

## Domain 2 — Manufacturing & logistics

These words cover making and moving goods — from raw materials on an assembly line to freight leaving the warehouse. Expect them in factory tours (Part 4), shipment e-mails (Part 7) and word-form sets (Part 5).

*VI: Nhóm từ này bao quát việc sản xuất và vận chuyển hàng hóa — từ nguyên liệu thô trên dây chuyền lắp ráp đến lô hàng rời kho. Hãy chờ gặp chúng trong bài thăm quan nhà máy (Part 4), e-mail về lô hàng (Part 7) và bộ câu dạng từ (Part 5).*

### assembly line (n) — dây chuyền lắp ráp
**Collocations:** work on an assembly line · shut down the assembly line · assembly-line workers
**Example:** A conveyor jam shut down the assembly line for an hour.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: assemble (lắp ráp) vs resemble (trông giống nhau). "The two parts resemble each other" looks like assembly talk but is a comparison — Part 5 enjoys the costume.

### manufacture (v) — sản xuất
**Collocations:** manufacture goods / parts · manufactured in Vietnam · a manufacturing plant
**Example:** The firm manufactures water pumps for export markets.
**Note:** Word-form trap: manufacture (v: sản xuất) vs manufacturer (n: nhà sản xuất) vs manufacturing (ngành sản xuất). Part 5 asks which hat the word wears in the blank.

### plant (n) — nhà máy
**Collocations:** a manufacturing plant · the plant manager · open / close a plant · at the plant
**Example:** The plant manager walked the floor with the safety inspectors.
**Note:** Polysemy trap: plant (nhà máy) vs plant (cây cảnh) vs plant (v: trồng). In Part 1 a photo of an office plant can distract you while the line says "The plant is operating" — trust the verb.

### equipment (n) — thiết bị
**Collocations:** office / safety equipment · a piece of equipment · install / maintain equipment · equipment failure
**Example:** All climbers must wear the safety equipment provided on site.
**Note:** Grammar trap: equipment is uncountable — "a piece of equipment," never "equipments." Part 5 plants the wrong plural and hopes you take the bait.

### raw materials (n) — nguyên liệu thô
**Collocations:** the cost of raw materials · import raw materials · raw-material prices · process raw materials
**Example:** Rising raw-material costs forced the mill to raise its prices.
**Note:** Hyphen note: open as a noun ("buy raw materials") but hyphenated before another noun ("raw-material costs") — the same pattern as *round trip* in Domain 1.

### warehouse (n) — nhà kho
**Collocations:** warehouse space · store goods in a warehouse · a warehouse worker · a warehouse clearance sale
**Example:** The chairs are stored in a warehouse until the showroom opens.
**Note:** B5 nuance: a warehouse stores goods for the long term, while a distribution center moves them onward fast. Part 7 logistics letters use both, with different delivery promises.

### inventory (n) — hàng tồn kho
**Collocations:** take inventory · inventory levels · update the inventory · in inventory
**Example:** Staff count every shelf during the year-end inventory.
**Note:** Confusable pair: inventory (tồn kho — Anh-Mỹ trong kinh doanh) vs stock (Anh-Anh, đời thường). Both give "in stock / out of stock," the phrases Part 3 shop talk uses constantly.

### shipment (n) — lô hàng
**Collocations:** a shipment of parts · an early / delayed shipment · receive a shipment · track a shipment
**Example:** A delayed shipment of glass panels held up the whole project.
**Note:** Word family: ship (v: gửi hàng) vs ship (n: con tàu) vs shipment (lô hàng). "Has the shipment arrived?" is about boxes, not boats — you can ship by truck.

### supplier (n) — nhà cung cấp
**Collocations:** a reliable / local supplier · switch suppliers · a supplier of parts · supplier discounts
**Example:** We switched to a local supplier to shorten delivery times.
**Note:** Word-family nuance: supply (v: cung cấp) vs supplier (n: nhà cung cấp). Factories deal with suppliers; offices and hospitals deal with vendors (nhà cung cấp dịch vụ, hàng nhỏ lẻ).

### quality control (n) — khâu kiểm soát chất lượng
**Collocations:** pass / fail quality control · the quality-control team · strict quality control
**Example:** Every batch is tested before it passes quality control.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): quality (chất lượng) vs quantity (số lượng). "Do we need more?" asks quantity; "Is it good enough?" asks quality — factory tours contrast the two in one line.

### defect (n) — lỗi (của sản phẩm)
**Collocations:** a product defect · a manufacturing defect · detect / fix a defect · defective (adj) parts
**Example:** The recall covered every unit with the same brake defect.
**Note:** Word-form trap: defect (n: lỗi) vs defective (adj: bị lỗi) — "a defect in the sensor" but "a defective sensor." Part 5 swaps the pair across neighboring questions.

### output (n) — sản lượng
**Collocations:** monthly output · increase / reduce output · production output · output levels
**Example:** Automation raised monthly output without adding a shift.
**Note:** Confusable pair (B5): output (sản lượng — cái được tạo ra) vs outcome (kết quả — hệ quả của một hoạt động). A factory measures output; a project team measures outcomes.

### freight (n) — hàng vận tải; cước vận tải
**Collocations:** air / sea / rail freight · freight charges · send goods by freight · a freight company
**Example:** Sea freight is slower but far cheaper for heavy machinery.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: freight (hàng vận tải) vs fright (sự sợ hãi) — "freight charges" never takes "gh." Shipping announcements in Part 4 are where freight lives.

### packaging (n) — bao bì, việc đóng gói
**Collocations:** packaging materials · eco-friendly packaging · packaging design · packaging and labeling
**Example:** The tea is sold in resealable, eco-friendly packaging.
**Note:** Confusable family (B5): package (gói hàng, gói dịch vụ), packaging (bao bì), packing (việc đóng gói). Packaging design is how the box looks; packing is the act of filling it.

### inspect (v) — kiểm tra
**Collocations:** inspect the goods / facility · inspect for defects · a routine inspection · inspect the site
**Example:** Engineers inspect the bridge joints every winter.
**Note:** Sound-alike family (Part 2): inspect (kiểm tra) vs expect (mong đợi) vs respect (tôn trọng). "When do you expect the inspection?" hides two of the three in one question.

### load (v, n) — chất, bốc (hàng); một lượt hàng
**Collocations:** load the truck · load crates onto a forklift · a load of supplies · a full load
**Example:** Two workers loaded the crates onto the waiting truck.
**Note:** Word-family nuance: load (chất lên) vs unload (dỡ xuống) vs upload (tải lên máy tính). Part 5 plays "the loading dock" (nơi bốc dỡ hàng) against "downloading files."

2. Finished chairs are stored in the ______ until the showroom reopens.
3. Every batch must pass ______ control before it leaves the plant.
4. Engineers ______ the cargo doors before each departure.

## Domain 3 — Housing & real estate

Housing words fill apartment listings, lease agreements and repair notices — the most everyday documents in Part 7. Learn lease, tenant and landlord together, because every lease has two sides.

*VI: Từ ngữ nhà cửa tràn ngập tin cho thuê căn hộ, hợp đồng thuê và thông báo sửa chữa — những tài liệu đời thường nhất ở Part 7. Hãy học lease, tenant, landlord cùng nhau, vì mỗi hợp đồng thuê luôn có hai phía.*

### lease (n, v) — hợp đồng thuê; cho thuê
**Collocations:** sign / extend / break a lease · a one-year lease · lease an apartment · the lease expires
**Example:** They signed a two-year lease with an option to extend.
**Note:** Confusable pair: lease (hợp đồng thuê dài hạn nhà hoặc xe) vs rent (tiền thuê hàng tháng hoặc việc thuê ngắn hạn). You pay rent under a lease; signs say "for lease" on buildings, "for rent" on apartments.

### tenant (n) — người thuê nhà
**Collocations:** a long-term tenant · tenant complaints · prospective tenants · the tenant is responsible for
**Example:** The tenant agreed to water the plants on the balcony.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: tenant (người thuê nhà) vs attendant (nhân viên phục vụ) — the endings -ant and -ent blur in fast speech. Listen for whether the topic is an apartment or a cabin.

### landlord (n) — chủ nhà, bên cho thuê
**Collocations:** the landlord raised the rent · a reliable landlord · landlord and tenant · the landlord's insurance
**Example:** The landlord repainted the hallway between the two leases.
**Note:** Word family: landlord (chủ cho thuê) pairs with tenant (người thuê); landlady is the older female form. Complaint talk in Part 3 names the landlord before the repair.

### utilities (n) — tiền điện, nước, mạng
**Collocations:** utilities are included · pay the utilities · utilities and maintenance · basic utilities
**Example:** The rent is high, but all utilities are included in the monthly price.
**Note:** Number trap: singular utility (công ty tiện ích) rarely appears in listings; plural utilities means the electricity, water and Internet bills. "Utilities included" is the phrase to scan for in Part 7 ads.

### furnished (adj) — có sẵn nội thất
**Collocations:** a fully / partially furnished apartment · furnished rooms · come furnished with
**Example:** The flat comes fully furnished, right down to the kettle.
**Note:** Word-form trap: furnish (v: trang bị nội thất) vs furniture (n: đồ nội thất — không đếm được) vs furnished (adj). "A furnished apartment has furniture" — Part 5 tests all three forms.

### spacious (adj) — rộng rãi
**Collocations:** a spacious living room / office · bright and spacious · a spacious layout
**Example:** The corner unit is bright and spacious, with windows on two walls.
**Note:** B5 nuance: spacious is always positive (rộng rãi, thoáng đãng); *large* is neutral and *huge* can feel cold. Part 7 listings choose "spacious" because it sounds bigger than "big."

### suburb (n) — vùng ngoại ô
**Collocations:** a suburb of the city · live / move to the suburbs · the northern suburbs · suburban (adj) homes
**Example:** They moved to a quiet suburb with good schools.
**Note:** Grammar trap: the general idiom is plural — "live in the suburbs"; use the singular only with *of*: "a suburb of the capital." Part 5 tests "the suburbs of the capital."

### relocate (v) — dời đến nơi ở hoặc nơi làm mới
**Collocations:** relocate to another city / office · relocate for a job · a relocation package
**Example:** The firm paid moving costs for employees who relocated to the new headquarters.
**Note:** B5 word family: relocate (v) vs relocation (n). "A relocation package" (trợ cấp chuyển công tác) is standard in Part 7 job postings and Part 3 offer negotiations.

### renovate (v) — sửa sang, cải tạo
**Collocations:** a newly renovated kitchen · renovate an office building · under renovation · extensive renovations
**Example:** The hotel closed its top floor while the suites were renovated.
**Note:** Confusable pair: renovate (sửa sang cái cũ) vs innovate (đổi mới cách làm). Same rhythm, different worlds — a made-to-measure Part 5 near-twin.

### property (n) — bất động sản; tài sản
**Collocations:** commercial / residential property · property values · property management · a piece of property
**Example:** Property values in the district doubled after the metro opened.
**Note:** Polysemy trap (B5): property (bất động sản), property (tài sản — "damaged company property"), property (thuộc tính — hóa học). Part 7 letters reach for the second meaning.

### real estate (n) — ngành bất động sản
**Collocations:** a real estate agent · the real estate market · commercial real estate · real estate listings
**Example:** A real estate agent showed them three units on Saturday morning.
**Note:** Grammar note: real estate is uncountable and takes no article in general use — "She works in real estate." The person is a real estate agent (Anh-Mỹ) or an estate agent (Anh-Anh).

### down payment (n) — khoản trả trước
**Collocations:** make a down payment on · a large / small down payment · save for a down payment
**Example:** They saved for two years to make the down payment on the shop.
**Note:** Confusable pair: down payment (khoản trả trước khi mua — trừ vào giá) vs deposit (tiền cọc giữ chỗ). You *make* both, but only a down payment shrinks the price; "on a house" is the signal phrase.

### maintenance (n) — việc bảo trì, bảo dưỡng
**Collocations:** building maintenance · a maintenance fee / staff · regular maintenance · a maintenance request
**Example:** Submit a maintenance request online for the leaking faucet.
**Note:** Word family: maintain (v: bảo trì, duy trì) vs maintenance (n) — note the spelling keeps the "e." Part 5 loves the vowel shift between the two.

### neighborhood (n) — khu dân cư, khu phố
**Collocations:** a quiet / safe neighborhood · in the neighborhood · neighborhood businesses
**Example:** The corner café became a fixture of the neighborhood within a year.
**Note:** Word-family trap (Part 5): neighbor (người hàng xóm) vs neighborhood (khu phố) vs neighboring (lân cận — "neighboring towns"). Three forms, three meanings, one root.

### move in (phrasal v) — dọn vào ở
**Collocations:** move in on the first of the month · move into a new place · ready to move in · a move-in date
**Example:** The new tenants move in on the first of the month.
**Note:** Grammar trap: you move in (không tân ngữ) hoặc move *into* a place (có tân ngữ); the reverse is move *out of* a place. The hyphenated forms — a move-in date, a move-out inspection — work as nouns.

### appraisal (n) — việc định giá (bất động sản)
**Collocations:** a property appraisal · order an appraisal · the appraisal came in at · an independent appraisal
**Example:** The bank ordered its own appraisal before approving the loan.
**Note:** Confusable pair (B5): appraisal (định giá tài sản; kỳ đánh giá — "performance appraisal") vs appreciation (lòng biết ơn; sự tăng giá). A house gains appreciation but gets an appraisal.

**Use it now (gapped collocations — keys at the end of this file):**

2. The ______ agreed to replace the broken heater at no cost.
3. The rent is manageable because most ______ are included in the price.
4. The bank ordered an independent ______ before approving the loan.

## Domain 4 — Health & medical

Medical entries cluster around two TOEIC situations: making or changing appointments, and handling insurance paperwork. The vocabulary is practical, not clinical — clinics, coverage and sick leave rather than hospital drama.

*VI: Từ y tế gom về hai tình huống hay gặp trong TOEIC: đặt và đổi lịch hẹn, và xử lý giấy tờ bảo hiểm. Từ vựng thiên về thực tế chứ không lâm sàng — phòng khám, quyền lợi bảo hiểm và nghỉ ốm, thay vì kịch tính bệnh viện.*

### appointment (n) — cuộc hẹn (khám)
**Collocations:** make / cancel / reschedule an appointment · an appointment with Dr. Vu · a dental appointment
**Example:** Please reschedule my appointment with the dermatologist for next week.
**Note:** Collocation trap: you *make* an appointment, never "do" one — and later you keep or miss it. B5 nuance: appointments are with services (bác sĩ, tiệm); meetings are with colleagues.

### physician (n) — bác sĩ (từ trang trọng)
**Collocations:** a primary-care physician · consult a physician · a licensed physician · the attending physician
**Example:** Consult your physician before starting any exercise plan.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): physician (bác sĩ) vs physicist (nhà vật lý). Both wear lab coats in jokes; the scene — a clinic or a university open day — picks the right one.

### prescription (n) — đơn thuốc
**Collocations:** fill a prescription · write a prescription for · a repeat prescription · a prescription drug
**Example:** The pharmacist will fill the prescription while you wait.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: prescription (đơn thuốc) vs subscription (gói thuê bao tạp chí, dịch vụ). "I'd like to cancel my prescription" sounds like a magazine line; the answer mentions medicine, not renewals.

### pharmacy (n) — hiệu thuốc
**Collocations:** a twenty-four-hour pharmacy · the pharmacy counter · pick up medication at a pharmacy
**Example:** A twenty-four-hour pharmacy opened next to the clinic.
**Note:** Person-place trap: pharmacy (quầy hoặc hiệu thuốc) vs pharmacist (người cấp thuốc). American drugstores also sell snacks; Part 3 directions send people "to the pharmacy on the ground floor."

### deductible (n) — mức tự trả trước khi bảo hiểm chi trả
**Collocations:** meet the deductible · a high / low deductible · the annual deductible · the deductible and co-pay
**Example:** You pay the full fee until you meet your annual deductible.
**Note:** B5 insurance term: the deductible is what you pay before the plan starts; once you have "met" it, insurance pays its share. Confusable: deduction (khoản khấu trừ thuế) belongs to tax forms.

### symptoms (n) — triệu chứng
**Collocations:** mild / severe symptoms · flu-like symptoms · the symptoms disappeared · common symptoms include
**Example:** Anyone with flu-like symptoms should stay home until the fever passes.
**Note:** Pattern note: "common symptoms include..." introduces a list. B5 contrast: symptom (triệu chứng đơn lẻ) vs syndrome (hội chứng — một cụm triệu chứng có tên riêng).

### checkup (n) — buổi khám sức khỏe định kỳ
**Collocations:** an annual / routine checkup · schedule a checkup · a six-month dental checkup
**Example:** The company offers a free annual checkup at its partner clinic.
**Note:** Spelling trap: the noun is one word — a checkup; the verb phrase is two — check up on an order. Part 5 uses both in one section and tests the spacing.

### allergy (n) — dị ứng
**Collocations:** have an allergy to peanuts · a severe allergy · allergy season · an allergic (adj) reaction
**Example:** The intake form asks whether you have any food allergies.
**Note:** Word-form trap: allergy (n), allergic (adj — "be allergic to shellfish"), allergen (chất gây dị ứng). The test expects allergic *to* — never "allergic of/with."

### side effects (n) — tác dụng phụ
**Collocations:** mild / serious side effects · common side effects include · suffer from side effects
**Example:** Drowsiness is one of the common side effects of this medicine.
**Note:** Reinforcement of the classic pair: affect (v: ảnh hưởng đến) vs effect (n: tác động, kết quả). Side effects are noun territory; "the medicine affects your driving" is verb territory — Part 5 returns to this pair again and again.

### dentist (n) — nha sĩ
**Collocations:** a dental appointment · go to the dentist · the dentist's office · a six-month checkup
**Example:** Her dental appointment is at four, so she will leave the office early.
**Note:** Word-form trap: dentist (người) vs dental (adj: thuộc răng) vs dentistry (ngành nha khoa). "The dental clinic's dentist" is exactly the pairing Part 5 builds blanks from.

### emergency room (n) — phòng cấp cứu
**Collocations:** be taken to the emergency room · an emergency room nurse · a trip to the ER
**Example:** After the fall, he was taken to the emergency room for an X-ray.
**Note:** Confusable pair: emergency room (phòng cấp cứu ở bệnh viện) vs first-aid kit (bộ sơ cứu tại chỗ). Injury talk in Part 3 mentions the kit first and the ER only if things get worse.

### sick leave (n) — ngày nghỉ ốm
**Collocations:** take / be on sick leave · a sick-leave certificate · call in sick
**Example:** Employees who call in sick must submit a certificate when they return.
**Note:** Collocation gold: call in sick (gọi điện xin nghỉ ốm) is the Part 3 phrase. The noun sick leave is uncountable — "two days of sick leave"; "a sick day" is its countable American cousin.

### injury (n) — chấn thương
**Collocations:** a minor / serious injury · sustain an injury · a workplace injury · recover from an injury
**Example:** The safety officer recorded two minor workplace injuries this quarter.
**Note:** Word family: injure (v), injured (adj), injury (n). B5 collocation: accident reports say "sustained an injury" (formal) where everyday talk says "got hurt."

### treatment (n) — việc điều trị, liệu pháp
**Collocations:** undergo treatment · medical treatment · a course of treatment · treatment options
**Example:** The physiotherapy treatment lasts six weeks.
**Note:** Polysemy trap: treat (điều trị) vs treat (đãi ai — "let me treat you to lunch"). "My treat" at the end of a Part 3 conversation means someone is paying, not prescribing.

### surgery (n) — ca phẫu thuật
**Collocations:** undergo / perform surgery · minor / major surgery · recover from surgery · surgery is scheduled for
**Example:** The surgeon scheduled the procedure for Friday morning.
**Note:** Grammar note: surgery is normally uncountable in American medical English — "she needs surgery"; British English prefers "have an operation." The person is a surgeon.

### X-ray (n, v) — việc chụp X-quang; phim X-quang
**Collocations:** get / take an X-ray · an X-ray technician · an X-ray shows · X-ray the arm
**Example:** The X-ray showed a hairline fracture in the wrist.
**Note:** Capitalization note: the X stays capital in every form — X-ray, X-rayed, X-raying. Hospital talks in Part 4 and insurance forms in Part 7 both use "an X-ray was taken" as a fixed chunk.
