# Business Vocabulary III — Dining, Events, Retail & IT
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Domains:** 4 · **Entries:** 64 · **Every entry:** collocations + example + note or trap

*VI: Từ vựng kinh doanh III — Nhà hàng, Sự kiện, Bán lẻ và Công nghệ thông tin. Bốn chủ đề, mỗi chủ đề 16 mục từ; mỗi mục đều có: cụm từ thường đi kèm (collocation), một câu ví dụ và một ghi chú về bẫy hoặc sắc thái sử dụng.*

## How to use this file

Learn the collocation, not the word alone. Knowing *reservation* is not knowing **make a reservation under the name Tran** — and test items are built out of collocations, not out of single words. For each entry: read it, cover the example sentence, then say your own sentence aloud using one of the collocations. Then let the error log pick your reviews: any headword that appears in a miss you have logged (see `error-log-template.md`) goes to the top of your review pile; everything else can wait. Study one domain per day — 16 entries, the "Use it now" drill, then tomorrow's cold redo of the gaps you missed.

*VI: Học cả cụm từ, đừng học từ đơn lẻ. Biết từ reservation chưa bằng biết "make a reservation under the name Tran" — vì các câu thi được dựng từ collocation chứ không phải từ đơn. Với mỗi mục từ: hãy đọc, che câu ví dụ, rồi tự nói to một câu của riêng bạn dùng một collocation trong mục đó. Sau đó hãy để sổ ghi lỗi chọn giúp bạn thứ cần ôn: mục từ nào xuất hiện trong một câu sai đã ghi sổ (xem error-log-template.md) sẽ được đưa lên đầu danh sách ôn; phần còn lại có thể chờ. Mỗi ngày học một chủ đề — 16 mục, làm bài "Use it now", hôm sau tự làm lại những chỗ còn trống.*

Per band: **B1–B2** master the first two collocations of each entry plus the example sentence. **B3–B4** learn all the collocations plus the note. **B5** mine the notes — your misses live in nuance (register, paraphrase, sound-alikes), not in definitions.

*VI: Theo từng băng: B1–B2 nắm vững hai collocation đầu và câu ví dụ của mỗi mục; B3–B4 học đủ collocation kèm ghi chú; B5 khai thác sâu phần ghi chú — điểm mất của bạn nằm ở sắc thái (văn phong, cách diễn đạt lại, từ gần âm), không nằm ở nghĩa từ.*

## Domain 1 — Dining & hospitality

Restaurants, cafeterias and catered meals run through the whole Listening section — reservations go missing, dishes arrive wrong, parking needs validating. These sixteen entries cover the talk around the table.

*VI: Nhà hàng, căng tin và các bữa tiệc được phục vụ chạy suốt phần Nghe — chỗ đặt bị mất, món ăn sai, vé đỗ xe cần xác nhận. 16 mục dưới đây bao quát các tình huống quanh bàn ăn.*

### reservation (n) — đặt chỗ trước
**Collocations:** make a reservation · confirm a reservation · cancel the reservation · a table for six under the name...
**Example:** I'd like to confirm a reservation for four under the name Tran at half past seven.
**Note:** Collocation trap: you *make* or *have* a reservation — never "do a reservation". In Part 3 the twist is usually a lost booking or a wrong time, so catch the name and the hour, not just the word "reservation".

### catering (n) — dịch vụ phục vụ đồ ăn cho tiệc
**Collocations:** order catering for thirty guests · an outside catering company · on-site catering · the catering budget
**Example:** We ordered catering for thirty, but only eighteen people came, so we have trays left over.
**Note:** Word family worth logging on one card: cater (v) an event, catering (n) the service, caterer (n) the company. Part 4 announcements often say "refreshments will be provided by the caterer".

### complimentary (adj) — miễn phí (dùng để đãi khách)
**Collocations:** complimentary breakfast · complimentary wi-fi · a complimentary drink for guests
**Example:** The seminar fee includes a complimentary lunch and all workshop materials.
**Note:** Confusable pair: complimentary (miễn phí) vs complementary (bổ sung, phối hợp). A stock Part 5 item; in listening the two sound nearly identical, so context decides.

### banquet (n) — tiệc chiêu đãi đông người
**Collocations:** the annual banquet · a banquet hall · host a banquet · the banquet menu
**Example:** The awards banquet has been moved to the larger hall on the second floor.
**Note:** B5 nuance: a banquet is a large seated formal meal; a reception is standing, with drinks. Part 7 invitations mix both on purpose — the seating question tells you which one is meant.

### buffet (n) — tiệc tự chọn
**Collocations:** a buffet lunch · the breakfast buffet · a hot buffet · a buffet spread
**Example:** The hotel serves a hot buffet from six until ten every morning.
**Note:** Sound note: stress falls on the second syllable — bu-FET. First-syllable stress makes "buffer" (bộ đệm), and the two are close enough to fool a tired ear in Part 3 conference-lunch talk.

### server (n) — nhân viên phục vụ
**Collocations:** flag down a server · tell the server about an allergy · a friendly server
**Example:** Could you flag down a server and ask for more water?
**Note:** Sense-alike trap: a server is also the machine that hosts files or a website. In restaurant dialogues only the context separates "the server will be right with you" from "the server is down" — the second one belongs to the IT domain below.

### bill (n) — hóa đơn (ở nhà hàng)
**Collocations:** ask for the bill · split the bill · put the bill on the company account · settle the bill
**Example:** Let's split the bill three ways — I'll put the tip on my card.
**Note:** Confusable: in American restaurants the word is usually *check*; a *bill* in Part 7 is money owed for utilities (tiền điện, tiền nước). Hear where you are before you decide which bill it is.

### tip (n) — tiền bo
**Collocations:** leave a tip · a fifteen percent tip · tip the server · the tip is included
**Example:** Service was slow tonight, but we still left a small tip.
**Note:** B5 nuance: *gratuity* is the formal word on menus and banquet contracts — "an eighteen percent gratuity will be added for parties of eight or more" is a classic Part 7 detail line.

### vegetarian (adj, n) — (món/người) ăn chay
**Collocations:** a vegetarian option · vegetarian guests · a vegetarian set menu
**Example:** Half of our team chose the vegetarian option at the luncheon.
**Note:** Part 3 dietary-request trap: vegetarian (ăn chay) and vegan (ăn thuần chay) are different — vegan excludes dairy and eggs too. If the speaker says vegan, an option labeled vegetarian is the wrong answer.

### allergic (adj) — bị dị ứng
**Collocations:** allergic to shellfish · allergic to peanuts · a severe allergic reaction
**Example:** One of the delegates is allergic to shellfish, so the caterer is changing the appetizer.
**Note:** Collocation trap: allergic **to** something — never "allergic of/with". Log the family together: allergy (n) — a nut allergy — and "food allergies" in its plural form, which is what announcements use.

### takeout (n) — đồ ăn mang về
**Collocations:** order takeout · a takeout menu · a takeout container
**Example:** We ordered takeout from the new place downstairs and ate at our desks.
**Note:** Variant trap: American English *takeout* = British English *takeaway*. The test leans American, but reading passages may use either — learn the pair both directions.

### cafeteria (n) — căng tin
**Collocations:** the company cafeteria · a cafeteria discount · cafeteria hours
**Example:** The company cafeteria now stays open until eight for late shifts.
**Note:** Confusable: a cafeteria is a self-service canteen (tự phục vụ) in a workplace or school; a café serves coffee and light snacks. Part 4 announcements about "the cafeteria on the ground floor" rely on you keeping the two apart.

### cuisine (n) — ẩm thực, phong cách nấu ăn
**Collocations:** local cuisine · French cuisine · sample the region's cuisine
**Example:** The welcome dinner will showcase the region's cuisine.
**Note:** B5 nuance: cuisine is uncountable when it means a style of cooking; one prepared dish is "a dish", never "a cuisine". Word-form risk in Part 5: cuisine (n) vs culinary (adj).

### refill (v, n) — rót thêm; lần rót thêm
**Collocations:** free refills · refill a coffee · get a refill
**Example:** The café gives free refills on drip coffee before noon.
**Note:** Part 3 nuance: "Can I get you a refill?" is an offer, not a complaint — the expected answer is about the cup, not about service quality. Soft drinks "come with free refills"; a server or machine "refills" your cup.

### portion (n) — khẩu phần
**Collocations:** a generous portion · portion sizes · half portions
**Example:** The portions were so generous that nobody ordered dessert.
**Note:** Portion size turns up in surveys and feedback talks (Part 4): "many respondents found the portions too small." Pair it with *generous* — a ready-made paraphrase clue for "large".

### validate (v) — xác nhận (vé đỗ xe để được miễn/giảm phí)
**Collocations:** validate parking · validate a parking ticket · get the ticket validated
**Example:** Just have the host validate your parking ticket on your way out.
**Note:** B5 nuance: "validate parking" does not mean the parking was illegal — it means the restaurant stamps your ticket so you pay less or nothing (miễn/giảm phí gửi xe). A Part 3 favorite because a formal-looking verb sits inside a casual errand.

3. Could you ask the ______ for the bill?
4. Please get your parking ticket ______ at the front desk.

## Domain 2 — Events & conferences

Conferences, invitations and schedules fill Parts 3, 4 and 7 — venues change, speakers cancel, sessions run in parallel. Learn these sixteen and the event announcements stop blurring together.

*VI: Hội nghị, thư mời và lịch trình chiếm nhiều chỗ ở Part 3, 4 và 7 — địa điểm thay đổi, diễn giả hủy buổi, các phiên chạy song song. Nắm được 16 mục này, các thông báo về sự kiện sẽ không còn lẫn vào nhau.*

### venue (n) — địa điểm tổ chức sự kiện
**Collocations:** book a venue · the venue's capacity · change venues · an outdoor venue
**Example:** The venue can seat two hundred, but we expect closer to one hundred fifty.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: *venue* vs *vendor* (nhà cung cấp) — one vowel apart, and both words live in event talk. The course's own error-log example is built on this exact pair.

### register (v) — đăng ký
**Collocations:** register for the conference · register in advance · on-site registration · a registration desk
**Example:** Participants who register before May 1 pay the early rate.
**Note:** Word family for one log card: register (v) → registration (n) → registered (adj). Part 7 forms ask "Are you a registered guest?" — the word form is the whole item.

### attendee (n) — người tham dự
**Collocations:** expected attendees · a list of attendees · badge pickup for attendees
**Example:** Each attendee will receive a printed program at the registration desk.
**Note:** B5 nuance: the ending -ee marks the person receiving the action (compare train/trainee, employ/employee). The standard noun is attendee — do not invent "attender"; Part 5 word-form items count on it.

### keynote (n, adj) — bài phát biểu chính (mở đầu sự kiện)
**Collocations:** deliver the keynote · a keynote speaker · the keynote address
**Example:** This year's keynote will be delivered by the founder of the software company.
**Note:** Collocation trap: a speaker *delivers* or *gives* a keynote — never "says a keynote". In Part 4 schedules, "keynote" marks the big opening talk; the smaller parallel talks are breakout sessions.

### workshop (n) — hội thảo thực hành
**Collocations:** a hands-on workshop · lead a workshop · attend a workshop · a workshop on budgeting
**Example:** The hands-on workshop on spreadsheet skills filled up within a day.
**Note:** Confusable scale: a workshop is small and practical; a conference is large and multi-session; a seminar sits between. Part 7 program lists count on you keeping the sizes straight.

### schedule (n, v) — lịch trình; xếp vào lịch
**Collocations:** a tentative schedule · scheduled for June 3 · ahead of schedule · behind schedule
**Example:** The tentative schedule lists your talk for Saturday morning, but that may change.
**Note:** Part 3 favorite: "We're running behind schedule" indirectly means "we're late" — expect an apology or a rush next. The mirror phrase *ahead of schedule* (sớm hơn dự kiến) predicts finished-early news.

### booth (n) — gian hàng (trưng bày, giới thiệu)
**Collocations:** an exhibition booth · staff a booth · demo a product at the booth · booth twelve
**Example:** Our engineers will demo the new scanner at booth twelve all afternoon.
**Note:** B5 collocation: booths are *staffed* — "the booth is staffed by two engineers" is the passive of the verb staff. It baits Part 6 because "staff" is far more familiar as a noun.

### badge (n) — thẻ tên (đeo)
**Collocations:** a name badge · wear your badge · a badge holder · scan your badge
**Example:** Visitors must wear their badges at all times inside the building.
**Note:** Part 3/4 staple: "I forgot my badge" is the standard reason a colleague cannot enter — and the fix offered is usually a temporary pass, not a new badge. Listen for which one the staff member proposes.

### agenda (n) — chương trình (buổi họp, sự kiện)
**Collocations:** finalize the agenda · the next item on the agenda · attach the agenda · a packed agenda
**Example:** The agenda is attached; please add your items by Thursday.
**Note:** Confusable: the agenda is the list of topics; the itinerary is the travel schedule. Both can be "attached" in Part 7 e-mails — read what the attachment contains before answering.

### session (n) — buổi, phiên (trong chương trình)
**Collocations:** the opening session · a breakout session · a question-and-answer session · a training session
**Example:** The afternoon breakout sessions run in parallel, so choose one per time slot.
**Note:** B5 nuance: "in parallel" (chạy song song) is why Part 7 asks what an attendee will NOT be able to do — nobody can sit in two sessions at once. Paraphrase bait: "simultaneously" = "at the same time".

### capacity (n) — sức chứa
**Collocations:** venue capacity · seating capacity · reach capacity · at full capacity
**Example:** The workshop reached capacity within a week of opening registration.
**Note:** Memorize the paraphrase chain: reached capacity = no seats left = fully booked. In Part 7, "registration is closed" is the consequence — the question usually asks for the cause.

### panel (n) — phiên thảo luận chuyên gia
**Collocations:** a panel discussion · sit on a panel · moderate a panel · a panel of experts
**Example:** She was invited to sit on a panel about data security.
**Note:** B5 nuance: the moderator leads a panel; panelists sit on it. In Part 4 introductions, "our moderator tonight..." tells you who speaks next — a free answer to any who-question.

### postpone (v) — hoãn lại
**Collocations:** postpone the event until May · postponed due to bad weather · a postponed launch
**Example:** They postponed the product launch until the venue repairs are finished.
**Note:** Confusable trio: postpone = move to a later date (vẫn diễn ra); delay = start late or run late; cancel = not happening at all. Part 3 turns hinge on which verb the speaker actually says.

### exhibit (n, v) — gian trưng bày; trưng bày
**Collocations:** the exhibit hall · the exhibit floor · exhibit new products
**Example:** The exhibit hall opens an hour before the first session.
**Note:** Word-form trap: the room is the exhibit hall; the things shown are exhibits; the people showing them are exhibitors. Part 5 can put all three forms in one item — log them together.

### dress code (n) — quy định trang phục
**Collocations:** the dress code is business casual · adhere to the dress code · a strict dress code
**Example:** The invitation says the dress code is business casual, so no ties needed.
**Note:** B5 nuance: "business casual" is NOT "casual" — a Part 7 twist asks what to wear, and the tempting wrong option is simply "casual clothes". Know the ladder: formal > business formal > business casual > casual.

### RSVP (v) — xác nhận tham dự (trả lời thư mời)
**Collocations:** RSVP by June 5 · RSVP to the events team · regrets only
**Example:** Please RSVP by Friday so we can give the caterer a final headcount.
**Note:** B5 nuance: from French "répondez s'il vous plaît" — it just means "reply" (hãy trả lời). "RSVP — regrets only" means answer only if you are NOT coming. Part 7 invitations test the deadline more than the word.

**Use it now (gapped collocations — keys at the end of this file):**
1. The hall's seating ______ is two hundred, so register early.
2. Please ______ by Friday so the caterer gets a final headcount.
3. All ______ must pick up their badges at the registration desk.
4. Let's ______ the seminar until the keynote speaker has recovered.

## Domain 3 — Retail & shopping

Store announcements, return policies and shipment updates are staples of Part 4 talks and Part 7 notices — who pays, what is refundable, and when the next delivery lands.

*VI: Thông báo trong cửa hàng, chính sách đổi trả và tin cập nhật lô hàng là những "món quen" của Part 4 và Part 7 — ai trả tiền, món nào được hoàn tiền, và lô hàng kế tiếp về đến khi nào.*

### discount (n) — khoản giảm giá
**Collocations:** a seasonal discount · get a ten percent discount · a discount for members · at a discount
**Example:** Staff get a twenty percent discount on anything in the store.
**Note:** Collocation trap: "at a discount" takes no article. The Part 5 family is discount (n/v) → discounted (adj) → discounting; an item before a noun usually wants *discounted*.

### receipt (n) — biên lai
**Collocations:** keep the receipt · an itemized receipt · attach the receipt to the expense claim
**Example:** Without a receipt, the store can only offer store credit.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: receipt (biên lai) vs recipe (công thức nấu ăn) — near-identical spelling, and the "p" is silent only in receipt. If Part 3 is happening in a kitchen, you are hearing recipe; at a service counter, receipt.

### refund (n) — tiền hoàn lại
**Collocations:** issue a refund · request a full refund · a non-refundable deposit · the refund policy
**Example:** Refunds are issued to the original card within five business days.
**Note:** Trap on the label: non-refundable means the money does NOT come back (không hoàn lại). Part 7 ticket policies bury this in small print and then ask what happens to your fee.

### exchange (v) — đổi (hàng)
**Collocations:** exchange an item · exchange it for a larger size · the exchange policy
**Example:** You can exchange the jacket for another size within fourteen days.
**Note:** Collocation trap: you exchange something **for** something else — the preposition is the whole point. In-store, an exchange keeps your money in the store; a refund takes it out — Part 4 distinguishes them on purpose.

### return (n, v) — việc trả hàng; trả lại hàng
**Collocations:** the return policy · make a return · return an item within thirty days · a prepaid return label
**Example:** Online orders come with a return label already in the box.
**Note:** Redundancy trap: "return it back" is wrong — *return* already contains the idea of "back" (trả lại). Part 5 also likes "in return" as a fixed phrase meaning đổi lại.

### warranty (n) — bảo hành
**Collocations:** under warranty · a one-year warranty · an extended warranty · void the warranty
**Example:** The repair is free because the laptop is still under warranty.
**Note:** Confusable pair: a warranty is the maker's written, time-limited promise (được bảo hành theo thời hạn); a guarantee is any broader promise. The Part 3/7 signal phrase is "under warranty" = sửa miễn phí vì còn thời hạn.

### stock (n) — hàng (tồn kho)
**Collocations:** in stock · out of stock · back in stock · restock an item
**Example:** The color you want is out of stock until the next shipment.
**Note:** Learn the set as one card: in stock (còn hàng) / out of stock (hết hàng) / back in stock (đã về hàng lại) / restock (bổ sung hàng). Part 4 in-store announcements chain all four within one talk.

### aisle (n) — lối đi giữa các kệ hàng
**Collocations:** aisle five · the frozen-food aisle · halfway down the aisle
**Example:** You'll find the rice in aisle six, next to the sauces.
**Note:** Sound-alike: aisle and isle (hòn đảo) are perfect homophones — both /aɪl/, the "s" is silent. When an announcement gives an aisle number, an option about "a small island" is the echo trap.

### checkout (n) — quầy/khu thanh toán
**Collocations:** the checkout counter · an express checkout · self-checkout machines
**Example:** Ten items or fewer in the express checkout, please.
**Note:** Form trap: checkout (một từ, danh từ) vs check out (hai từ, động từ) — "you check out at the checkout". Same spelling split as login/log in; Part 5 and Part 6 both use it.

### purchase (v, n) — mua; việc mua hàng
**Collocations:** purchase tickets online · a purchase order · confirm a purchase
**Example:** All purchases over fifty dollars qualify for free delivery.
**Note:** Business nuance: a purchase order (PO) is the document a company sends to buy — not the act of buying. Part 7 invoices cite PO numbers, and the family purchase/purchaser/purchasing feeds Part 5 word-form items.

### coupon (n) — phiếu giảm giá
**Collocations:** redeem a coupon · a coupon code · a coupon expires · present a coupon
**Example:** The coupon expires at the end of the month, so use it soon.
**Note:** You *redeem* a coupon (đổi/áp dụng phiếu), not "use it up". B5 nuance: redeem also pairs with gift cards and points — one verb, three nouns, all Part 3/4 material.

### gift card (n) — thẻ quà tặng
**Collocations:** redeem a gift card · a gift card balance · load money onto a gift card
**Example:** She paid with a gift card and put the balance on her debit card.
**Note:** Confusable: a gift card holds a balance you spend down (tiêu dần); a coupon gives one discount and dies; a gift certificate is the paper version. Part 7 store policies test which one expires.

### shipment (n) — lô hàng
**Collocations:** a delayed shipment · the next shipment · receive a shipment · a partial shipment
**Example:** The delayed shipment of desks finally arrived this morning.
**Note:** Word family on one card: ship (v, gửi hàng) → shipment (n, lô hàng) → shipping (n, việc vận chuyển / phí vận chuyển). "Shipping is free" is not "the shipment is free" — a quiet Part 3 paraphrase gap.

### try on (phrasal v) — thử (quần áo, giày)
**Collocations:** try on a jacket · try it on · try these on
**Example:** Try the boots on before you buy — sizes run small here.
**Note:** Grammar trap: separable phrasal verb — "try it on", never "try on it". Bonus retail phrase for the log: "sizes run small" = mẫu này may hơi nhỏ so với nhãn ghi.

### sale (n) — đợt giảm giá
**Collocations:** the end-of-season sale · on sale · for sale · a sale price
**Example:** These floor lamps are on sale until Sunday.
**Note:** The classic preposition trap: **on sale** = đang giảm giá; **for sale** = đang rao bán (chưa chắc giảm). One preposition flips the meaning — both Part 3 and Part 7 bank on it.

### clearance (n) — việc xả kho thanh lý (giảm sâu)
**Collocations:** the clearance section · a clearance sale · clearance items
**Example:** Clearance items are final sale — no returns or exchanges.
**Note:** B5 nuance: "final sale" (bán chốt) is why the clearance rack can refuse refunds that the normal policy allows — a Part 7 exception question compressed into one line.

**Use it now (gapped collocations — keys at the end of this file):**
1. We can only issue a refund if you still have the ______.
2. That shelf is empty — the item is out of ______ until Friday.
3. These lamps are on ______ until the end of the month.
4. You can ______ the coupon at any register in the store.

## Domain 4 — IT & communications

E-mail verbs, login trouble and software updates drive everyday workplace talk in every listening part — and supply Part 5 with a steady stream of word-form items. Most of these sixteen are verbs you will use at work tomorrow.

*VI: Các động từ viết thư, sự cố đăng nhập và bản cập nhật phần mềm chiếm phần lớn hội thoại nơi làm việc ở mọi phần Nghe — đồng thời cung cấp cho Part 5 nguồn câu hỏi dạng từ dồi dào. Phần lớn 16 mục này là động từ bạn sẽ dùng ngay ngày mai ở công sở.*

### attach (v) — đính kèm
**Collocations:** attach a file · please find attached · a large attachment · the attachment didn't go through
**Example:** I've attached the revised schedule; let me know if it doesn't open.
**Note:** Word-form pair for Part 5: attach (v) → attachment (n). "Please find attached" is the formal e-mail formula; casual Part 3 speech prefers "I'll send it along" or "it's attached".

### forward (v) — chuyển tiếp (thư)
**Collocations:** forward an e-mail · forward it to the whole team · forward the confirmation
**Example:** Could you forward the confirmation e-mail to me? I never got mine.
**Note:** Confusable buttons: reply (trả lời người gửi) · reply all (trả lời tất cả) · forward (chuyển tiếp cho người khác). Part 3 office plots hide in which button was pressed — the wrong one is the problem.

### inbox (n) — hộp thư đến
**Collocations:** clear your inbox · check the inbox · the message is in your inbox
**Example:** Your invoice is sitting in my inbox — I'll process it this afternoon.
**Note:** B5 nuance: "sitting in my inbox" = đã nhận nhưng chưa xử lý — an indirect "yes, I have it; no, I haven't acted on it". Part 3 inference questions love this delay.

### log in (v) — đăng nhập
**Collocations:** log in to the portal · log in with a temporary password · unable to log in
**Example:** Employees can log in to the portal from any device.
**Note:** Form trap: log in (động từ, hai từ) vs login (danh từ/tính từ, một từ — "your login", "the login page"). Same rule as check out/checkout, and Part 5/6 items use exactly this split.

### password (n) — mật khẩu
**Collocations:** reset a password · a strong password · enter your password · a password expires
**Example:** Click the link to reset your password, then choose one you haven't used before.
**Note:** Part 3 script shape: "I can't log in" + "my password expired" → the offer is a reset link, not a new account. Passwords are *reset* (đặt lại), never "repaired".

### update (n, v) — bản cập nhật; cập nhật
**Collocations:** install the latest update · update the software · an automatic update · update someone on the project
**Example:** Install the update before Friday — after that, the old version stops connecting.
**Note:** Two traps in one word: update (bản cập nhật) vs upgrade (nâng cấp, thường tốn phí); and "update someone" = cập nhật tình hình cho ai, the verb of Part 3 status talk. Context picks the meaning.

### install (v) — cài đặt
**Collocations:** install the app · install an update · installation instructions
**Example:** The technician will install the new printers on Thursday afternoon.
**Note:** Word-form family for Part 5: install (v) → installation (n) → installer (n, chương trình cài đặt). Before choosing, count what surrounds the blank: a noun ahead usually wants an adjective or another noun.

### download (v, n) — tải về
**Collocations:** download the app · download a file · available for download
**Example:** The training video is available for download from the staff portal.
**Note:** Direction trap: download = về máy của bạn; upload = đưa từ máy lên máy chủ. If Part 3 says "I uploaded it to the shared drive", the option "she downloaded it" reverses the direction — an easy miss that feels correct.

### back up (phrasal v) — sao lưu
**Collocations:** back up your files · back everything up to the cloud · an automatic backup · a backup copy
**Example:** Back up your files tonight — the system upgrade wipes local storage.
**Note:** Grammar trap: separable phrasal (back **it** up), and the noun is one word — a backup. "We lost the backup" (danh từ) vs "we backed up the data" (động từ): Part 6 blanks test the spacing.

### glitch (n) — lỗi kỹ thuật nhỏ, tạm thời
**Collocations:** a technical glitch · iron out the glitches · a minor glitch · a scheduling glitch
**Example:** A technical glitch delayed the online registration for an hour.
**Note:** Register/tone: glitch is informal — a formal memo says "a technical issue". B5 nuance: "iron out the glitches" (khắc phục dần các trục trặc) tells you the fix is in progress, not finished.

### crash (v, n) — sập (máy, ứng dụng)
**Collocations:** the system crashed · the app keeps crashing · a server crash
**Example:** The scheduling app keeps crashing whenever two people book the same slot.
**Note:** Collocation sets the severity: "keeps crashing" (sập liên tục) means report it; "it crashed once" means restart it. In Part 3 the offered fix — restart or call the help desk — must match the severity you heard.

### help desk (n) — bộ phận hỗ trợ (kỹ thuật)
**Collocations:** contact the help desk · file a help desk ticket · the help desk is open until six
**Example:** File a help desk ticket and someone will remote into your machine.
**Note:** Near-synonyms to keep straight: help desk, service desk, IT support — Part 7 staff directories list them as separate lines. "Remote into your machine" = kết nối từ xa để xử lý sự cố.

### network (n) — mạng (máy tính)
**Collocations:** the network is down · a wireless network · a secure network · a network outage
**Example:** The office network is down, so please use your mobile data for now.
**Note:** Part 3 staple: "the network is down" (mạng sập) → expect a workaround: a hotspot, another floor, paper forms. The word "down" is soft and short — easy to miss in fast speech.

### device (n) — thiết bị
**Collocations:** a mobile device · register your device · a supported device · device settings
**Example:** Register each device you plan to use on the conference wi-fi.
**Note:** Sound-alike word-form pair: device (n, thiết bị) vs devise (v, nghĩ ra phương án) — the difference is one final sound. A made-to-order Part 5 couple.

### compatible (adj) — tương thích
**Collocations:** compatible with older models · backward compatible · be compatible with the system
**Example:** The new software isn't compatible with older tablets, so we're replacing those first.
**Note:** Collocation trap: compatible **with**, never "compatible to". B5 paraphrase bait: "works with" = "is compatible with" — spec sheets use one, the question the other.

### notification (n) — thông báo (tự động trên thiết bị)
**Collocations:** a push notification · turn off notifications · receive a notification that... · a delivery notification
**Example:** I got a push notification saying my package was delivered, but it isn't here.
**Note:** B5 nuance: a notification is machine-sent (thông báo tự động); an announcement is made by a person (thông báo do người đọc). The missing-package twist above is a stock Part 3 problem: the notification and reality disagree.
