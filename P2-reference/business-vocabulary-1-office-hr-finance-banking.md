# Business Vocabulary I — Office, HR, Finance & Banking
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Domains:** 4 · **Entries:** 64 · **Every entry:** collocations + example + note or trap

*VI: Từ vựng kinh doanh I — Văn phòng, Nhân sự, Tài chính kế toán và Ngân hàng – Bảo hiểm. Mỗi chủ đề 16 mục từ; mỗi mục kèm collocation, câu ví dụ và ghi chú phân biệt bẫy hoặc sắc thái.*

## How to use this file

Learn the collocation, not the lonely word. Knowing *deadline* is not knowing **meet a tight deadline** — test items are built from chunks like these, not from single words. For every entry: read it, hide the example, then produce your own sentence using one of the collocations. After the "Use it now" drill, anything you missed goes to the top of your review pile, and the error log decides what comes back and when.

*VI: Hãy học cả cụm từ, đừng học từ đơn lẻ. Biết từ deadline chưa bằng biết "meet a tight deadline" — vì các câu thi được dựng từ những cụm như vậy, không phải từ đơn. Với mỗi mục: đọc, che câu ví dụ, rồi tự tạo một câu của riêng mình dùng một collocation trong mục đó. Sau bài "Use it now", câu nào làm sai sẽ được đưa lên đầu danh sách ôn, và error log sẽ quyết định nội dung nào cần quay lại và khi nào.*

Per band: **B1–B2** own the headword plus the first two collocations and the example sentence. **B3–B4** add the remaining collocations and read every note. **B5** push hardest on the notes — your lost points sit in nuance, register and sound-alike pairs, not in core meanings.

*VI: Theo từng băng: B1–B2 nắm từ chính, hai collocation đầu và câu ví dụ; B3–B4 học thêm phần collocation còn lại và đọc hết ghi chú; B5 luyện sâu phần ghi chú — điểm mất của bạn nằm ở sắc thái, văn phong và các cặp từ gần âm, không nằm ở nghĩa cốt lõi.*

## Domain 1 — Office & workplace

The daily flow of office life: meetings, memos, supplies, and the people around you. These words run through Part 3–4 conversations and Part 7 notices and e-mails.

*VI: Nhịp sinh hoạt văn phòng hằng ngày: cuộc họp, bản ghi nhớ, văn phòng phẩm và những người xung quanh. Các từ này xuyên suốt hội thoại Part 3–4 và thông báo, e-mail ở Part 7.*

### agenda (n) — chương trình họp
**Collocations:** set / draw up an agenda · an item on the agenda · the first item on the agenda · circulate the agenda
**Example:** The chairperson circulated the agenda two days before the quarterly review.
**Note:** Confusable pair: agenda (danh sách nội dung cuộc họp) vs itinerary (lịch trình chuyến đi). A meeting has an agenda; a trip has an itinerary — Part 5 and Part 7 both play this pair.

### deadline (n) — hạn cuối, thời hạn chót
**Collocations:** meet / miss a deadline · a tight deadline · extend the deadline · ahead of the deadline
**Example:** We asked the client to extend the deadline because the artwork needed one more revision.
**Note:** Collocation trap: you *meet* or *miss* a deadline, never "win" or "reach" one. In Part 2, "What's the deadline for the report?" is answered with a date, not a reason.

### memo (n) — bản ghi nhớ nội bộ
**Collocations:** circulate a memo · a memo to all staff · according to the memo · a company memo
**Example:** A memo to all staff explained the new parking arrangements.
**Note:** Confusable pair: memo (thông báo ngắn nội bộ) vs minutes (biên bản họp). Minutes record what happened and take a plural verb — "the minutes are attached" — while a memo tells people what to do.

### supplies (n) — văn phòng phẩm, vật tư
**Collocations:** office supplies · order supplies · run out of supplies · a supply closet
**Example:** Please notify facilities before we run out of supplies like toner and notepads.
**Note:** Word-form trap: supply (v: cung cấp) vs supplies (n: văn phòng phẩm, vật tư phải đặt mua). Part 7 order forms say supplies; "the water supply" is the uncountable noun.

### workstation (n) — chỗ làm việc (bàn và thiết bị)
**Collocations:** an ergonomic workstation · set up a workstation · a temporary workstation · workstation assignments
**Example:** New hires receive a workstation with two monitors on their first morning.
**Note:** B5 nuance: a workstation is more than a desk — it is the whole desk-plus-equipment unit, which is why Part 7 office-move notices can talk about "workstation assignments" (bố trí chỗ ngồi cho từng người).

### reschedule (v) — dời lịch, xếp lại lịch
**Collocations:** reschedule a meeting / an appointment · reschedule for Friday · reschedule to a later date
**Example:** Since the client will be traveling, we rescheduled the presentation for Thursday.
**Note:** Confusable pair: reschedule (hẹn lại với thời gian mới ngay) vs postpone (hoãn, chưa hẹn lại). In Part 2, "Can we reschedule?" wants an answer containing a new time, not a bare "Yes."

### attend (v) — tham dự, dự
**Collocations:** attend a meeting / conference / training · attend in person · be attended by
**Example:** All department heads are expected to attend the briefing in person.
**Note:** Preposition trap: you attend a meeting (no preposition), but you attend to a problem (xử lý, để tâm đến). Part 5 uses "attend to" for tasks and bare "attend" for events.

### distribute (v) — phân phát, phân phối
**Collocations:** distribute materials to participants · distribute a survey · distribute evenly · widely distributed
**Example:** The trainer distributed the manuals to each participant before the session began.
**Note:** Preposition trap: distribute something *to* someone, never "for" someone. Part 5 also rotates the family — distribute (v), distribution (n), distributor (nhà phân phối).

### vacant (adj) — còn trống, bỏ trống
**Collocations:** a vacant seat / office · the position is vacant · a vacant lot · remain vacant
**Example:** The corner office has been vacant since the deputy director transferred to Hanoi.
**Note:** Word-form trap: vacant (adj) vs vacancy (n: chỗ trống, vị trí tuyển dụng). Job ads say "a vacancy in the sales team"; vacant also implies officially available, while "empty" just means not occupied.

### overtime (n) — giờ làm thêm
**Collocations:** work overtime · overtime pay / hours · be paid overtime · approve overtime
**Example:** Staff who work overtime on the holiday receive double their normal rate.
**Note:** Grammar trap: overtime is uncountable — "a lot of overtime," never "an overtime." Payroll talk in Part 3 chunks it as "overtime hours" and "overtime pay."

### supervisor (n) — người giám sát, quản lý trực tiếp
**Collocations:** report to a supervisor · a direct supervisor · a shift supervisor · a supervisor's approval
**Example:** Ask your supervisor to sign the form before you send it to HR.
**Note:** Word family: supervise (v), supervision (n), supervisor (người giám sát). Part 5 chains them — "under the supervision of her supervisor" — so learn the set as one card.

### colleague (n) — đồng nghiệp
**Collocations:** a close colleague · a colleague from the accounting team · colleagues and clients · greet a colleague
**Example:** A colleague from the Seoul office will cover for me while I am on leave.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): colleague (đồng nghiệp) vs college (trường đại học). One weak vowel apart — "My colleague teaches at a college" is the kind of line built to blur them.

### file (v) — nộp, lưu (hồ sơ)
**Collocations:** file a report / complaint / claim · file paperwork with HR · file for bankruptcy
**Example:** You must file the reimbursement form by the last working day of the month.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): fill (điền) vs file (nộp, lưu). You fill out a form and then file it with HR — "I filled it out but haven't filed it yet" uses both verbs in one breath.

### bulletin (n) — bản tin, thông báo ngắn
**Collocations:** a bulletin board · post a bulletin · the weekly bulletin · a company bulletin
**Example:** The fire-drill notice was posted on the bulletin board in the break room.
**Note:** Collocation trap: bulletin rarely walks alone — bulletin board (bảng thông báo), company bulletin (bản tin nội bộ). Part 7 hides notices "on the bulletin board near the lobby."

### teleconference (n) — cuộc họp trực tuyến, qua đường truyền
**Collocations:** join a teleconference · set up a teleconference · a teleconference with the branch office
**Example:** The design team in Osaka joined the teleconference instead of flying in for it.
**Note:** B5 nuance: strictly a teleconference is voice-only, but in Parts 3–4 people just say "conference call." Part 5 prefers "participate in a teleconference" over "make a teleconference."

### brief (v) — báo cáo tóm tắt, cập nhật cho ai
**Collocations:** brief the team / a client · brief someone on the changes · a briefing session · a press briefing
**Example:** The manager will brief the interns on the new data-privacy rules tomorrow.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): brief (adj: ngắn gọn) vs brief (v: trình bày tóm tắt) vs briefly (adv). "Who will brief us?" expects a person; "Will it be brief?" expects a yes/no plus a reason.

2. Can we ______ the budget meeting to Thursday? The client is out of town that day.
3. Employees who work ______ during the product launch will be paid extra.
4. The fire-drill notice is posted on the ______ board near the elevators.

## Domain 2 — Personnel & HR

HR words track a career from application to retirement. Expect them in job postings (Part 7), hiring and promotion talks (Parts 3–4), and word-form questions (Part 5).

*VI: Từ vựng nhân sự đi theo một sự nghiệp từ lúc nộp đơn đến khi nghỉ hưu. Hãy chờ gặp chúng trong tin tuyển dụng (Part 7), hội thoại tuyển dụng – thăng chức (Part 3–4) và câu hỏi dạng từ (Part 5).*

### résumé (n) — sơ yếu lý lịch
**Collocations:** update / submit a résumé · a résumé and cover letter · tailor a résumé to the job
**Example:** She tailored her résumé to the warehouse supervisor opening before applying online.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: résumé (n: lý lịch — trọng âm ở đầu) vs resume (v: tiếp tục — trọng âm ở cuối). Part 5 can set "resume the meeting" right beside "attach your résumé."

### applicant (n) — ứng viên, người ứng tuyển
**Collocations:** a qualified applicant · interview applicants · screen applicants · an applicant pool
**Example:** Only three of the forty applicants were invited back for a second interview.
**Note:** Word family: apply (v), application (đơn ứng tuyển), applicant (người ứng tuyển). Part 5 favors the person shift — "All applicants must complete an application form."

### interview (n, v) — cuộc phỏng vấn; phỏng vấn
**Collocations:** conduct / schedule an interview · an interview for the position of · a follow-up interview
**Example:** The panel conducted the interview by video because one member was overseas.
**Note:** Collocation trap: you *conduct* or *hold* an interview, never "make an interview." In Part 2, "When is your interview?" is answered with a time, and the follow-up usually names the position.

### hire (v) — tuyển dụng
**Collocations:** hire staff / an assistant · be hired as · newly hired employees · hire a contractor
**Example:** The clinic hired two part-time receptionists for the evening shift.
**Note:** Confusable pair: hire (thuê người) vs rent (thuê xe, thiết bị, phòng). Americans rent a car, never "hire" one; Part 5 pairs "hire personnel" with "rent equipment."

### recruit (v) — tuyển mộ, tuyển dụng
**Collocations:** recruit graduates / engineers · recruit for a position · a recruiting event · a recruitment agency
**Example:** The firm recruits engineering graduates from the regional university every autumn.
**Note:** B5 nuance: recruit is the active hunt for people (tìm kiếm, thu hút ứng viên); hire is the final act of employing them. Firms "recruit at job fairs" but "hire the best candidate."

### position (n) — vị trí, chức danh
**Collocations:** apply for the position of · a full-time / entry-level position · fill a position · a position becomes available
**Example:** The position of night auditor has been open since March.
**Note:** Polysemy trap: position (chức danh), position (vị trí — "a corner position on the shelf"), position (quan điểm — "a neutral position"). Let the sentence choose the meaning.

### qualification (n) — bằng cấp, năng lực chuyên môn
**Collocations:** meet the qualifications · relevant qualifications · academic / professional qualifications
**Example:** Candidates who meet the minimum qualifications will be contacted within a week.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: qualification (bằng cấp, năng lực) vs quality (chất lượng). "Does she have the qualifications for the post?" asks about credentials and experience, not about a product.

### promotion (n) — đợt thăng chức; đợt khuyến mãi
**Collocations:** get / deserve a promotion · a promotion to senior buyer · a special promotion
**Example:** His promotion to regional manager came with a larger travel allowance.
**Note:** Dual-meaning trap: promotion (thăng chức) vs promotion (khuyến mãi). "A two-week promotion" at a store is a sale; *coupon* and *discount* signal the store meaning, *salary* and *title* the career one.

### raise (n) — khoản tăng lương
**Collocations:** get / ask for a raise · a raise in salary · a modest raise · a raise and a bonus
**Example:** She received a raise after leading the software migration successfully.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: raise (n: khoản tăng lương — Anh-Mỹ) vs rise (v: tăng lên). British English pays "a pay rise." In Part 2, "Did you get a raise?" is about salary, not about standing up.

### benefits (n) — phúc lợi
**Collocations:** employee benefits · a benefits package · health / retirement benefits · fringe benefits
**Example:** The benefits package includes dental coverage and a transit pass.
**Note:** Collocation trap: benefits travel with *package*, *plan* and *coverage*, and Part 3 job-offer talk pairs "salary and benefits" as a set. Singular "benefit" is usually the verb or the abstract idea.

### transfer (v, n) — chuyển công tác; việc chuyển
**Collocations:** transfer to another branch / department · request a transfer · a transfer to headquarters
**Example:** He requested a transfer to the Da Nang office to be closer to his family.
**Note:** Confusable trio (Part 5): transfer (chuyển người hoặc tiền), transform (biến đổi hoàn toàn), transport (vận chuyển hàng hóa). An employee is transferred; freight is transported; a process is transformed.

### resign (v) — từ chức
**Collocations:** resign from a position · resign to start a business · resign effective immediately
**Example:** The purchasing director resigned to take a role at one of our suppliers.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap: resign (từ chức) vs re-sign (ký lại hợp đồng) — the hyphen flips the meaning. Part 5 can set "re-signed the lease" against "resigned from the board."

### lay off (phrasal v) — cho nghỉ việc vì cắt giảm
**Collocations:** lay off workers · be laid off · layoffs in the industry · lay off seasonal staff
**Example:** The plant laid off sixty assembly workers when orders dropped.
**Note:** B5 nuance: be laid off (mất việc vì công ty cắt giảm) vs be fired (bị đuổi vì lỗi cá nhân) vs resign (tự rời đi). Someone laid off talks about the company's situation, not a personal mistake.

### retire (v) — nghỉ hưu
**Collocations:** retire from the company · retire at sixty · retire early · a retirement party
**Example:** After thirty years at the bank, the head teller retired last Friday.
**Note:** Confusable pair: retire (nghỉ hưu, theo độ tuổi hoặc thâm niên) vs resign (từ chức, ở bất kỳ độ tuổi nào). A Part 7 invitation to a retirement party signals a career ending, not a job change.

### performance review (n) — kỳ đánh giá hiệu suất
**Collocations:** an annual performance review · conduct a performance review · a positive performance review
**Example:** Managers will conduct performance reviews during the first week of December.
**Note:** Confusable trio (Part 5): review (xem lại, đánh giá), preview (xem trước), overview (tóm lược tổng thể). "An overview of the review process" is a normal Part 7 heading using two of them at once.

### orientation (n) — buổi định hướng cho nhân viên mới
**Collocations:** a new-employee orientation · attend an orientation session · orientation materials · orientation day
**Example:** All new hires attend an orientation session on their first Monday.
**Note:** Word family: orient (v: hướng dẫn làm quen), orientation (n). B5 nuance: at work it is the welcome session for newcomers; for products, "orientation" means which way something faces.

**Use it now (gapped collocations — keys at the end of this file):**
1. She updated her ______ before applying for the designer position.
2. After five years with the firm, he finally received a ______ to senior manager.
3. The airline will ______ two hundred cabin crew before the summer season.
4. Dental coverage and paid vacation are part of the company's ______ package.

## Domain 3 — Finance & accounting

The paperwork side of business: invoices, budgets, audits, and the people who keep the numbers straight. Learn invoice, receipt and quotation as one family so Part 7 forms stop blurring together.

*VI: Phía "giấy tờ" của công việc: hóa đơn, ngân sách, kiểm toán và những người giữ sổ sách. Hãy học invoice, receipt, quotation như một gia đình từ để các mẫu đơn ở Part 7 hết bị nhầm lẫn.*

### budget (n) — ngân sách
**Collocations:** a tight budget · within / over budget · the annual budget · cut the budget
**Example:** The renovation stayed within budget because the contractor reused the old cabinets.
**Note:** B5 nuance: under budget (rẻ hơn kế hoạch), over budget (vượt ngân sách), within budget (trong mức cho phép). Part 5 tests the preposition — "the project came in under budget."

### invoice (n) — hóa đơn (yêu cầu thanh toán)
**Collocations:** issue / send an invoice · settle an invoice · an unpaid invoice · the invoice number
**Example:** Please quote the invoice number when you wire the payment.
**Note:** Confusable family: invoice (hóa đơn người bán gửi đi) vs receipt (biên lai chứng nhận đã trả) vs bill (thông báo phải trả, thường cho hộ gia đình). Part 7 rotates all three inside one question set.

### receipt (n) — biên lai, hóa đơn thanh toán
**Collocations:** keep the receipt · a printed / electronic receipt · receipts for expenses · attach receipts
**Example:** Attach the taxi receipts to your expense report or the claim will be denied.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): receipt (biên lai) vs receive (nhận). "Did you receive the receipt?" fires both words; the useful answer mentions proof of payment, not an envelope.

### expense (n) — chi phí
**Collocations:** business expenses · travel expenses · an expense report · at the company's expense
**Example:** Train tickets are a reimbursable expense when the trip is under four hours.
**Note:** Word-form trap: expense (n: chi phí) vs expensive (adj: đắt tiền) — there is no plural "expensives." Part 5 offers "an expense report" and "an expensive report" as near twins.

### revenue (n) — doanh thu
**Collocations:** annual revenue · generate / increase revenue · revenue streams · a revenue forecast
**Example:** Online orders now generate more revenue than the physical store.
**Note:** Confusable pair (B4–B5): revenue (doanh thu — toàn bộ tiền thu vào) vs profit (lợi nhuận — phần còn lại sau khi trừ chi phí). A firm can post record revenue and still earn no profit; reports use both on purpose.

### profit (n) — lợi nhuận
**Collocations:** make a profit · net / gross profit · a profit margin · a profitable product line
**Example:** The bakery made a small profit in its first winter despite the renovation costs.
**Note:** B5 nuance: gross profit (lãi gộp, sau trừ giá vốn) vs net profit (lãi ròng, sau mọi chi phí). The verb phrase is "make a profit," never "do a profit."

### audit (n, v) — cuộc kiểm toán; kiểm toán
**Collocations:** conduct / undergo an audit · an internal audit · audit the accounts · an audit trail
**Example:** The committee will conduct an audit of last year's travel spending.
**Note:** Collocation trap: an auditor *conducts* an audit, but a company *undergoes* it. Part 5 rotates audit (n/v), auditor (người) and auditing (hoạt động) within one section.

### accountant (n) — kế toán (người)
**Collocations:** a certified accountant · hire an accountant · the accountant prepares the statements
**Example:** The accountant flagged two duplicate payments before the report went out.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): accountant (kế toán viên) vs accounting (phòng hoặc khoa kế toán). "She works in accounting" names the department; "She is an accountant" names the profession.

### reimbursement (n) — khoản hoàn trả chi phí
**Collocations:** claim reimbursement · a reimbursement form / policy · submit receipts for reimbursement
**Example:** Reimbursement for the conference fee takes about ten business days.
**Note:** B5 word family: reimburse (v: hoàn tiền), reimbursement (n). Expense forms chunk it — "submit a reimbursement claim," "per the reimbursement policy."

### expenditure (n) — khoản chi (ngân sách)
**Collocations:** capital expenditure · cut expenditure · planned expenditures · monthly expenditures
**Example:** Capital expenditure on new ovens was postponed until the spring quarter.
**Note:** B5 nuance: expenditure is the formal budget word (khoản chi trong kế hoạch tài chính), while expenses are day-to-day costs you claim back. Board reports prefer expenditure; claim forms prefer expenses.

### estimate (n, v) — bản dự toán; ước tính
**Collocations:** a rough estimate · give / provide an estimate · an estimate for the repairs
**Example:** The contractor provided a written estimate for the roof repairs.
**Note:** Pronunciation trap: the noun ends in a weak "-mut" and the verb in "-mate" — same spelling, different final sound. Part 5 assumes you know both exist.

### quotation (n) — báo giá
**Collocations:** submit a quotation · a quotation for the project · a written quotation · the lowest quotation
**Example:** Three vendors submitted quotations for the lobby redesign.
**Note:** B4 nuance: in business, quotation = báo giá; in a writing class it = trích dẫn. Speech (Parts 3–4) shortens it to *quote*; formal documents (Parts 6–7) keep the full form.

### balance (n) — số dư
**Collocations:** an outstanding balance · the account balance · pay off the balance · a minimum balance
**Example:** There is still an outstanding balance on the equipment lease.
**Note:** B5 trap: outstanding usually means "xuất sắc," but an outstanding balance is money still owed (chưa thanh toán). In banking letters, read *outstanding* as *unpaid*.

### fiscal year (n) — năm tài chính
**Collocations:** the current fiscal year · fiscal year end · within this fiscal year
**Example:** The budget must be approved before the fiscal year ends in June.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): fiscal (thuộc tài chính) vs physical (thuộc thể chất). "When does the fiscal year end?" asks about accounting dates, not gym schedules.

### ledger (n) — sổ cái
**Collocations:** the general ledger · keep the ledger up to date · enter figures in the ledger
**Example:** Every payment is entered in the general ledger the day it clears.
**Note:** B5 accounting word: the ledger is the master book of accounts; the everyday idiom for the same job is "keep the books." Expect it inside Part 7 audit notices.

### overdue (adj) — quá hạn
**Collocations:** an overdue payment / invoice · fall overdue · an overdue notice
**Example:** A late fee applies to any invoice more than thirty days overdue.
**Note:** Confusable pair: due (đến hạn — "due on Friday") vs overdue (quá hạn — đã trễ rồi). In Part 2, "It's overdue" means already late, not merely scheduled.

**Use it now (gapped collocations — keys at the end of this file):**
1. Attach your receipts to the form to claim ______ for the taxi fare.
2. Invoices more than thirty days ______ are charged a late fee.
3. The auditor entered every payment in the general ______.
4. Thanks to cheaper materials, the project came in under ______.

## Domain 4 — Banking & insurance

Banking words sit on the customer's side of the counter — accounts, loans, statements — while insurance words describe policies, premiums and claims. Part 2 loves bank sound-alikes; Part 7 loves policy letters.

*VI: Từ ngân hàng đứng về phía khách hàng — tài khoản, khoản vay, sao kê — còn từ bảo hiểm mô tả hợp đồng, phí và bồi thường. Part 2 chuộng các cặp âm dễ nhầm về ngân hàng; Part 7 chuộng thư về hợp đồng bảo hiểm.*

### account (n) — tài khoản
**Collocations:** open / close an account · a savings / checking account · an account number · account for (v: giải trình)
**Example:** You can open a savings account with just a passport and a small deposit.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): account (tài khoản) vs amount (số tiền). "What's the account number?" vs "What amount did you deposit?" Part 5 also uses the verb account for (giải trình) — "account for the difference."

### deposit (n, v) — tiền gửi; gửi tiền, đặt cọc
**Collocations:** make a deposit · a security deposit · deposit a check · a large deposit
**Example:** A security deposit of one month's rent is due when you sign the lease.
**Note:** Confusable pair: deposit (tiền cọc giữ chỗ hoặc tiền gửi vào tài khoản) vs down payment (khoản trả trước khi mua). You *make* either one — rentals take deposits, purchases take down payments.

### withdraw (v) — rút (tiền)
**Collocations:** withdraw cash / funds · withdraw money from an account · a cash withdrawal
**Example:** She withdrew enough cash for the weekend farmers' market.
**Note:** Word-form trap: withdraw (v) vs withdrawal (n). Part 5 shows "a withdrawal slip"; in Part 2 the sound-alike is "withdraw" vs "with," so listen for the full verb.

### loan (n) — khoản vay
**Collocations:** take out a loan · pay off / repay a loan · a low-interest loan · apply for a loan
**Example:** The bakery took out a small loan to buy a second oven.
**Note:** Collocation trap: you *take out* a loan, never "take a loan." Triangle for Part 5: borrow (v — chủ ngữ là người mượn), lend (v — chủ ngữ là người cho), loan (n — khoản vay).

### interest rate (n) — lãi suất
**Collocations:** a low / fixed interest rate · interest rates rise · pay interest on a loan
**Example:** The credit union offered a fixed interest rate for the first year.
**Note:** Sound-alike trap (Part 2): interest (lãi) vs interested (hứng thú). "Are you interested in our new rates?" blends both; an answer about money points to the noun, an answer about enthusiasm to the adjective.

### mortgage (n) — khoản thế chấp mua nhà
**Collocations:** take out a mortgage · monthly mortgage payments · pay off the mortgage · refinance the mortgage
**Example:** Their monthly mortgage payments dropped after refinancing.
**Note:** B5 pronunciation note: the "t" in mortgage is silent — it sounds closer to "more-gij." Learners who meet the word in print first often miss it in Parts 3–4.

### statement (n) — sao kê; tuyên bố
**Collocations:** a bank / monthly statement · check a statement · an official statement · the enclosed statement
**Example:** Check your monthly statement for charges you do not recognize.
**Note:** Polysemy trap: statement (sao kê ngân hàng), statement (tuyên bố chính thức), closing statement (tổng kết cuối kỳ). Banking letters say "the enclosed statement"; press offices "issue a statement."

### branch (n) — chi nhánh
**Collocations:** a local branch · the branch manager · open a new branch · branch out into (mở rộng sang)
**Example:** The branch manager approved the loan on the spot.
**Note:** B5 polysemy: branch (chi nhánh) vs branch out (mở rộng sang lĩnh vực mới). "The bank branched out into insurance" hides both meanings in one line.

### teller (n) — giao dịch viên ngân hàng
**Collocations:** a bank teller · the teller window / counter · a part-time teller · teller training
**Example:** The teller counted the deposit twice before stamping the slip.
**Note:** Confusable pair: teller (nhân viên giao dịch ngân hàng) vs cashier (thu ngân ở cửa hàng). Bank postings in Part 7 say teller; store postings say cashier.

### credit limit (n) — hạn mức tín dụng
**Collocations:** raise / increase the credit limit · exceed the credit limit · a monthly credit limit
**Example:** She asked the bank to raise her credit limit before buying the equipment.
**Note:** Confusable family: credit limit (trần chi tiêu), credit rating / credit score (điểm tín dụng), letter of credit (thư tín dụng thương mại). Banking letters swap them — find which "credit" is on the table.

### wire (v) — chuyển tiền (điện)
**Collocations:** wire money / funds to · a wire transfer · wire the deposit today
**Example:** The supplier asked us to wire the deposit before Monday.
**Note:** Polysemy trap: wire (v: chuyển tiền) vs wire (n: dây điện) vs wiring (hệ thống điện trong nhà). In Part 3, "I'll wire it this afternoon" means money, not electricity.

### policy (n) — hợp đồng bảo hiểm; chính sách
**Collocations:** an insurance policy · the policyholder · renew a policy · company policy
**Example:** Renew the policy before it expires or the coverage lapses.
**Note:** Dual-meaning trap: policy (hợp đồng bảo hiểm) vs policy (chính sách, quy định). "The policy covers water damage" is insurance; "our return policy" is store rules — read the letter's topic sentence first.

### premium (n) — phí bảo hiểm
**Collocations:** pay a premium · an annual / monthly premium · raise premiums · a premium payment
**Example:** The monthly premium rises slightly once the policyholder turns forty.
**Note:** B5 dual meaning: premium (phí bảo hiểm) vs premium (cao cấp — "premium seats," "a premium price"). In insurance talk it is money; in ads it is luxury.

### claim (n, v) — yêu cầu bồi thường
**Collocations:** file / submit a claim · process a claim · claim on the policy · deny a claim
**Example:** File the claim within thirty days of the accident.
**Note:** Confusable pair: claim (yêu cầu bồi thường bảo hiểm) vs complaint (khiếu nại). You *file* a claim to receive money and *make* a complaint to express dissatisfaction — Part 5 fixes "file a claim" as a chunk.

### coverage (n) — phạm vi, quyền lợi bảo hiểm
**Collocations:** insurance coverage · full / partial coverage · dental coverage · coverage begins
**Example:** Dental coverage begins ninety days after your start date.
**Note:** Word family: cover (v: chi trả, bảo vệ), coverage (n), covered (adj: được bảo hiểm). "Is it covered?" is the stock Part 3 benefits question; the answer names the treatment and the plan.

### beneficiary (n) — người thụ hưởng
**Collocations:** name a beneficiary · the sole / primary beneficiary · update the beneficiary
**Example:** Name a beneficiary when you enroll in the life insurance plan.
**Note:** B5 word: the beneficiary receives the payout (người nhận tiền), while the policyholder owns the contract. Retirement-plan talks in Part 4 contrast the two roles in one breath.
