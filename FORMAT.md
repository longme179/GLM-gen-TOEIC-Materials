# FORMAT.md — Test format: verified facts, sources, and course choices

**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Purpose:** single source of truth for what this course claims about the TOEIC® Listening & Reading format

*VI: Tài liệu này ghi lại những dữ kiện về định dạng đề TOEIC® L&R đã được kiểm chứng (kèm nguồn), các giả định chưa kiểm chứng, và các điều chỉnh riêng của khóa học. Mọi tài liệu khác trong khóa học đều lấy dữ kiện từ đây.*

## Verified facts (checked 2026-11-02)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Total questions | 200 (two timed sections of 100 questions each) |
| Listening section | ~45 minutes, 100 questions |
| — Part 1: Photographs | 6 questions |
| — Part 2: Question–Response | 25 questions |
| — Part 3: Conversations | 39 questions |
| — Part 4: Talks | 30 questions |
| Reading section | 75 minutes, 100 questions |
| — Part 5: Incomplete Sentences | 30 questions |
| — Part 6: Text Completion | 16 questions |
| — Part 7: Reading Comprehension | 54 questions (29 single-passage + 25 multiple-passage) |
| Total test time | about 2 hours for the two timed sections |
| Scoring | Listening 5–495, Reading 5–495, total 10–990, in 5-point steps |
| Question type | multiple choice; Part 2 has three response options, other parts four |

## Marked as UNVERIFIED / course assumptions (do not treat as ETS facts)

- The exact mix of 2-speaker vs 3-speaker conversations inside Part 3, and the exact passage-type mix inside Part 7, **varies by test form**. Our practice tests use a realistic mix (see D8 below) but no claim is made that every real form matches it.
- Recommended internal time splits for the Reading section (used in this course's strategy sheets) are coaching conventions, not ETS statements.
- No raw-to-scaled conversion tables appear anywhere in this course: real conversions vary by test form, and this course's rule is to give only clearly labelled rough estimates.

## Course adaptations (deliberate, logged in STATE.md)

1. **Part 1 scene cards (D2):** the real Part 1 shows a photograph; this text-only course replaces photos with precisely written "scene cards". The skill trained (matching a scene to a paraphrased description) is preserved, but visual scanning practice is not — learners near test day should preview sample photo tasks on ETS's public sample materials.
2. **Scripts instead of audio (D3):** the real Listening section is delivered by audio. This course ships full scripts plus a read-aloud / TTS-app / partner protocol with pause marks and pacing guidance.
3. **Part 3 speakers (D8):** our conversations use 2 and 3 speakers; Part 7 uses 10 single passages (29 questions) + 5 linked multi-passage sets (25 questions).
4. **Bands (D5):** B1–B5 are course routing labels over the verified 10–990 scale, not ETS categories.
