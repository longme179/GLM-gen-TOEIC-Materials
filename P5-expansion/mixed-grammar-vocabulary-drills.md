# Mixed Grammar & Vocabulary Drills — All Bands
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Blocks:** A Foundation (B1 B2) · B Core (B3) · C Challenge (B4 B5) · **Total:** 40 items

This file is a mixed circuit of grammar and vocabulary in Part 5/6 single-sentence style: every item is one sentence with one blank (marked ----) and four options. Unlike the grammar atlases, nothing tells you in advance whether a blank tests a pattern or a word choice — that is the point. On the real test the categories are hidden, so the triage habit (label the slot before you read the options) has to survive without labels.

*VI: Đây là vòng luyện trộn ngữ pháp và từ vựng theo dạng câu đơn của Part 5/6: mỗi câu là một câu tiếng Anh có một chỗ trống (ký hiệu ----) và bốn phương án. Khác với các cuốn grammar atlas, không dòng nào báo trước chỗ trống đang kiểm tra pattern ngữ pháp hay lựa chọn từ — và đó chính là mục đích. Trong đề thi thật, thể loại câu hỏi được giấu đi, nên thói quen "định danh chỗ trống trước khi đọc phương án" phải sống được mà không cần nhãn.*

## How to use this file

1. **Pick your band's block.** Block A (B1–B2) drills the foundations: core word forms, agreement, simple tenses, high-frequency collocations, basic connectors. Block B (B3) is the heart of the real Part 5: passive, perfect tenses, relatives, despite / because of, and word choice between near-synonyms. Block C (B4–B5) trains the rare discrimination: subjunctive, inversion, reduced relatives, fine collocation pairs, word forms in unusual slots. When your block feels comfortable, move up one block — never down.
2. **Day 25 of the course uses this file for the review-day drill.** On that day you run your band's block timed, straight through, before the random re-test of old log rows (see `P3-daily-plan/day-25.md`). Target pace: about 20 seconds per item for B3 and above; B1–B2 work for accuracy first.
3. **Label before you look.** Before reading (A)–(D), name the slot out loud: verb form, noun, adjective, adverb, connector, preposition, or word choice. Only then read the options. The options are written to confirm a label you already hold — if you have no label, the options will decide for you, and that is how traps get chosen.
4. **Check per block, not per item.** Do the whole block on paper, then read the **Key**, **Why**, and **Traps** lines together — both the English line and the *VI:* line. The English teaches you the terminology; the Vietnamese line makes sure you actually understood it.
5. **Every miss becomes one error-log row** (`P2-reference/error-log-template.md`): a pattern applied wrong → **Grammar** bucket; a word-choice or paraphrase miss → **Meaning** bucket; a blank never reached under time → **Process** bucket. A miss without its row is a miss that will win again.

*VI: Cách dùng: (1) Chọn đúng khối theo băng của bạn — khối A (B1–B2) luyện nền tảng, khối B (B3) là phần lõi của Part 5 thật, khối C (B4–B5) luyện phân biệt nâng cao; khi khối hiện tại đã "nhẹ", lên một khối, không bao giờ hạ hai khối. (2) Ngày 25 của khóa học dùng file này cho bài luyện ngày ôn: làm trọn khối của mình có tính giờ, một mạch, trước khi làm lại ngẫu nhiên các câu cũ trong sổ; tốc độ mục tiêu khoảng 20 giây/câu với B3 trở lên, riêng B1–B2 ưu tiên độ chính xác. (3) Định danh trước khi nhìn: gọi tên vị trí trống thành tiếng (dạng động từ, danh từ, tính từ, trạng từ, từ nối, giới từ, hay lựa chọn từ) rồi mới đọc phương án — nếu bạn không có nhãn, phương án sẽ quyết định thay bạn, và đó là cách bẫy được chọn. (4) Chấm theo khối, không chấm từng câu; đọc kỹ cả dòng tiếng Anh lẫn dòng *VI:* của phần giải thích. (5) Mỗi câu sai ghi đúng một dòng sổ lỗi: sai pattern → nhóm Grammar; sai chọn từ/diễn đạt → nhóm Meaning; bỏ lỡ câu vì hết giờ → nhóm Process — một câu sai không được ghi sổ là một câu sai sẽ thắng lại lần sau.*

### Block A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2) — 12 items (Q1–Q12)

*Focus: core word forms (Q1–Q3), subject–verb agreement (Q4–Q5), simple tenses (Q6–Q7), high-frequency collocations (Q8–Q9), basic connectors (Q10–Q11), fixed phrases (Q12).*

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
The warehouse crew loaded the delivery trucks ----, finishing two hours ahead of schedule.
(A) efficiently
(B) efficient
(C) efficiency
(D) efficiencies

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The blank tells us how the trucks were loaded, so it modifies the verb "loaded" and must be the adverb "efficiently". / *VI: Chỗ trống trả lời câu hỏi "làm thế nào" đối với hành động xếp hàng lên xe, tức là bổ nghĩa cho động từ "loaded", nên phải dùng trạng từ "efficiently" (một cách hiệu quả).*
- **Traps:** (B) "efficient" is the adjective form and cannot modify a verb; (C) "efficiency" is the abstract noun of the family; (D) "efficiencies" is a plural count noun for savings, not a description of an action.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
The trainer gave a clear ---- of the new invoicing procedure at the morning briefing.
(A) explain
(B) explanation
(C) explanatory
(D) explaining

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The blank follows the article "a" and the adjective "clear", so only a noun can stand in the slot; "explanation" is the noun of the family. / *VI: Chỗ trống đứng sau mạo từ "a" và tính từ "clear" nên chỉ danh từ mới đứng được ở vị trí này; "explanation" là dạng danh từ của họ từ này.*
- **Traps:** (A) "explain" is the base verb and cannot follow "a clear"; (C) "explanatory" is an adjective and would leave the phrase with no head noun; (D) "explaining" is a gerund, not the noun the slot demands.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
The orientation session was so ---- that several new hires asked for a copy of the slides.
(A) inform
(B) information
(C) informative
(D) informatively

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The frame "so + ---- + that" requires an adjective between "so" and "that", and "informative" is the adjective of the family. / *VI: Khung "so + tính từ + that" đòi hỏi một tính từ đứng giữa "so" và "that", và "informative" chính là dạng tính từ của họ từ này.*
- **Traps:** (A) "inform" is a verb; (B) "information" is an uncountable noun that cannot follow "so" in this frame; (D) "informatively" is an adverb, and adverbs do not describe the noun "session" through the linking verb "was".

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
The schedule of departures ---- updated at the terminal every morning at six.
(A) are
(B) were
(C) have been
(D) is

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The grammatical subject is "The schedule" (singular); "of departures" is only a prepositional phrase, and "every morning" calls for the simple present. / *VI: Chủ ngữ ngữ pháp là "The schedule" (số ít); "of departures" chỉ là cụm giới từ, còn "every morning" đòi hỏi hiện tại đơn.*
- **Traps:** (A) "are" copies the plural of "departures", the noun nearest the verb; (B) is plural and past; (C) "have been" repeats the plural error and adds a wrong tense.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
Each of the conference rooms ---- equipped with a projector and a whiteboard.
(A) is
(B) are
(C) were
(D) be

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Each of the conference rooms" is grammatically singular — the true subject is "Each" — and the present passive "is equipped" matches both number and tense. / *VI: "Each of the conference rooms" về mặt ngữ pháp là chủ ngữ số ít — chủ ngữ thật là "Each" — nên bị động hiện tại "is equipped" đúng cả về số lẫn thì.*
- **Traps:** (B) "are" agrees with "rooms" instead of "Each"; (C) "were" is plural and past for a standing fact; (D) "be" is a base form that cannot serve as the main verb here without a modal.

#### Q6. (Bands: B1 B2)
The shuttle to the convention center ---- every thirty minutes on weekends.
(A) left
(B) leaves
(C) is leaving
(D) has left

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Every thirty minutes" marks a fixed timetable, and timetables take the simple present: "leaves". / *VI: "Every thirty minutes" là dấu hiệu của lịch trình cố định, và lịch trình cố định dùng hiện tại đơn: "leaves".*
- **Traps:** (A) the past "left" cuts the schedule off from the present; (C) the progressive describes one run happening right now, not a timetable; (D) the present perfect reports a completed event, not a recurring one.

#### Q7. (Bands: B1 B2)
Last quarter, the purchasing department ---- sixty cases of printer paper from a new supplier.
(A) orders
(B) will order
(C) ordered
(D) is ordering

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Last quarter" is a finished past time marker, so the completed purchase needs the simple past "ordered". / *VI: "Last quarter" là mốc thời gian quá khứ đã khép lại, nên lần mua hàng đã hoàn tất này phải dùng quá khứ đơn "ordered".*
- **Traps:** (A) the present "orders" ignores the past marker; (B) the future contradicts a purchase already made; (D) the progressive places the action in the present moment.

#### Q8. (Bands: B1 B2)
The catalog team worked over the weekend to ---- the printing deadline.
(A) catch
(B) chase
(C) reach
(D) meet

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The fixed collocation is "meet a deadline" — to finish the work by the required time; the other verbs do not pair with "deadline" in standard business English. / *VI: Cụm cố định là "meet a deadline" — hoàn thành công việc đúng thời hạn; các động từ khác không đi cặp với "deadline" trong tiếng Anh công sở chuẩn.*
- **Traps:** (A) "catch" belongs to trains and buses ("catch a train"); (B) "chase" means to pursue something that moves; (C) "reach" fits places and goals ("reach a target"), not deadlines.

#### Q9. (Bands: B1 B2)
Please ---- an order for more toner and notepads before Friday.
(A) place
(B) put
(C) make
(D) do

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The fixed business collocation for ordering goods is "place an order"; no other verb in the set takes "order" as its object with this meaning. / *VI: Cụm cố định để đặt mua hàng hóa là "place an order"; không động từ nào khác trong bộ này lấy "order" làm tân ngữ với nghĩa đặt hàng.*
- **Traps:** (B) "put an order" is a word-by-word translation shape that English does not use; (C) "make" pairs with "a decision" or "an appointment", not with "order" in this sense; (D) "do an order" is not a standard phrase.

#### Q10. (Bands: B1 B2)
The freight elevator is out of service, ---- please carry the boxes up the east stairs.
(A) or
(B) so
(C) but
(D) yet

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The first clause gives the cause (the elevator is broken) and the second gives its practical result (use the stairs); "so" links a cause to its result. / *VI: Vế đầu nêu nguyên nhân (thang máy hỏng), vế sau nêu kết quả thực tế (hàng đi cầu thang phía đông); "so" nối nguyên nhân với kết quả.*
- **Traps:** (A) "or" offers a choice between two actions; (C) "but" signals contrast, which reverses the logic; (D) "yet" also concedes or contrasts, and no contrast exists here.

#### Q11. (Bands: B1 B2)
---- the shipment arrives, call the warehouse manager right away.
(A) While
(B) Until
(C) When
(D) During

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The sentence needs a time link for one event followed by an immediate action: "When the shipment arrives, call ...". / *VI: Câu cần một từ nối thời gian cho một sự kiện đơn lẻ đi kèm hành động ngay sau đó: "When the shipment arrives, call ...".*
- **Traps:** (A) "while" joins two ongoing actions of some length; (B) "until" would mean the calling stops once the shipment arrives; (D) "during" is a preposition and cannot introduce a clause with a verb.

#### Q12. (Bands: B1 B2)
Ms. Reyes will be ---- charge of payroll while the supervisor is on leave.
(A) on
(B) at
(C) for
(D) in

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The fixed phrase for being responsible for an area of work is "in charge of". / *VI: Cụm cố định chỉ việc phụ trách một mảng công việc là "in charge of".*
- **Traps:** (A) "on charge" does not exist in standard English; (B) "at" marks locations and clock times; (C) "for charge" is not a fixed pair — the preposition belongs to "in charge", not to the noun that follows.

### Block B — Core (Bands: B3) — 14 items (Q1–Q14)

*Focus: passive voice (Q1–Q3), perfect tenses (Q4–Q6), relative pronouns (Q7–Q8), despite / because of (Q9–Q10), near-synonym word choice (Q11–Q14).*

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
Applications for a trade-fair booth must ---- no later than Friday.
(A) submit
(B) submitting
(C) be submitted
(D) are submitted

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The applications receive the action, so after the modal "must" the passive is needed: "must + be + past participle". / *VI: Đơn đăng ký chịu tác động của hành động, nên sau trợ động từ khuyết thiếu "must" cần dạng bị động: "must be submitted".*
- **Traps:** (A) is the active base verb; (B) is a gerund, which cannot follow "must"; (D) "are submitted" adds a conjugated verb where the modal already carries tense and agreement.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
During the safety inspection several machines were found to be defective, and production ---- for two days.
(A) was suspended
(B) suspended
(C) is suspended
(D) has suspended

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The past context ("were found") plus a subject that receives the action requires the past passive "was suspended". / *VI: Ngữ cảnh quá khứ ("were found") cùng chủ ngữ chịu tác động của hành động đòi hỏi bị động quá khứ "was suspended".*
- **Traps:** (B) is the active past, as if production suspended something itself; (C) switches to the present for a finished event; (D) is present perfect active and points the action the wrong way.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
The annual report ---- to all shareholders at the end of every fiscal year.
(A) mails
(B) is mailing
(C) mailed
(D) is mailed

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The report receives the mailing, and "every fiscal year" marks a standing routine, so the habitual passive "is mailed" is required. / *VI: Báo cáo chịu hành động được gửi, còn "every fiscal year" đánh dấu việc lặp lại thường lệ, nên cần bị động hiện tại đơn "is mailed".*
- **Traps:** (A) the active "mails" would make the report do the sending; (B) "is mailing" is active progressive; (C) "mailed" reads as active past or as a participle with no auxiliary, leaving the sentence without a proper verb.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
Ms. Ferrante ---- the night shift since the plant began operating around the clock.
(A) supervises
(B) supervised
(C) has supervised
(D) is supervising

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Since + a past event" ("since the plant began") demands the present perfect for the action that continues up to now. / *VI: "Since + sự kiện quá khứ" ("từ khi nhà máy bắt đầu") đòi hỏi hiện tại hoàn thành cho hành động kéo dài liên tục đến hiện tại.*
- **Traps:** (A) the simple present erases the link to the past starting point; (B) the simple past closes the supervision entirely in the past; (D) the progressive suggests a temporary arrangement rather than an unbroken period.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
By the time the moving truck arrived, the staff ---- most of the boxes to the new office.
(A) moved
(B) have moved
(C) moves
(D) had moved

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** Of the two past events, the moving was completed before the truck arrived, and the past perfect marks the earlier action. / *VI: Trong hai hành động quá khứ, việc chuyển đồ hoàn tất trước khi xe tải đến, và quá khứ hoàn thành đánh dấu hành động xảy ra sớm hơn.*
- **Traps:** (A) the simple past loses the before/after sequence; (B) the present perfect cannot sit before a past reference point; (C) a present form clashes with the finished past frame.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
We ---- three complaints about the new parking policy this week.
(A) have received
(B) received
(C) receive
(D) are receiving

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "This week" is an unfinished period that includes the present, and only the present perfect connects the past complaints to now. / *VI: "This week" là khoảng thời gian chưa khép lại và bao gồm cả hiện tại, nên chỉ hiện tại hoàn thành mới nối các lời phàn nàn trong quá khứ với thời điểm hiện tại.*
- **Traps:** (B) the simple past fits a closed period ("last week") and severs the link to now; (C) the simple present states a habit, not a running total; (D) the progressive would mean complaints are arriving at this very moment.

#### Q7. (Bands: B3)
The auditor ---- examined our accounts last spring has requested two additional documents.
(A) who
(B) whom
(C) whose
(D) which

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** The relative pronoun acts as the subject of "examined", and for a person in the subject role the word is "who". / *VI: Đại từ quan hệ giữ vai trò chủ ngữ của động từ "examined", và với vai trò chủ ngữ chỉ người, từ cần dùng là "who".*
- **Traps:** (B) "whom" is for objects, not subjects; (C) "whose" shows possession, and no possession is involved; (D) "which" refers to things, and an auditor is a person.

#### Q8. (Bands: B3)
The parking garage ---- visitors may leave their cars is located behind the convention center.
(A) that
(B) where
(C) which
(D) when

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The clause after the blank is already complete ("visitors may leave their cars"), so what is missing is the place link "in which" — the job of "where". / *VI: Mệnh đề sau chỗ trống đã đầy đủ ("visitors may leave their cars"), nên thứ còn thiếu là liên kết về nơi chốn "in which" — nhiệm vụ của "where".*
- **Traps:** (A) "that" would have to fill a subject or object slot inside the clause, but the clause already has both; (C) "which" fails for the same reason; (D) "when" links times, and a garage is a place.

#### Q9. (Bands: B3)
---- the long lines at check-in, every passenger boarded the ferry on time.
(A) Although
(B) Even though
(C) Despite
(D) Because of

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** What follows the blank is a noun phrase ("the long lines..."), so only the preposition-style "Despite" can head it — and the meaning of the sentence is contrast. / *VI: Phần đứng sau chỗ trống là một cụm danh từ ("the long lines..."), nên chỉ "Despite" — loại từ nối kiểu giới từ — mới dẫn đầu được, và nghĩa của câu là tương phản.*
- **Traps:** (A) "Although" must be followed by subject + verb; (B) "Even though" carries the same clause requirement; (D) "Because of" also takes a noun phrase but reverses the logic — the lines would then explain the punctual boarding.

#### Q10. (Bands: B3)
The morning workshop was canceled ---- too few people registered for it.
(A) because of
(B) because
(C) due to
(D) in spite of

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** What follows is a full clause with its own subject and verb ("too few people registered"), and only "because" introduces a clause of cause. / *VI: Phần theo sau là một mệnh đề đầy đủ có chủ ngữ và động từ riêng ("too few people registered"), và chỉ "because" mới dẫn vào được mệnh đề chỉ nguyên nhân.*
- **Traps:** (A) "because of" demands a noun phrase; (C) "due to" likewise needs a noun and never stands alone; (D) "in spite of" takes a noun and signals contrast rather than cause.

#### Q11. (Bands: B3)
Before the tour bus departed, each passenger received a printed ---- listing every stop and its departure time.
(A) agenda
(B) itinerary
(C) memo
(D) minutes

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** A list of the stops and times of a journey is an "itinerary" — the travel word of the set. / *VI: Danh sách các điểm dừng và giờ khởi hành của một hành trình là một "itinerary" — từ chỉ lịch trình chuyến đi trong bộ phương án này.*
- **Traps:** (A) an "agenda" is the list of items for a meeting, not for a journey; (C) a "memo" is a short internal notice to staff; (D) "minutes" are the written record of what happened at a meeting.

#### Q12. (Bands: B3)
The human resources department has posted the ---- position in the sales team on three job boards.
(A) vacant
(B) empty
(C) blank
(D) hollow

- **Key:** A
- **Why:** A job that officially exists and is open for applicants is "vacant"; the word belongs to positions, rooms, and lots. / *VI: Vị trí công việc chính thức còn trống và đang tuyển người được gọi là "vacant"; từ này dùng cho chức danh, phòng và đất trống.*
- **Traps:** (B) "empty" means physically unoccupied, which does not describe a job posting; (C) "blank" describes an unwritten surface, like a form or a screen; (D) "hollow" describes a physical inside, like a tube or a tree.

#### Q13. (Bands: B3)
If the toaster stops working, return it to the store with your ---- for a full refund.
(A) invoice
(B) recipe
(C) receipt
(D) prescription

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The proof of purchase from a store is a "receipt" — exactly the paper a refund policy asks to see. / *VI: Giấy chứng nhận đã mua hàng ở cửa hàng là "receipt" — chính là tờ giấy mà chính sách hoàn tiền yêu cầu xuất trình.*
- **Traps:** (A) an "invoice" is a business document that requests payment; (B) "recipe" is the near-sound look-alike meaning a set of cooking instructions; (D) a "prescription" is a doctor's order for medicine.

#### Q14. (Bands: B3)
Passengers who miss the morning ferry may ---- their tickets to an afternoon departure at no extra cost.
(A) refuse
(B) return
(C) exchange
(D) reschedule

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** To move a booking to a later time is to "reschedule" it, and the pair "reschedule ... to" fits the phrase that follows. / *VI: Dời một lượt đặt chỗ sang thời gian muộn hơn là "reschedule", và cặp "reschedule ... to" khớp với cụm theo sau.*
- **Traps:** (A) "refuse" means to say no to something offered; (B) "return" is for giving merchandise back to a store; (C) "exchange" means to swap one thing for another thing, not to move a booking in time.

### Block C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5) — 14 items (Q1–Q14)

*Focus: subjunctive (Q1–Q2), inversion (Q3–Q4), reduced relatives (Q5–Q6), fine collocation and look-alike pairs (Q7–Q10), word forms in rare slots (Q11–Q14).*

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
The consultant suggested that the branch ---- its customer-service hours until sales recover.
(A) shortens
(B) shorten
(C) shortened
(D) will shorten

- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "Suggested that" triggers the mandative subjunctive, so the base form "shorten" is required — no -s on the third-person subject. / *VI: "Suggested that" kích hoạt dạng giả định mệnh lệnh, nên phải dùng động từ nguyên mẫu "shorten" — không thêm -s cho chủ ngữ ngôi ba số ít.*
- **Traps:** (A) the indicative "shortens" is what the ear expects after a singular subject; (C) the past form doubles the error; (D) "will shorten" imports a future meaning that the subjunctive frame does not allow.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
The client insists that every outgoing shipment ---- inspected before it leaves the warehouse.
(A) is
(B) was
(C) has been
(D) be

- **Key:** D
- **Why:** "Insists that" demands the subjunctive, and for an action received, the base form "be" stands before the participle: "be inspected". / *VI: "Insists that" đòi hỏi dạng giả định, và với hành động bị nhận, dạng nguyên mẫu "be" đứng trước phân từ: "be inspected".*
- **Traps:** (A) "is" is the normal indicative that the ear reaches for; (B) the past "was" breaks both mood and tense; (C) "has been" reads as a real completed event rather than a demanded one.

#### Q3. (Bands: B4 B5)

(A) the managers realized
(B) the managers had realized
(C) did the managers realize
(D) realized the managers

- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "Not until ..." is a negative opener, so the auxiliary must invert with the subject — "did the managers realize" — with do-support for the simple past. / *VI: "Not until ..." là cấu trúc mở đầu phủ định, nên trợ động từ phải đảo lên trước chủ ngữ — "did the managers realize" — có "did" hỗ trợ cho quá khứ đơn.*
- **Traps:** (A) keeps the normal word order that the negative opener forbids; (B) inverts nothing and chooses the wrong tense; (D) drops the required do-support and puts the lexical verb itself before the subject.

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)

#### Q5. (Bands: B4 B5)

#### Q7. (Bands: B4 B5)

(B) conform
(C) obey
(D) follow

#### Q8. (Bands: B4 B5)
