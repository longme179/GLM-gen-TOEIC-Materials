# Part 7 Speed-Reading Set — Timed Blocks
**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Blocks:** A (B1 B2) · B (B3) · C (B4 B5) · **Total:** 16 questions in 3 timed blocks

The P4 drill file taught the Part 7 loop with the clock off: question stem → type tag → anchor scan → decide. This file switches the clock on. It is a speed-reading circuit of 16 questions in three timed blocks — Block A for bands B1–B2 (target: 5 minutes), Block B for B3 (target: 8 minutes), and Block C for B4–B5 (target: 8 minutes) — built from fresh passages: e-mails, notices, an advertisement, a memo, a company policy, a customer-service exchange, and a dense article excerpt. The per-passage budgets behind the targets — roughly 1.5–2 minutes for a single passage, roughly 3 minutes for a double set — are this course's pacing conventions: coaching rules, not ETS rules.

*VI: File luyện tập ở P4 dạy vòng lặp Part 7 khi tắt đồng hồ: thân câu hỏi → gắn nhãn dạng → quét mỏ neo → quyết định. File này bật đồng hồ lên. Đây là bài luyện đọc nhanh gồm 16 câu trong ba block tính giờ — Block A cho băng B1–B2 (mục tiêu 5 phút), Block B cho B3 (mục tiêu 8 phút), Block C cho B4–B5 (mục tiêu 8 phút) — dựng từ các đoạn văn hoàn toàn mới: e-mail, thông báo, quảng cáo, bản ghi nhớ, chính sách công ty, thư qua lại với bộ phận chăm sóc khách hàng, và một trích đoạn báo khá dày. Định mức sau mỗi mục tiêu — khoảng 1,5–2 phút cho một đoạn đơn, khoảng 3 phút cho một bộ đôi — là quy ước phân bổ thời gian của khóa học: quy tắc luyện tập, không phải quy định của ETS.*

## How to use this file

Run ONE block per session — the block for your band (B1–B2 → A, B3 → B, B4–B5 → C) — with a stopwatch running from the first passage to the last question of the block. On every question, run the loop: question stem → type tag → anchor scan → decide. Stop the clock at the end of the block, and only then check the keys. After checking, log two kinds of entries, not one: every miss, AND every question you answered over budget — a slow correct answer is a point you will lose somewhere else on test day. Use the per-passage budgets to decide what counts as over budget, and write each logged item as "Block + question number" (for example, "Block B, Q5"), because question numbering restarts in every block. When your band's block is comfortable inside its target, move up one block as a stretch goal.

*VI: Mỗi phiên chỉ chạy MỘT block — đúng block của băng bạn (B1–B2 → A, B3 → B, B4–B5 → C) — và bấm đồng hồ từ lúc đọc đoạn văn đầu tiên đến hết câu cuối của block. Ở mỗi câu hỏi, chạy vòng lặp: thân câu hỏi → gắn nhãn dạng → quét mỏ neo → quyết định. Dừng đồng hồ ở cuối block, và chỉ lúc đó mới mở đáp án. Sau khi chấm, hãy ghi nhật ký hai loại, chứ không phải một: mọi câu sai, VÀ mọi câu bạn làm quá thời gian dự toán — một câu đúng nhưng chậm là điểm bạn sẽ đánh mất ở câu khác trong ngày thi thật. Dùng định mức từng đoạn để biết thế nào là "quá dự toán", và ghi mỗi mục là "Block + số câu" (ví dụ "Block B, Q5"), vì số câu hỏi khởi động lại từ 1 ở mỗi block. Khi block của băng bạn đã thoải mái trong khung giờ, hãy lên một block để luyện vươn xa.*

### Block A — Foundation (Bands: B1 B2) · Target: 5 minutes

Two single passages — an e-mail and a notice — two questions each, at a foundation pace of about 2.5 minutes per passage (Questions 1–4).

*VI: Hai đoạn đơn — một e-mail và một thông báo — mỗi đoạn hai câu hỏi, với nhịp nền tảng khoảng 2,5 phút mỗi đoạn (câu 1–4).*

### Reading Passage 1 (Questions 1–2)

> **From:** d.okafor@lindenplaza.com
> **To:** tenants@lindenplaza.com
> **Date:** March 4
> **Subject:** Rooftop terrace closed Tuesday for floor resealing
>
> Dear Residents,
>
> The rooftop terrace will be closed on Tuesday, March 12, from 8:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M. while a work crew reseals the floor surface. The job was originally planned over two days, but the contractor has now confirmed it can be finished in one.
>
> During the closure, the third-floor lounge will stay open two hours later than usual, until 10:00 P.M., for residents who want a quiet place to relax. To reach the lounge, please use the stairway beside Unit 312; the elevator at the end of that corridor will be reserved for the crew's equipment all day.
>
> Thank you for your patience. If you have questions about the schedule, simply reply to this e-mail.
>
> Dolores Okafor, Building Manager

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
Why did Ms. Okafor send this e-mail?
(A) To explain a temporary closure and how residents can work around it
(B) To apologize for an error in a resident's monthly bill
(C) To recruit volunteers to help the crew reseal the floor
(D) To ask residents to vote on new lounge opening hours
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Purpose question** — a building-wide e-mail that announces a one-day closure (terrace, March 12) and immediately offers the workaround (lounge open late, stairway route) has one job: inform and give options. / *VI: Dạng mục đích — một e-mail gửi toàn thể cư dân, thông báo việc đóng cửa một ngày (sân thượng, ngày 12/3) và ngay lập tức đưa ra phương án thay thế (lounge mở khuya, đường đi bằng cầu thang), chỉ có một nhiệm vụ: thông báo và đưa lựa chọn.*
- **Traps:** (B) out of scope — no bill is mentioned; (C) distortion — a hired contractor, not volunteers, does the work; (D) quiet reversal — the manager changes the lounge hours herself; nothing is put to a vote.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
What should residents do to reach the lounge on March 12?
(A) Take the elevator at the end of the corridor
(B) Wait until 4:00 P.M., when the terrace reopens
(C) Use the stairway next to Unit 312
(D) Reserve a time slot by replying to the e-mail
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Detail question** — anchor: "To reach the lounge" → "please use the stairway beside Unit 312". / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo: "để đến lounge" → "hãy dùng cầu thang bên cạnh căn hộ 312".*
- **Traps:** (A) quiet reversal — that elevator is reserved for the crew's equipment; (B) out of scope — the terrace's reopening has nothing to do with reaching the lounge, which is open all day; (D) echo trap — the reply line is for questions, not for booking.

### Reading Passage 2 (Questions 3–4)

> **NOTICE — Eastgate Business Park Shuttle: Temporary Schedule**
>
> From Monday, October 20, through Friday, October 31, the park's shuttle will follow a temporary schedule while the North Bridge is inspected. All trips will use the River Road route, which adds about ten minutes to each journey.
>
> | Departure from Gate 2 | Arrival at Riverside Station |
> |---|---|
> | 6:40 A.M. | 7:05 A.M. |
> | 7:20 A.M. | 7:45 A.M. |
> | 4:50 P.M. | 5:15 P.M. |
>
> The 7:50 A.M. departure is canceled during the inspection. Passengers with early-morning trains should plan accordingly. Regular service resumes on Monday, November 3. For questions, please visit the front desk in Building A.

#### Q3. (Bands: B1 B2)
According to the temporary schedule, how long does a trip from Gate 2 to Riverside Station take?
(A) 10 minutes
(B) 25 minutes
(C) 45 minutes
(D) 50 minutes
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** **Detail question (schedule math)** — every row pairs a departure and an arrival twenty-five minutes apart (6:40 → 7:05). / *VI: Dạng chi tiết (tính từ lịch trình) — mỗi dòng của bảng cho một cặp giờ đi và giờ đến cách nhau 25 phút (6:40 → 7:05).*
- **Traps:** (A) echo trap — ten minutes is what the detour adds, not the trip length; (C) distortion — 4:50 misread as a duration; (D) echo trap — 7:50 is the canceled departure, not a travel time.

#### Q4. (Bands: B1 B2)
What will happen on November 3?
(A) The North Bridge will close for repairs.
(B) The River Road route will become the permanent route.
(C) The 7:50 A.M. departure will be canceled again.
(D) The shuttle will return to its regular schedule.
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** **Detail question** — anchor: "November 3" → "Regular service resumes on Monday, November 3." / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo: "ngày 3/11" → "Lịch chạy bình thường sẽ tiếp tục vào thứ Hai, ngày 3/11".*
- **Traps:** (A) distortion — the inspection ends October 31; no repair closure is announced; (B) out of scope — the detour is explicitly temporary; (C) quiet reversal — the cancellation runs only through October 31.

**Pacing note:** If the block cost more than 5 minutes, do not reread the passages — reread your log: find the question that sent you back into the passage without an anchor, and redo the block tomorrow hunting anchors only. *VI: Nếu block này tốn hơn 5 phút, đừng đọc lại các đoạn văn — hãy đọc lại nhật ký: tìm câu hỏi đã đẩy bạn quay vào đoạn văn mà không có mỏ neo, rồi ngày mai làm lại block, lần này chỉ săn mỏ neo.*

### Block B — Core (Bands: B3) · Target: 8 minutes

Numbering restarts. Two single passages — an advertisement and a memo — then one linked double set: an e-mail and the company policy it must satisfy, with one cross-reference question that needs both texts (Questions 1–6).

*VI: Số câu bắt đầu lại từ 1. Hai đoạn đơn — một quảng cáo và một bản ghi nhớ — rồi một bộ đôi có liên kết: một e-mail kèm chính sách công ty mà e-mail đó phải tuân theo, trong đó có một câu đối chiếu bắt buộc dùng cả hai văn bản (câu 1–6).*

### Reading Passage 1 (Questions 1–2)

> **ADVERTISEMENT — Cornerstone Home Painting: The Spring Refresh**
>
> One crew, one van, fourteen years of happy customers. Book any two rooms this month, and we paint the hallway joining them at no charge.
>
> | Room | One coat | Two coats |
> |---|---|---|
> | Bedroom (up to 20 m²) | $140 | $190 |
> | Living room (up to 35 m²) | $210 | $270 |
> | Hallway or stairwell | $95 | $120 |
>
> Our low-odor paint dries in two hours, so painted rooms can be used again the same evening. Furniture is moved and covered by our crew at no extra cost, and walls are repaired and primed before the first coat. Every job is inspected the following week, and any spots we missed are repainted free. For a free estimate, call 555-0198.

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
What does Cornerstone Home Painting promise to do after completing a job?
(A) Return every piece of furniture to its original position
(B) Apply a second coat at no extra charge
(C) Send the customer a written quality report
(D) Inspect the work and repaint any spots that were missed
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** **Detail question** — anchor: after the job → "Every job is inspected the following week, and any spots we missed are repainted free." / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo: sau khi xong việc → "Mỗi công trình đều được kiểm tra trong tuần kế tiếp, và mọi chỗ sơn sót sẽ được sơn lại miễn phí".*
- **Traps:** (A) echo trap — furniture is moved and covered during the job; no return promise is made; (B) lookalike — a second coat is a paid option in the price table; (C) distortion — an inspection happens, but no report is ever mentioned.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
What can be inferred about a bedroom painted on Monday morning?
(A) The room can be used again before the end of that day.
(B) The crew will charge extra to move the bed out of the room.
(C) The hallway beside the bedroom will be painted free of charge.
(D) A second coat will be needed on the following day.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Inference question** — the paint "dries in two hours, so painted rooms can be used again the same evening"; a bedroom painted Monday morning is usable Monday night. / *VI: Dạng suy luận — loại sơn ít mùi "khô trong hai tiếng, nên phòng mới sơn có thể dùng lại ngay buổi tối hôm đó"; phòng ngủ sơn sáng thứ Hai là dùng được ngay tối thứ Hai.*
- **Traps:** (B) quiet reversal — moving furniture costs nothing extra; (C) half-right echo — the free hallway requires booking two rooms, and one bedroom is not two; (D) quiet reversal — two coats is a choice in the price table, not a requirement.

### Reading Passage 2 (Questions 3–4)

> **MEMORANDUM**
> **To:** All staff, Portside Insurance Group
> **From:** Priya Raman, Facilities Coordinator
> **Date:** October 6
> **Re:** New coffee vendor in the staff lounge
>
> Beginning Monday, October 14, Halloran Coffee will operate the kiosk in the staff lounge. Our previous vendor's contract ended last month, and Halloran received the most votes in the tasting sessions held during the September staff picnic.
>
> The kiosk will be open from 7:00 A.M. to 2:00 P.M., and a price list will be posted beside the counter. Staff who bring their own cups receive a fifty-cent discount on drip coffee. Please note that the lounge refrigerator will be unavailable on Friday, October 11, while it is moved to a new position to make room for the vendor's refrigerated display case.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
How was Halloran Coffee chosen as the new vendor?
(A) It offered the lowest prices among the candidates.
(B) It was the only company that applied for the contract.
(C) It earned the most votes in staff tasting sessions.
(D) It had run the kiosk before the previous vendor did.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Detail question** — anchor: "Halloran received the most votes in the tasting sessions held during the September staff picnic." / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo: "Halloran nhận được nhiều phiếu nhất trong các buổi nếm thử tổ chức tại buổi dã ngoại của nhân viên vào tháng Chín".*
- **Traps:** (A) out of scope — prices appear only as a future price list; (B) distortion — other candidates existed, because votes were counted; (D) quiet reversal — Halloran is the new vendor; the previous vendor's contract ended.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
Why will the refrigerator be unavailable on October 11?
(A) It is being replaced with a larger model.
(B) It must be moved to clear space for a display case.
(C) It will be cleaned before the kiosk's opening day.
(D) It has been reserved for the vendor's supplies.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** **Detail question (cause)** — anchor: "October 11" → the refrigerator is "moved to a new position to make room for the vendor's refrigerated display case". / *VI: Dạng chi tiết (tìm nguyên nhân) — mỏ neo: "ngày 11/10" → chiếc tủ lạnh được "dời sang vị trí mới để nhường chỗ cho tủ trưng bày giữ lạnh của nhà cung cấp".*
- **Traps:** (A) lookalike — it is moved, not replaced; (C) out of scope — a plausible-sounding chore that never appears in the memo; (D) distortion — what is reserved is floor space, not the refrigerator.

### Reading Passages 3A & 3B (Questions 5–6)

> **Text 3A — E-mail**
>
> **From:** j.lindqvist@bluehavenfoods.com
> **To:** a.tanaka@bluehavenfoods.com
> **Date:** May 2
> **Subject:** Approval to attend the Sustainable Packaging Forum
>
> Hello Mr. Tanaka,
>
> The Sustainable Packaging Forum takes place in Chicago on June 12 and 13, and I would like to represent our team there. My presentation proposal was accepted, so I would speak on the second morning about our compostable-wrap project. Registration costs $280 until the May 10 early deadline and $340 afterward. If you approve, I will submit the registration form this Friday, while the lower fee still applies. After the forum I will circulate a summary of the sessions to the department.
>
> Jasmin Lindqvist, Packaging Development

> **Text 3B — Staff Conference Policy, Bluehaven Foods Human Resources**
>
> Employees may attend up to two professional conferences per year with their department manager's written approval. Requests must reach the manager at least four weeks before the event. Registration fees are reimbursed in full when the fee is under $300; for fees of $300 or more, the department pays 70 percent and the employee pays the remaining 30 percent. Hotel and travel costs are reimbursed at cost when receipts are submitted. A one-page attendance report is due within two weeks of returning to work.

#### Q5. (Bands: B3)
If Ms. Lindqvist registers before May 10, how much of her registration fee will the company cover?
(A) 70 percent of $340
(B) 70 percent of $280
(C) The entire $280 fee
(D) None of it, because her request is too late
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** **Cross-reference question — needs BOTH texts.** From 3A: the fee is $280 if she registers by May 10. From 3B: fees under $300 are reimbursed in full — so the whole $280 is covered. / *VI: Dạng đối chiếu hai đoạn — cần CẢ HAI văn bản. Từ 3A: mức phí là 280 đô nếu đăng ký trước ngày 10/5. Từ 3B: phí dưới 300 đô được hoàn trả toàn phần — nên cả 280 đô đều do công ty chi trả.*
- **Traps:** (A) wrong text — $340 is the late fee, and the 70 percent share applies only at $300 or more; (B) distortion — the 70/30 split never applies to a fee under $300; (D) out of scope — a May 2 request is more than four weeks before June 12.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
According to the policy in Text 3B, what must happen before an employee's conference costs are reimbursed?
(A) The department manager must approve the trip in writing at least four weeks before it.
(B) The employee must pay 30 percent of the registration fee in advance.
(C) The employee must be listed as a presenter at the conference.
(D) Receipts must be attached to the registration form.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** **Detail question (policy rule)** — the first two sentences of 3B: attendance requires the manager's written approval, and requests must reach the manager at least four weeks before the event. / *VI: Dạng chi tiết (quy tắc chính sách) — hai câu đầu của 3B: việc tham dự cần phê duyệt bằng văn bản của quản lý, và đề nghị phải đến tay quản lý trước sự kiện ít nhất bốn tuần.*
- **Traps:** (B) distortion — the 30 percent share applies only to fees of $300 or more; (C) wrong text — presenting is Ms. Lindqvist's situation in 3A, not a policy requirement; (D) distortion — receipts belong with the reimbursement claim, not the registration form.

**Pacing note:** If the block cost more than 8 minutes, find where the minutes went — most B3 readers lose them reading the double set straight through; on the redo, hold a one-line summary of each text in mind and let the questions pull you back to the anchor lines. *VI: Nếu block này tốn hơn 8 phút, hãy xem thời gian mất ở đâu — phần lớn người đọc băng B3 mất thời gian vì đọc trọn vẹn bộ đôi từ đầu đến cuối; lần làm lại, hãy giữ trong đầu tóm tắt mỗi văn bản một dòng và để câu hỏi kéo bạn về đúng các dòng mỏ neo.*

### Block C — Challenge (Bands: B4 B5) · Target: 8 minutes

Numbering restarts. One dense single — an article excerpt with an inference question — then one linked double set with a cross-reference question, and one short announcement whose single question tests vocabulary in context (Questions 1–6).

*VI: Số câu bắt đầu lại từ 1. Một đoạn đơn dày — trích đoạn báo kèm một câu suy luận — rồi một bộ đôi có liên kết với câu đối chiếu, và một thông báo ngắn có đúng một câu hỏi tập trung vào từ vựng trong ngữ cảnh (câu 1–6).*

### Reading Passage 1 (Questions 1–2)

> **ARTICLE — *Ferndale Examiner* (excerpt): "Library's Weekend Hours Draw Few Visitors"**
>
> Last year, the Ferndale Public Library extended its weekend hours as a convenience for working families. Twelve months on, the picture is more complicated. The new Saturday-evening hours draw only 9 percent of weekly visits, yet they consume nearly a quarter of the branch's staffing budget. Meanwhile, weekday-morning visits by older patrons have risen sharply, and all four meeting rooms are booked solid every afternoon.
>
> Library director Camille Boyd has not proposed cutting the weekend hours. Instead, she is asking the city council to fund two part-time aides for routine tasks — shelving, checkouts, room setup — so that librarians can, in her words, "spend their time helping readers rather than moving furniture." The council's budget committee responds next month; several members have reportedly described the request as premature.

#### Q1. (Bands: B4 B5)
What can be inferred about the budget committee members who described the request as premature?
(A) They believe the weekend hours should be ended immediately.
(B) They doubt the staffing problem justifies the cost of the aides.
(C) They think librarians should spend more time on routine tasks.
(D) They have rejected the director's requests in previous years.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** **Inference question** — "premature" judges the request, not the library's work: calling funding "too early" means the case for spending has not yet been made, so these members doubt the problem is big enough to justify the cost. / *VI: Dạng suy luận — chữ "vội vàng/còn sớm" phán xét đề nghị, chứ không phán xét hoạt động của thư viện: gọi khoản ngân sách là "chưa đến lúc" nghĩa là lý do chi tiền chưa được chứng minh, nên nhóm ủy viên này nghi ngờ vấn đề có đủ lớn để xứng đáng với khoản chi.*
- **Traps:** (A) out of scope — cutting hours is not on the table; even Director Boyd has not proposed it; (C) quiet reversal — the aides exist precisely so librarians spend less time on routine tasks; (D) out of scope — no previous requests or votes are reported.

#### Q2. (Bands: B4 B5)
What is Ms. Boyd asking the city council to fund?
(A) A reduction in weekend operating hours
(B) The hiring of two additional librarians
(C) A new meeting-room reservation system
(D) Part-time aides to take over routine tasks
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** **Detail question** — anchor: "asking the city council to fund" → "two part-time aides for routine tasks — shelving, checkouts, room setup". / *VI: Dạng chi tiết — mỏ neo: "đề nghị hội đồng thành phố cấp ngân sách" → "hai trợ lý bán thời gian đảm nhận các công việc thường nhật — sắp sách, mượn trả, bố trí phòng".*
- **Traps:** (A) quiet reversal — she has not proposed cutting the weekend hours; (B) lookalike — aides, not librarians; (C) out of scope — meeting-room demand is background evidence, not the request.
