# Test Anatomy — The 7 Parts at a Glance

**Bands:** B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 · **Verified against official sources (see FORMAT.md)** · **One sample item per part inside**

*VI: Cấu trúc đề thi — tổng quan 7 Part. Số liệu đã được kiểm chứng theo nguồn chính thức (xem FORMAT.md). Mỗi Part có một câu mẫu bên dưới.*

## The big picture

| Section | Questions | Time | Who controls the clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listening (Parts 1–4) | 100 | ~45 minutes | The audio — items pass whether you are ready or not |
| Reading (Parts 5–7) | 100 | 75 minutes | You — which is why pacing is a score factor |

- Total: 200 questions, about 2 hours of timed testing.
- Scoring (verified): Listening 5–495, Reading 5–495, total 10–990, in 5-point steps. Employers and programs set their own target scores.
- Each item is one multiple-choice question: **Part 2 has three options; every other part has four.** There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so never leave a blank — but note this file's strategy sections assume you answer everything.

*VI: Tổng quan: 200 câu, khoảng 2 giờ thi chính thức. Điểm: Listening 5–495, Reading 5–495, tổng 10–990, bước nhảy 5 điểm. Part 2 có 3 phương án, các Part còn lại có 4. Không trừ điểm câu sai nên không bỏ trống.*

## Part 1 — Photographs (Scene Cards): 6 questions

You look at a photo (here: a written scene card) and hear four statements; pick the one that best describes it. What it measures: present-continuous vocabulary of actions and positions, and the discipline to reject near-misses. Six items only — small, so each one is worth calm accuracy, not speed.

### Scene Card — Break room
> An office break room. Two employees are standing by the coffee machine, talking. One is holding a mug; the other is reading a notice on the wall. The chairs are pushed in and empty.

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
(A) The employees are sitting at a table.
(B) The men are washing dishes in the sink.
(C) One of the employees is reading something on the wall.
(D) The employees are leaving the room.
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** "reading a notice on the wall" is directly described. / *VI: "đang đọc thông báo trên tường" — đúng như mô tả cảnh.*
- **Traps:** (A) contradicts "standing" and "empty chairs"; (B) invents an action nobody does; (D) invents a departure.

## Part 2 — Question–Response: 25 questions, 3 options

You hear a question or statement and three possible responses; pick the best one. What it measures: understanding what is actually being asked, and accepting **indirect answers** ("When…?" → "Right after lunch."). Nothing is printed — the whole item lives in your ear.

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
M: Where can I get a visitor's badge for the tour this afternoon?
(A) At the reception desk, next to the elevators.
(B) Yes, the tour is this afternoon.
(C) The badges are plastic.
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** A WHERE question gets a place. / *VI: Câu hỏi WHERE (ở đâu) thì nhận câu trả lời chỉ địa điểm.*
- **Traps:** (B) answers an unasked yes/no question and echoes "this afternoon"; (C) is true about badges but answers nothing.

## Part 3 — Conversations: 39 questions (13 conversations × 3)

A conversation between two or three speakers, then three questions (printed, with options). What it measures: following a workplace exchange — problems, requests, schedule changes — and catching paraphrased details. The core technique you will drill: **read the three questions before the audio starts** (using the pause marks in our scripts).

#### Q1. (Bands: B3)
> Script: W: Marcus, the client moved the walkthrough from 2:00 to 3:30. M: That works — I'll be back from the warehouse by then. W: Perfect. I'll resend the calendar invite so nobody shows up early.
Why was the calendar invite resent?
(A) The warehouse closed early.
(B) The meeting time changed.
(C) A new client joined the walkthrough.
(D) Marcus missed the first meeting.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The meeting moved from 2:00 to 3:30, so the invite was updated. / *VI: Cuộc họp đổi từ 2:00 sang 3:30 nên lịch được gửi lại.*
- **Traps:** (A) borrows "warehouse"; (C) invents a new client; (D) invents a missed meeting.

## Part 4 — Talks: 30 questions (10 talks × 3)

One speaker, 60–110 seconds: announcements, voicemails, briefings, recorded messages. Three printed questions per talk. What it measures: extracting structure from one-way speech — who is speaking, to whom, what changes, what to do next. Our scripts include the pause marks a reader must honor.

#### Q1. (Bands: B3 B4)
> Script: "Attention staff. Starting Monday, the parking lot behind Building C will be closed for resurfacing for two weeks. Employee parking moves to the gravel lot on River Road, where a shuttle will run every twenty minutes from six a.m."
What will happen on Monday?
(A) Building C will open a new entrance.
(B) A parking area will become unavailable.
(C) The shuttle schedule will be printed.
(D) River Road will be resurfaced.
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The lot "will be closed for resurfacing" from Monday. / *VI: Bãi đỗ "sẽ bị đóng để trải lại mặt đường" từ thứ Hai.*
- **Traps:** (A) invents an entrance; (C) invents a printing job; (D) swaps what gets resurfaced.

## Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences: 30 questions

One sentence with one blank; choose the word/phrase that fits. What it measures: grammar and vocabulary in workplace sentences — word forms (the biggest family), verb tense/voice, prepositions, connectors, and near-synonym word choice. These 30 items are your speed reserve for Part 7: the course trains them in ~10 minutes.

#### Q1. (Bands: B2 B3)
Ms. Alvarez was promoted after she successfully ---- the merger negotiations.
(A) conducted
(B) conducts
(C) conducting
(D) was conducted
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** Past-tense active verb needed after "she … successfully". / *VI: Cần động từ quá khứ ở dạng chủ động sau "she successfully".*
- **Traps:** (B) wrong tense; (C) a fragment; (D) passive reverses who did what.

## Part 6 — Text Completion: 16 questions (4 texts × 4 blanks)

A short workplace text — email, memo, notice, article — with four blanks; each blank has four options. What it measures: the same grammar and vocabulary as Part 5, **plus cohesion**: what the surrounding sentences and the whole text's purpose demand (a connector, a pronoun, a sentence that fits the paragraph).

> **Internal email — To: Design team · From: R. Okafor, Project Lead**
> The client approved the second draft, so we can move to production. __(1)__ , the launch date stays June 12.

#### Q1. (Bands: B2 B3)
For blank (1), choose the transition that fits.
(A) Because of that
(B) As a result of
(C) That said
(D) In spite of
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The second sentence limits the good news (approval, but the date is unchanged) — a contrast transition fits. / *VI: Câu sau "hạn chế" tin tốt (được duyệt nhưng ngày không đổi) — cần từ nối tương phản.*
- **Traps:** (A)/(B) signal consequence, not contrast; (B) is also grammatically incomplete here ("as a result of" needs a noun).

## Part 7 — Reading Comprehension: 54 questions (29 single + 25 multi)

Ten single passages (email, letter, ad, notice, schedule, form, article), then five linked sets (double or triple passages — e.g., an email plus the schedule it discusses). More than half the Reading section by item count. What it measures: finding specified information fast, understanding purpose and audience, and inference within the lines. The course's reading toolkit builds the scanning method and the timing plan.

#### Q1. (Bands: B2 B3)
> **Notice — Fitness Center, Tower 2**
> The Tower 2 fitness center is open to all badge holders from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Group classes require sign-up at the front desk by 8 p.m. the previous evening; walk-ins are admitted only if the class is not full.

Who most likely is this notice for?
(A) External gym members
(B) Employees of the company
(C) Front-desk staff
(D) Fitness class instructors
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "open to all badge holders" — badges imply company employees. / *VI: "open to all badge holders" — thẻ công implies nhân viên công ty.*
- **Traps:** (A) external members would not have badges; (C)/(D) are people the notice mentions, not its audience.

## Reading-section time plan (course convention — not an ETS rule)

## What each part means for your study routing

- **B1–B2:** Parts 2 and 5 are your fastest point sources — pattern logic with no speed-reading demand. Part 7 singles next.
- **B3:** your gains live in timing (Parts 5–6 speed) and Part 7 volume tolerance.
- **B4–B5:** your gains live in Part 7 hard types and execution under fatigue; Parts 1–2 are maintenance only.

*VI: Định hướng luyện tập theo băng: B1–B2 lấy điểm nhanh nhất ở Part 2 và Part 5, sau đó là bài đơn Part 7; B3 cải thiện nhờ kỷ luật thời gian Part 5–6 và sức bền Part 7; B4–B5 tập trung vào dạng khó Part 7 và sự ổn định khi mệt.*
