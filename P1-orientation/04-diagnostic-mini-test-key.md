## Part 5 — Key & Explanations

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** "So ---- that" needs an adjective, and "popular" is the adjective of the family. / *VI: Cấu trúc "so + tính từ + that" cần một tính từ; "popular" là dạng tính từ.*
- **Traps:** (A) "populated" describes places with people; (C) "popularity" is the noun; (D) "population" is also a noun.

#### Q2. (Bands: B1 B2)
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** "Before she left ... last week" sets the action in the past — simple past "completed". / *VI: "before she left ... last week" đặt hành động trong quá khứ — dùng quá khứ đơn "completed".*
- **Traps:** (B) "will complete" is future; (C) "completes" is a present habit; (D) "is completing" is happening right now — all clash with "last week".

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** The posting stays open up to a deadline — "until May 15". / *VI: Thông báo tuyển dụng mở đến hạn cuối — dùng "until" (đến ngày 15 tháng 5).*
- **Traps:** (A) "at" does not fit a date as an end point; (B) "since" marks a starting point with perfect tenses; (C) "for" measures a length of time, not an end date.

#### Q4. (Bands: B3)
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The passive verb "had been labeled" is modified by an adverb — "inaccurately". / *VI: Động từ bị động "had been labeled" cần một trạng từ bổ nghĩa — "inaccurately".*
- **Traps:** (A) "inaccuracy" is a noun; (B) "inaccurate" is an adjective and cannot modify the verb; (D) "inaccuracies" is a plural noun.

#### Q5. (Bands: B1 B2)
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** Applications "are reviewed by the personnel office" — present passive for a standing procedure. / *VI: "are reviewed BY phòng nhân sự" — câu bị động thời hiện tại diễn tả quy trình thường lệ.*
- **Traps:** (B) active "review" leaves the agent "by..." ungrammatical; (C) "are reviewing" is active progressive; (D) "have reviewed" is active perfect.

#### Q6. (Bands: B3)
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The hotel being fully booked is the cause of reserving rooms elsewhere — "Because". / *VI: Việc khách sạn hết chỗ là nguyên nhân dẫn đến việc đặt phòng ở nơi khác — dùng "Because".*
- **Traps:** (A) "So that" expresses purpose; (B) "In spite of" needs a noun phrase and shows contrast; (D) "Even though" shows contrast, but the logic here is cause and effect.

#### Q7. (Bands: B4 B5)
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** After "its", the sentence needs the abstract noun "durability" — how well the coating lasts. / *VI: Sau "its", câu cần danh từ trừu tượng "durability" — tức độ bền của lớp sơn.*
- **Traps:** (A) "durable" is the adjective; (B) "durably" is the adverb; (C) "duration" means length of time and does not fit "its".

#### Q8. (Bands: B4 B5)
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The fixed collocation is "comply with" the regulations. / *VI: Cụm từ cố định là "comply with" (tuân thủ quy định).*
- **Traps:** (A) "comply about" does not exist; (C) "to" belongs to "conform to", a different verb; (D) "toward" shows direction, not this collocation.

#### Q9. (Bands: B4 B5)
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** More staff, same sixty spaces — contrast; "nevertheless" links it correctly. / *VI: Nhân sự tăng gần gấp đôi nhưng chỗ đỗ vẫn 60 — quan hệ tương phản; "nevertheless" nối đúng.*
- **Traps:** (B) "therefore" signals result, the opposite link; (C) "likewise" adds a parallel fact; (D) "for example" introduces an illustration — none fits the contrast.

#### Q10. (Bands: B4 B5)
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** For a signed document, you keep "a copy" for your records. / *VI: Với văn bản đã ký, người ta giữ lại "một bản sao" (copy) làm lưu liệu cá nhân.*
- **Traps:** (A) a "sample" is a trial product; (B) a "model" is a design or example to imitate; (D) a "draft" is an unfinished version — a signed agreement is final.

## Part 6 — Key & Explanations

#### Q1. (Bands: B1 B2)
- **Key:** C
- **Why:** The service happens on a coming Friday and the heating system receives the action — future passive "will be". / *VI: Về ngữ pháp, đây là câu bị động tương lai với "will be", vì việc bảo trì diễn ra vào thứ Sáu tới và hệ thống sưởi nhận hành động.*
- **Traps:** (A) "was" is past, but June 6 is upcoming; (B) "will have" makes the system the doer, which the passive "serviced by" forbids; (D) "would have been" needs an unreal condition that is absent.

#### Q2. (Bands: B3)
- **Key:** A
- **Why:** A time clause after "will start" takes the present perfect for a completed-by-then action — "have left". / *VI: Mệnh đề thời gian sau "will start" dùng hiện tại hoàn thành cho hành động sẽ hoàn tất trước đó — "have left".*
- **Traps:** (B) "will leave" is not used in a time clause; (C) "are leaving" suggests an action in progress; (D) simple past "left" does not fit the future reference.

#### Q3. (Bands: B3)
- **Key:** D
- **Why:** After "please", the sentence needs the base verb "secure". / *VI: Sau "please", câu cần động từ nguyên mẫu "secure".*
- **Traps:** (A) adverb "securely"; (B) noun "security"; (C) "securing" — none can serve as the verb after "please".

#### Q4. (Bands: B4 B5)
- **Key:** B
- **Why:** The fire-drill change is an extra piece of news on top of the heating notice — "In addition". / *VI: Tin về buổi diễn tập cháy là một thông tin bổ sung bên cạnh thông báo bảo trì — dùng "In addition".*
- **Traps:** (A) "However" signals contrast, but nothing is being contradicted; (C) "Instead" would replace a stated alternative that does not exist; (D) "As a result" claims the drill moved because of the heating work — no such link.
